## Before any of this goes on the site

1. Confirm each number and name on the service's own official page, and record the address of that page.

2. Find out the languages each service works in. Many learners may not speak Thai, and the directory does not say. If English support is not available, say so plainly on the page.

3. Ask each service whether calls can appear on a phone bill, and note the answer.

4. Confirm the emergency numbers (police, medical, and the right one for danger from a partner) with an official government source.

5. Check whether the services are open to foreign residents and visitors as well as Thai citizens.

6. Record the date verified, and set a reminder to recheck in six months, with link checks more often.

7. Also list one or two services for a Thai-speaking pastor or counsellor referral if you can verify them, and any services that offer help in English or in other languages your learners use.

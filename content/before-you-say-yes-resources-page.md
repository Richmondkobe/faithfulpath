# Before You Say Yes — International Resources

*This is the live version of the resources page printed at the back of* Before You Say Yes. *Every entry below was last independently checked on 6 September 2026. If a number or link is wrong, please tell us at info@faithfulpathcommunity.com so it can be corrected for everyone.*

This page lists places to begin. It cannot list everything, and helpline numbers, hours and websites change. If you are not in immediate danger and can search safely, begin with the global directories below; they are maintained by the services themselves. If you are in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service or move to the safest available place without delaying to search this page.

**A note on safety.** If you think your phone or internet activity is being watched, use a safer device — one the other person has never used — to visit these websites or make these calls, and do not leave this page open. If you cannot identify a safer device, contact a specialist abuse service when you can do so safely. Do not immediately delete monitoring software, clear shared accounts or change settings; visible changes may alert the person monitoring you. See Chapter 19 and *When to Seek Help* in the book.

### Global directories

- **Find a Helpline** — findahelpline.com. A searchable directory of verified helplines in more than 150 countries, covering suicide, emotional distress, abuse and other concerns. Run in partnership with the International Association for Suicide Prevention. Available contact methods, hours and languages vary by country and service.
- **Lila.help** — lila.help. A directory of vetted helplines and support services for gender-based violence, including domestic abuse, sexual violence and forced marriage, maintained through the Global Network of Women's Shelters.
- **Befrienders Worldwide** — befrienders.org. A global network of emotional-support centres, with a directory by country.

### Crisis and emotional support

*If you are thinking about harming yourself or not wanting to live.*

Hours, languages, eligibility, confidentiality and call charges vary. A line listed as 24/7 may still experience delays or temporary interruption. If one service does not answer and you cannot remain safe, try another service or contact emergency help. Note the difference between a crisis line (trained to respond to immediate risk), a listening service (emotional support), an emergency mental-health service (clinical assessment) and a general counselling line; they do not all provide the same intervention.

### Forced marriage and family-based coercion

*If you are being pressured into a marriage, or fear punishment, removal or travel abroad for refusing one.* Do not approach the family, arrange mediation or announce your plans unless a specialist familiar with forced marriage has advised that it is safe. Family involvement can increase risk or accelerate travel and marriage plans.

**United Kingdom** — Karma Nirvana (independent; honour-based abuse and forced marriage, all genders): [0800 5999 247](tel:08005999247) · karmanirvana.org.uk. Forced Marriage Unit (government): [020 7008 0151](tel:02070080151) · gov.uk/stop-forced-marriage
**Australia** — My Blue Sky, run by Anti-Slavery Australia (independent): mybluesky.org.au
**United States** — Tahirih Justice Center, Forced Marriage Initiative (independent): tahirih.org
**Elsewhere** — lila.help lists forced-marriage services by country. Where no specialist service is listed, contact a domestic-abuse service and ask whether it can refer you.

### Debt and money

*Free, non-judgemental debt advice, distinct from commercial financial advisers.*

- United Kingdom: StepChange (stepchange.org, [0800 138 1111](tel:08001381111)); National Debtline (nationaldebtline.org)
- Ireland: MABS (mabs.ie)
- Australia: National Debt Helpline ([1800 007 007](tel:1800007007), ndh.org.au)
- New Zealand: MoneyTalks ([0800 345 123](tel:0800345123))
- United States: a non-profit credit-counselling agency accredited by the NFCC (nfcc.org); verify the individual agency's accreditation
- Canada: an agency accredited by Credit Counselling Canada (creditcounsellingcanada.ca); verify the individual agency
- Elsewhere: look for a government consumer-protection service, a regulated non-profit debt-advice organisation, a citizens' advice service, or an independent financial-counselling body. Avoid organisations that demand substantial upfront fees or promise to erase debt immediately.

### Finding a counsellor

Regulation differs by country, and sometimes by state or province. Before booking, check the person's qualification, professional membership or licence, relevant experience, supervision, ethical code and complaints process — the questions are in *When to Seek Help*.

- United Kingdom: BACP or UKCP registers
- Ireland: IACP directory and any applicable statutory register
- Australia: PACFA or ACA for counsellors and psychotherapists; the Psychology Board / AHPRA register for psychologists
- New Zealand: NZAC for counsellors; verify any profession-specific statutory registration
- United States: verify the practitioner's licence through the relevant state licensing board. Directories such as the ACA's or Psychology Today's can help you find names, but they do not replace licence verification
- Canada: regulation varies by province. Verify registration with the applicable provincial college where the profession is regulated; CCPA membership alone is not a provincial licence
- South Africa: verify through the HPCSA or another applicable statutory body
- Singapore: the Singapore Association for Counselling keeps a professional register; confirm the practitioner's training and current status
- Elsewhere: ask a doctor, hospital or national mental-health helpline which professional body regulates counsellors or psychologists in your country

A Christian counselling association may help you assess faith alignment, but membership does not replace recognised clinical training or professional accountability.

### A note on this list

This list was independently checked in September 2026, but numbers, hours and services can change. Find a Helpline and Lila.help are periodically maintained global directories, but no printed or online directory can guarantee immediate availability. If one contact does not work, try another verified service. If you are in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service or move to the safest available place. A disconnected number does not mean that help is unavailable.

Lesson 4 · The Fear Beneath the Thought · Slide 8 of 12

A small number of fears appear again and again in these conversations. I list them not so you can diagnose yourself, but so you can recognise what you find. Fear of rejection: if they really knew me, they would not want me. Fear of failure. Fear of losing money. Fear of disappointing people. Fear of choosing wrongly and having only yourself to blame. Fear of losing control: if I stop managing everything, something terrible will happen. Fear that God will not come through, which is often the hardest to admit. And fear of the past repeating: what happened before will happen again, and I will not survive it twice. You may recognise one, or several, or none. Do not force a choice if nothing is clear.

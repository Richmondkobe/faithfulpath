Welcome to Lesson 4. Here is today's question. Why does a thought keep coming back, even after you have looked at the facts and done what you reasonably can? Sometimes the answer is that the thought was never only about the thing on the surface. The teaching takes about eight minutes. Near the end there is one small, optional writing practice, so have a pen nearby.

Now, if it feels manageable, your turn. This practice is optional and private. Take one thought that keeps returning, and ask once: if this happened, what would it mean to me? Ask it of your answer, up to three times. Then write one sentence: "I am afraid that..." or, just as good, "No deeper fear appeared." Then come back to the present: say where you are, and one thing that is true right now. If at any point it becomes too much, if you feel panicked, or far away, or unable to return to the present, put the page aside, contact someone safe, and use the support link under this lesson.

MEET SAMUEL

The numbers were fine.
He still could not stop checking them.

Lesson 4 · The Fear Beneath the Thought · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a man I will call Samuel. He was the church treasurer, careful and capable, and he had been thinking about the annual budget for three weeks. The numbers were not the problem. Giving was slightly down, the shortfall was manageable, and he had already found two sensible adjustments. Yet he could not stop. He recalculated figures he had already confirmed. He rehearsed his presentation to the elders again and again. So I asked him a question I have learnt to ask. "Suppose the elders looked at the numbers and were unhappy. What would that mean to you?" "That I'd let them down." "And if you had?" "That I'm not as capable as they think I am." "And if that were true?" A long pause. "That they wouldn't want me." Samuel had not only been thinking about a budget. He had been guarding a door, and behind it was a fear of being found inadequate and set aside.

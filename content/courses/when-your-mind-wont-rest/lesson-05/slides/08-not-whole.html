I want to be as clear here as anywhere in this course. Not every spiritual struggle has a physical cause. Some anxious thinking comes from real spiritual questions, real sin, a wound, grief, or the fears we met in Lesson 4, and caring for the body does not replace caring for those. Elijah still needed Horeb. And food and sleep do not cure anxiety disorders. If you live with an anxiety condition, depression, obsessive-compulsive symptoms or trauma, better sleep and regular meals may help you cope, but they are not treatment. Treatment is what qualified professionals provide, and it may include therapy, medication or both. Please do not let anyone, including this lesson, tell you that you have simply not been resting enough. Physical causes and spiritual realities are not rivals.

MEET JOSEPH

A spiritual crisis. Four hours' sleep a night. A two-week chest infection.

Lesson 5 · Your Body Is Part of the Story · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a pastor I will call Joseph. He phoned me late one evening in what he called a spiritual crisis. He had been questioning his calling for a week. He felt distant from God. He had begun to wonder whether the last fifteen years had been a mistake. I listened for a long time. Then I asked some questions that had nothing to do with his calling. How much had he slept? Four or five hours a night, maybe less. When had he last taken a day off? He could not remember. Had he eaten properly? Something, at some point. And he had been fighting a chest infection for two weeks, drinking a great deal of coffee to push through it. I did not tell him his crisis was imaginary. I told him I did not think either of us could hear his questions clearly until he had slept, eaten, and let his body recover, and I asked him to see a doctor about the infection.

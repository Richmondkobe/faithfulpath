Lesson 5 · Your Body Is Part of the Story · Slide 1 of 12

Welcome to Lesson 5, the last lesson in Module 1. Here is today's question. When did you last ask what your body is telling you? Most overthinkers have examined their thoughts, their faith and their fears in great detail, and have never once asked whether their body is part of the noise. The teaching takes about eight minutes. Near the end, you will pause for one small practice, so have a pen nearby.

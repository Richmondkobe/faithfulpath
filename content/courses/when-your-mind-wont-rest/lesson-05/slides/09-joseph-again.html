Lesson 5 · Your Body Is Part of the Story · Slide 9 of 12

Joseph slept eleven hours that night and saw his doctor the next day. The questions about his calling did not disappear. But when we spoke the following week, they had become smaller and more specific, and he could think about them without despair. His body had been part of the story all along. He had simply not been reading that part.

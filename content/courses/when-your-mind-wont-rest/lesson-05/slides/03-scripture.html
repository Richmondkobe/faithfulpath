Lesson 5 · Your Body Is Part of the Story · Slide 3 of 12

Our verse is 1 Kings 19, verse 5, in the English Standard Version. "And behold, an angel touched him and said to him, 'Arise and eat.'" This is Elijah, straight from Mount Carmel, where fire fell from heaven. Then Jezebel threatens his life, he runs into the wilderness, sits down under a broom tree, and asks God to let him die. And the first thing God sends is not a sermon. It is a touch, bread, water, and sleep.

Lesson 5 · Your Body Is Part of the Story · Slide 7 of 12

So, without pretending to be a doctor, here are the things to check. Sleep: a problem that looks manageable at noon can look overwhelming at three in the morning. Weeks of stress: a body braced for a long time produces a scanning, vigilant mind. Skipped meals. Illness or pain, which drain the reserves that keep worry in proportion. Caffeine, which in some people adds restlessness and a faster heartbeat. Medication, which can affect mood and sleep, especially when it is started, stopped or changed; and please, never change a medication because of anything in this course. Speak to the prescriber or a pharmacist. And long seasons without rest, the tiredness that one night's sleep does not fix. None of these is a sin. Nobody has failed God by being tired or unwell. But each one can make a restless mind more restless.

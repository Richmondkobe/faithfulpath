THE MAIN TRUTH

You are not only a mind.

Be slow to draw conclusions about your faith or your future from an exhausted, hungry or unwell body.

Lesson 5 · Your Body Is Part of the Story · Slide 6 of 12

Here is the main truth. You are not only a mind. God made your body and called it good. Jesus grew tired, slept in boats and asked for water. Scripture is not embarrassed by the fact that people who are hungry, exhausted or ill think and feel differently from people who are fed and rested. So be slow to draw conclusions about your faith or your future from an exhausted, hungry or unwell body. Ask first: what is my body telling me?

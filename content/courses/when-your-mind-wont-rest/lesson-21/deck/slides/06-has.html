Lesson 21 · You Do Not Have to Solve Your Whole Life Tonight · Slide 6 of 12

What it has offered, instead, is a way of understanding what your mind is doing when it will not rest: the difference between thinking and circling, the fear beneath the thought, the body that is part of the story. Ways of recognising the particular shapes overthinking takes. Practices for renewing the mind with what is true rather than what is merely reassuring. And a way of living: receiving peace before the answers, and building a small rhythm that holds the rest.

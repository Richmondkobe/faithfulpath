Lesson 21 · You Do Not Have to Solve Your Whole Life Tonight · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember, and it is the whole course in a sentence. You do not have to solve your whole life tonight. Only respond to what is actually before you: act, wait, seek help, move to safety, or surrender, and let tomorrow be tomorrow's. This completes the twenty-one lessons of the foundation course. If you have finished all of them, your certificate is now available. Whatever comes next, one small practice carried forward is enough.

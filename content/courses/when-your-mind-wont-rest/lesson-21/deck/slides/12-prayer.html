A FINAL PRAYER OF SURRENDER

Lord, I cannot solve my whole life tonight,
and I no longer need to.

What is mine to do, I will do.
What must wait, I will let wait.

What I lack, I will ask for.
What is not mine, I release to you.

Not my will, but yours. Amen.

A final prayer of surrender. Lord, I cannot solve my whole life tonight, and I no longer need to. What is mine to do, I will do. What must wait, I will let wait. What I lack, I will ask for. Where there is danger, lead me to safety. And what is not mine to carry, I release to you, as the psalmist poured out his heart and then took refuge, as you yourself asked for the cup to pass and then said, not my will, but yours. In Jesus' name. Amen. ... Thank you for walking this course. Whatever you carry forward, carry it lightly. You do not have to solve your whole life tonight.

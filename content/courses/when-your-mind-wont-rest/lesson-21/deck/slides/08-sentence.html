Lesson 21 · You Do Not Have to Solve Your Whole Life Tonight · Slide 8 of 12

Here, then, is what the whole course has been trying to say. You do not have to solve your whole life tonight. Your mind may still try: it may gather work, family, money, health, conversations, regrets and questions about God into one impossible night-time assignment. But no single night was made to carry the whole of a life, and God has not arranged it that way. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble. Each day contains responsibilities, conversations, decisions, prayers and acts of trust, but only those that genuinely belong to that day. Faithfulness does not require carrying every possible future responsibility at once.

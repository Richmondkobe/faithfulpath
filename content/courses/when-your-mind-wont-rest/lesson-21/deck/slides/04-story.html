Lesson 21 · You Do Not Have to Solve Your Whole Life Tonight · Slide 4 of 12

This course began with a woman I called Grace, lying awake with a mind that would not switch off. It has passed through a good many others since: Miriam waiting fourteen months to make a decision she already knew, Caleb at his post, Jonathan with a set of good tools and no life to put them in, and others besides. They are illustrative figures, some drawn broadly from recurring pastoral situations, and no story should be read as a complete account of one particular person. But nothing about their restlessness was invented. If you have recognised yourself in some of them, you have recognised something true.

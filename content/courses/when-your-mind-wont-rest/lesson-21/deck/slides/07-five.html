Lesson 21 · You Do Not Have to Solve Your Whole Life Tonight · Slide 7 of 12

Underneath all of it there has been one pattern. For much of what troubles a restless mind, there are a few faithful responses, and the work is to find which one the present moment requires. Act: something is mine to do now. Wait: something will be mine, but not yet, and I will write down when. Seek help: I lack what I need, and I will ask someone who has it. Move to safety: there is real danger, and it comes first. Surrender: this is not mine, and I give it to God. In many ordinary situations, circling continues because the mind is trying to do all five at once, in the dark, without a pen. In others, a person may know exactly what is required and still be unable to disengage, because of anxiety, trauma or another condition, and these five responses are a pastoral framework, not a substitute for the help some restless minds need.

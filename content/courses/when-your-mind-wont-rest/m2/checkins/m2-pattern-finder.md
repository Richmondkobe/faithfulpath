# Pattern Finder

This is not a test or a diagnosis. Select any patterns that sound familiar, one, several or none, and "I am not sure" is a perfectly good answer. The course will suggest lessons you may want to visit first, but you remain free to follow the module in order, and nothing you select is scored, labelled or stored as a description of you.

Module 2 looks at seven shapes a restless mind tends to take. Most people recognise more than one, and the shapes often run into each other: a prediction becomes a rehearsal, a rehearsal becomes mind-reading. Recognising the pattern is half the work.

- My mind runs ahead to what could go wrong, and each imagined problem leads to the next.
- I keep going back to something I said or did, or a decision that went badly, even though it cannot be changed.
- I hold conversations in my head, supplying both sides, before they have happened.
- I work out what people think of me from small signs, a short reply, a silence, a look.
- I keep asking God, or other people, for one more confirmation before I can act.
- I check, redo and delay because nothing feels finished or safe to send.
- I lie awake holding other people's problems, planning and worrying on their behalf.
- I am not sure.

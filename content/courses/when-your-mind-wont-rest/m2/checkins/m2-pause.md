# Module 2 pause: Name the cycle

You have finished the seven lessons of Module 2. Before moving on, you may find it helpful to draw one of your own cycles, once, so that you can see its shape and notice earlier next time. This is optional, private and unscored. You can continue to Module 3 whether or not you complete it, and your answers are not monitored.

Choose one recent cycle, not the worst, just a recent one, and fill in the book's Worry-Cycle Map:

1. **The trigger** (what started it)
2. **The first thought**
3. **The feeling and the bodily reaction**
4. **What the mind did next** (predicted, replayed, rehearsed, read minds, sought certainty, checked, carried someone)
5. **What I did** (checked, asked, avoided, stayed up, scrolled, prayed anxiously, withdrew)
6. **Where it ended, and how long it took**
7. **The earliest point at which I could have noticed, and which practice fits there**

Complete the map once for one cycle. It is a way of seeing, not a form to perfect; if you notice yourself redrawing it to get it exactly right, that is the cycle again, and the answer is to stop.

Module 3 turns from recognising the pattern to renewing the mind: how Scripture confronts falsehood, reshapes attention, and teaches us to respond faithfully to thoughts without trying to suppress every unwanted mental event.

# Group Agreement

*Printable. Read aloud at the first session, before any sharing, and give each member a copy.*

## What we promise each other

- **What is said in the group stays in the group.** We do not discuss one another's disclosures outside it, even with spouses.
- **Nobody has to share.** Passing is an ordinary option and requires no explanation. Every sharing activity has a private alternative.
- **Nobody's anxiety will be treated as weak faith.** Nobody will be diagnosed, scored, categorised or advised about medication.
- **We start and end on time.**

## Sharing or passing

When an exercise is discussed, you may share what you have written, share only the part you choose, say what the exercise was like without its content, or pass. "Pass" is a complete answer, and no one will ask why.

Two worksheets stay out of the group entirely: **"If This Happened, What Would It Mean to Me?"** and **The Faithful Decision Sheet**. You may say that you used them. You will not be asked what is on them.

## The limits of confidentiality

Your leaders will protect your privacy, and they will reinforce this agreement. They cannot guarantee the conduct of every participant outside the group, so share accordingly.

Information may need to be shared in these circumstances: a safeguarding concern, a serious risk of harm to you or to someone else, an applicable legal duty, or necessary consultation with a safeguarding lead or professional supervisor. In those circumstances your leaders will share only what is necessary, only with those who need to know, and will, wherever possible, tell you first what they intend to do.

Your leaders will not promise to keep secret anything that would leave someone in danger, and they will not make promises about what the police, social services or health services will do with information.

## What this group is not

This group is pastoral formation, not medical or psychological treatment. It does not treat anxiety disorders, depression, OCD or trauma. If a qualified professional is supporting you, their guidance takes priority over anything here, including the exercises and their limits.

If you need more than this group, that is not a failure of the group. Finding Help Where You Live shows how to find support in your country.

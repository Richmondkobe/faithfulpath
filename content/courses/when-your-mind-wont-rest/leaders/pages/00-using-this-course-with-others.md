# Using This Course With Others

This section is for pastors, church leaders, small-group facilitators and anyone who will take another person through *When Your Mind Won't Rest*. It is not part of the ordinary learner pathway, and members are never required to read it.

> **Before you download the group materials**
>
> Reading this guidance does not qualify anyone as a counsellor or mental-health professional. Churches and group leaders must follow applicable safeguarding law and their own organisational procedures.
>
> **Please tick to continue:** *I have read the safety, confidentiality, safeguarding and referral guidance and understand that using these materials does not qualify me to provide counselling or mental-health treatment.*
>
> This acknowledgement unlocks the group downloads only. It generates no certificate and records no approval by Faithful Path. The course records that you ticked it and the date, and nothing else.

## What is in this section

1. **What This Course Is, and What It Is Not**
2. **Who Should Lead**
3. **Confidentiality and Its Limits**
4. **Safeguarding**
5. **Suicide and Self-Harm**
6. **Domestic Abuse and Coercive Control**
7. **Responding to a Disclosure**
8. **Referral: When to Send Someone On**
9. **The Worksheets Are Not Diagnosis, and Sharing Is Not Compulsory**
10. **Running the Six Weeks** — the group plan, with the printable Group Agreement and the pre-course Leader's Checklist
11. **One-to-One Use**
12. **A Word About Theology in the Room**
13. **Leader's Checklist** (printable)

Two further downloads sit behind the same acknowledgement: the **Group Agreement** (printable, to read aloud at the first session and give to each member) and the complete **Leaders' Guidance PDF**, which is the book's guidance in one document.

This section is for anyone who will use this course alongside another person: a pastor walking through it with a member of the congregation, a counsellor using it as a resource, a small-group leader running it over six weeks. I said in Lesson 21 that there are things here about safety and about limits that matter more than the exercises. That was not a figure of speech. Please read the whole of this before you begin, and please read “When This Course Is Not Enough” at the front of the course again, this time as the person who may be sitting across from someone it describes.

Nothing in this section is legal advice. Safeguarding law, confidentiality rules, mandatory-reporting duties and the availability of services differ by country and sometimes by region, and your church or organisation should have its own policies. Follow applicable law and your organisation’s current safeguarding, confidentiality and record-keeping policies. Where they appear to conflict, obtain advice from the organisation’s safeguarding lead or an appropriately qualified professional; applicable law takes precedence. If your organisation has no such policies, obtaining them is a prerequisite for using this course with others.

## The two worksheets that stay out of the group

Two pages are marked **Not for group sharing**, and this is not a preference: **"If This Happened, What Would It Mean to Me?"** (Lesson 4) and **The Faithful Decision Sheet** (Lesson 18). A member may mention that they used them. Nobody should be asked what is on them.

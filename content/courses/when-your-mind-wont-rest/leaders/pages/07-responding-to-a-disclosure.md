## Responding to a Disclosure

Whatever the disclosure, the shape of a good response is the same, and it is worth having it in front of you.

Listen without interrogating. Thank the person for telling you. Do not promise secrecy. Establish whether immediate safety is involved. Explain what you must do next, and why. Record facts and actions according to policy, not impressions. Consult or refer promptly. Follow up, without assuming responsibility that belongs to professionals.

## What This Course Is, and What It Is Not

This is a pastoral course, built from a pastoral book. It teaches Christians to understand a restless mind, to recognise the patterns overthinking falls into, to test those patterns against evidence and Scripture, and to build a life that supports peace. Its tools are pastoral tools. Several of them resemble techniques found in cognitive and behavioural therapies, and that resemblance is not accidental, because good pastoral care and good therapy both attend to what a person believes and does. But the resemblance does not make the course a therapy, and it does not make you a therapist by using it.

The course is not a treatment for generalised anxiety disorder, panic disorder, obsessive-compulsive disorder, post-traumatic stress, depression, or any other clinical condition. It says so in every chapter, and it will say so in your group. It is not a screening instrument. It is not designed for self-directed exposure work, and it must not be adapted into it. And it is written for adults. This edition should not ordinarily be used as a group course for minors. Any adapted use with teenagers requires appropriately trained youth leaders, the organisation’s safeguarding approval, age-appropriate materials, and the consent arrangements required by local law and policy; the exercises on fear, memory and decisions are not designed for minors, and parental knowledge alone is not a sufficient safeguard.

What the course can do, in your hands, is give a common language, a set of practices that a person can use between conversations, and a structure for six weeks of honest reflection in Christian community. That is worth a great deal. It is also the limit.

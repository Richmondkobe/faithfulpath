# When Your Mind Won't Rest — Using This Course With Others (draft for review)

The leaders' section, built from the book's "Guidance for Pastors, Counsellors and Small-Group Leaders". It sits outside the learner pathway, is member-gated, and is reached from the course home.

## What is in this package

- `pages/00-using-this-course-with-others.md` — the gate. Carries the warning that reading this guidance qualifies nobody as a counsellor, the single-statement acknowledgement (front matter: `unlocks: "group downloads only"`, `generates_certificate: false`, `records_approval_by_faithful_path: false`, stores checked status and date only), a contents list, the book's opening, and the two worksheets that stay out of the group named explicitly.
- `pages/01…13` — the book's thirteen sections, one page each, in its order: what the course is and is not · who should lead · confidentiality and its limits · safeguarding · suicide and self-harm · domestic abuse and coercive control · responding to a disclosure · referral · the worksheets are not diagnosis · running the six weeks · one-to-one use · theology in the room · the leader's checklist.
- `resources/group-agreement.md` — printable, new, drawn from the book's confidentiality, sharing-or-passing and limits material, to be read aloud at the first session and given to each member.
- `resources/leaders-checklist.md` — printable, the book's pre-course checklist, attached to both the six-weeks page and its own page.
- `downloads/leaders-guidance.pdf` — the complete Leaders' Guidance in one document (17 pages): the thirteen sections plus both printables, with its own cover note, the not-a-counsellor warning and the ESV notice.

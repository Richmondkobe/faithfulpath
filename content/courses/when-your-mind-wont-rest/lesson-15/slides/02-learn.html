Here is what you will learn today: the difference between what is accurate and what deserves your sustained attention, and a six-question test to decide. One thing first. Scrolling or monitoring that is hard to control, or that disrupts your sleep or daily life, deserves assessment rather than more willpower. And persistent low mood, or vivid images and nightmares connected to something you experienced or witnessed, are reasons to speak with a doctor or a mental-health professional.

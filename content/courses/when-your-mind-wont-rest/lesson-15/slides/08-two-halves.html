The mind is fed from two directions, so the practice has two halves. What you take in: for a week, notice what you give sustained attention to, without moralising, then run the test on the two or three sources that occupy you most. Andrew found his evening reading failed on established, proportionate and bounded. He did not stop reading the news. He stopped reading forecasts of it, limited himself to a reliable summary in the morning, and gave his evenings to something else. And what you generate: the mind also feeds itself, through the predictions, replays, rehearsals and mind-readings of Module 2. When you notice yourself dwelling on one, run the same test.

Lesson 15 · Think About What Is True · Slide 9 of 12

Paul does not stop at telling us what not to dwell on. A mind emptied of alarm will fill with something, and it is better to choose: Scripture, the actual people in front of you, the work that is yours, the good genuinely present in your day, the character of God. It will feel at first like a poorer diet than alarm, because alarm is stimulating. It is not poorer. And this is not a call to ignorance. A Christian cannot weep with those who weep without knowing that they are weeping. The question is how much sustained attention to give, and whether it serves anything. Andrew knew more about the economy than he needed, and less about his wife's week than he should.

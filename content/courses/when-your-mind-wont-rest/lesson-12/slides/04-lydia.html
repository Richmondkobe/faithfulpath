MEET LYDIA

Six people. Most of them did not know they were being carried.

Lesson 12 · Carrying Everyone in Your Mind · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a woman I will call Lydia. She could not sleep, and when I asked what kept her awake she gave me a list. Her adult son's marriage was in difficulty. Her mother, in her eighties, was becoming forgetful and refused to see a doctor. A friend from church had been made redundant and was not answering messages. A colleague going through a divorce had become short-tempered, and Lydia had been covering for her. Her husband had a hospital appointment next month that he was not worried about, but she was. "I feel as though I am holding all of them up," she said. "And if I put any of them down, something will happen." I asked her which of these people had actually asked for her involvement. Her mother's forgetfulness was a real concern, and as a daughter she had a real part, though not the whole. Her husband's appointment was his. The rest she had picked up, one by one, because she loved these people, and it had not occurred to her that there was any other way to love them.

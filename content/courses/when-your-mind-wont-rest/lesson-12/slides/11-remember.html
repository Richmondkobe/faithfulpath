Lesson 12 · Carrying Everyone in Your Mind · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember. The people you love are loved by God more than by you. Carry your own load. Help with what is too heavy. And entrust the rest to him. That is the end of Module 2. You have now named the seven patterns: predicting disaster, replaying the past, rehearsing conversations, reading minds, seeking confirmation, perfecting, and carrying. Module 3 will show you what to do with a thought once you have named it: how to test it, pray it, and decide.

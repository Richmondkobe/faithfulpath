THE TOOL · FIVE QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU PICK IT UP

  1. Did they ask?
  2. Do I have an established responsibility here?
  3. Is there a serious need they cannot address alone?
  4. Am I the appropriate person?
  5. Is the help wanted, safe and sustainable?

Lesson 12 · Carrying Everyone in Your Mind · Slide 6 of 12

Whether a burden is yours depends on role, need, capacity, consent, safety and consequence together. Five questions make the answer honest. One: did they ask? A request matters, though children, vulnerable adults and people in danger may need action without asking. Two: do I have an established responsibility, as a parent, spouse, carer, employee or leader? Three: is there a serious need they cannot presently address alone? Four: am I the appropriate person, or does this belong to a doctor, a counsellor, a social worker or a service? Five: is the proposed help wanted, safe and sustainable? Lydia's mother met several of these. Her son's marriage met almost none.

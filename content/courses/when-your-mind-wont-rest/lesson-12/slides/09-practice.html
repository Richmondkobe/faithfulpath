Lesson 12 · Carrying Everyone in Your Mind · Slide 9 of 12

So the practice is this. Notice: I am carrying. Ask the five questions of that person honestly. Sort the weight, and give each part its response. What is yours to release, release deliberately: say the name, say what you have been carrying, say that it is theirs and God's, and then do not pick it back up when the worry returns. Release is not a feeling that arrives. It is a direction you keep choosing. And about "if I do not, no one will": sometimes it is true, and then you may need help so you are not carrying alone. But do not withdraw abruptly from a real responsibility to test it. Reduce only what is unnecessary, unrequested, or beyond your role: the anxious message, the unasked advice, the mental planning of a capable adult's decisions.

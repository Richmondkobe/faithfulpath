Lesson 12 · Carrying Everyone in Your Mind · Slide 5 of 12

Lydia's pattern has many names: people-pleasing, rescuing, over-functioning. What it has in common everywhere is a confusion between caring about someone and carrying them. Caring notices, feels, prays, and offers what is appropriate: presence, help, a word, a referral. Carrying treats another person's life, feelings or decisions as yours to manage. It plans, worries and anticipates on their behalf, and quietly assumes their agency. It is hard to see because it looks like love, and it often begins as love. It feels necessary. It can be a way of managing your own anxiety. And it can shade into control. Most carriers are among the kindest people in any church. But kindness that has become carrying is not serving the people it carries, and it is slowly consuming the person doing it.

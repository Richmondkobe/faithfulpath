Here is what you will learn today: what "take every thought captive" does and does not mean, and a six-movement practice for a recurring thought that makes a claim you can examine. One thing I must say first. Frequent, distressing intrusive thoughts, thoughts tied to a frightening event you lived through, or distressing thoughts about harm coming to a baby, all deserve assessment by a qualified professional rather than more fighting. And a thought that comes with an urge or intention to act, or with confusion, hallucinations or loss of contact with reality, needs urgent medical help today. The support link is under this lesson.

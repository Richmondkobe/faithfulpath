Now it is your turn. Take your pen. Take one recurring thought that makes a claim. Write the claim as a sentence, and name which pattern it is. Then write your refusal in one sentence: "I do not know that, and I will not organise today around it." Once. Then, after this lesson, do something for half an hour that needs your attention outside your own mind: a conversation, a task with your hands, a walk with something to look at.

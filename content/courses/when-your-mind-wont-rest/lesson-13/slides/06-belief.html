A COMMON BELIEF

"If I were spiritual enough, I would not have these thoughts at all."

Maturity is not a quiet mind, which is not promised. It is a faithful response to a noisy one.

Lesson 13 · Take Every Thought Captive · Slide 6 of 12

"If I were spiritual enough, I would not have these thoughts at all." Benjamin believed this, and it was doing more damage than any of the thoughts. Scripture does not present maturity as mental silence. The Psalms voice fear, grief and confusion, and Jesus was deeply distressed in Gethsemane while remaining perfectly obedient. The presence of a thought you did not choose is not a measure of your spiritual state. What Scripture describes is people who bring their thoughts to God, answer falsehood with truth, and choose what to attend to and obey. That is the maturity on offer: not a quiet mind, which is not promised, but a faithful response to a noisy one. And there is a further danger. If unwanted thoughts are evidence of failure, you will monitor your mind for evidence, and monitoring tends to produce more of what it monitors.

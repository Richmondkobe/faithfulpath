TWO CAUTIONS

Not for every thought

Most thoughts do not need it. If you are monitoring your mind constantly, step back, not forward.

Once, not every time

Examine the claim once. When it returns, acknowledge it briefly and redirect. No new ritual.

Lesson 13 · Take Every Thought Captive · Slide 9 of 12

Two cautions. This is not a checklist to run on every mental event; most thoughts do not need it. And a thought may return many times. You do not need to perform the six movements each time. After examining the claim once, briefly acknowledge its return and redirect your attention, without creating another ritual. If you notice, as Benjamin did, that you have begun to monitor your mind constantly, that is the signal to step back rather than press on. Put the practice away for a day or two and do something ordinary. Attention that is locked inward needs somewhere else to go. That is part of the practice.

Welcome to Lesson 19, the first lesson of Module 4. Here is today's question. What are you waiting to know before you let yourself rest? Some of us have made peace conditional on an answer, and the answer keeps moving. The teaching takes about eight minutes. Near the end, you will pause for one small writing practice, so have a pen nearby.

Lesson 19 · Peace Before Answers · Slide 2 of 12

Here is what you will learn today: how Christ's peace is given before the answer arrives, not after it, and how to tell necessary watching from watching that only exhausts you. One thing first. Waiting for a serious result, a diagnosis, a verdict, news of someone you love, is one of the hardest things a person does, and heightened anxiety during such a wait is an ordinary response, not a spiritual failure. If the wait has disrupted your sleep, eating or ability to function for more than a couple of weeks, please tell your doctor or the clinical team; many hospitals support the families of patients too, and using that support is not weakness. The support link is under this lesson.

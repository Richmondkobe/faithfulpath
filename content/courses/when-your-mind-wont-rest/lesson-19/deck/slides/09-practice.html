Lesson 19 · Peace Before Answers · Slide 9 of 12

Here is the practice I worked through with Caleb. First, find the sentence: write it exactly, "I will permit myself peace when I know that ______," including the part that sounds unreasonable once it is on paper. Second, run the horizon test: if that answer came, and came well, what would be the next thing you needed to know? If you can see one, then the peace you are waiting for is not actually on the far side of any answer. Third, write two short lists: what I do not know, honestly, and what is already settled, both in your circumstances and in what Christ has given, which no outcome changes. The second list is where his peace is drawn from.

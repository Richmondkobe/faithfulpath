Lesson 19 · Peace Before Answers · Slide 3 of 12

Our passage is John chapter 14, verse 27, in the English Standard Version. "Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you. Not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid." It is the night before the crucifixion. Jesus has washed the disciples' feet, told them one will betray him and one will deny him, and said he is going where they cannot yet follow. Nothing about that night is resolved for the disciples. And it is into that room, on that night, that he gives them peace, before a single one of their questions has been answered.

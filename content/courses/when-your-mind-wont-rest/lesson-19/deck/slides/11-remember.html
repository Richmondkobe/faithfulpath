REMEMBER

His peace was given before the answers. You do not need permission you already have.

Lesson 19 · Peace Before Answers · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember. Christ's peace was given to the disciples before the answers, on the night when nothing was resolved, and he has not made it conditional on what you know. You may fear for what you love and still rest, because your watching does not change the outcome, and your presence is what the people you love actually need. Caleb went back to the midweek group the following Wednesday. He did not say he felt better. He said it was the first evening in two months he had not spent at his post, and that his wife had noticed.

WHAT CHRIST'S PEACE IS NOT

Not the absence of concern

Jesus gave his peace to men he had just told to expect trouble. Concern for what you love can coexist with it.

Not a guaranteed feeling

Some feel it as calm. Others feel a steadiness beneath the fear, or nothing at all in exhaustion or grief. It is given whether or not it is felt.

Not certainty about outcomes

It does not tell you the scan result. It tells you that neither you nor the person you love is beyond his care.

Not resignation

Caleb kept praying for his wife's healing. Peace is what lets hope wait without tearing itself apart.

Lesson 19 · Peace Before Answers · Slide 6 of 12

Because this lesson promises peace before answers, I need to say what kind of peace is meant, or it will be misheard. It is not the absence of concern; Jesus gave his peace to men he had just told to expect trouble. It is not a guaranteed feeling; some feel a settled calm, some feel steadiness beneath the fear, and some, in depression or exhaustion, feel nothing at all and must trust it is theirs on his word rather than their emotions. It is not certainty about outcomes; it does not tell you what the scan will show, but that neither you nor the one you love is beyond God's care, whatever it shows. And it is not resignation. Caleb kept praying for his wife's healing. Peace is what lets hope wait without tearing itself apart.

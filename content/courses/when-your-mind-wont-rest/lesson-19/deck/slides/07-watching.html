There are two kinds of watching, and this lesson only asks you to set down one of them. Necessary watching is the appointments, the questions, the practical support, and following the clinical team's guidance; that genuinely matters and remains yours to do. Anxious watching is the mental surveillance beyond that, the hours of scanning for danger that read no scan and change no outcome. Caleb's vigilance did not adjust his wife's treatment by a day. What it did was remove him from the meals, the conversations, and the ordinary tenderness that were the actual content of the months they had together. That watching was not care. Care was what it was preventing.

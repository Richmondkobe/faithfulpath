PRAYER

Lord Jesus, on the night before the cross,
with nothing resolved, you gave your peace.

I bring you what is unresolved,
and I do not pretend it is settled.

I receive, before I know, the peace you already gave.

Amen.

Lesson 19 · Peace Before Answers · Slide 12 of 12

Let us pray. Lord Jesus, on the night before the cross, with nothing resolved, you gave your peace to men who did not yet understand. I confess I have waited to understand before receiving it. I bring you what is unresolved, and I do not pretend it is settled. I go on asking for the outcome I long for. And I receive, before I know, the peace you already gave.

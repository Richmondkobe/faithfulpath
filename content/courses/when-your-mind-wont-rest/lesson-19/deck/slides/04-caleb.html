MEET CALEB

"I can't rest until we know. It would be like leaving my post."

Lesson 19 · Peace Before Answers · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a man I will call Caleb. His wife had finished cancer treatment, and the scan that would show whether it worked was six weeks away. Caleb stopped sleeping well. He stopped coming to the midweek group, and said, with complete sincerity, that he would come back when they knew. "I can't rest until we know," he told me. "It would be like leaving my post." The scan came back encouraging but not conclusive, and another was booked for three months out. Caleb said, in the same voice, that he would breathe in three months. I asked what he would do if that scan was also inconclusive. He did not answer, because he could see where the question went.

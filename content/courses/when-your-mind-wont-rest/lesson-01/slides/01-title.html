Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 1 of 12

Welcome to Lesson 1. Let me ask you something. Does your mind keep working when you want to rest? You lie down. The room is dark. Your body is tired. And your mind starts a meeting you never agreed to attend. Something you said this morning. Something that might go wrong tomorrow. A message that has not been answered yet. If that is you, you are in the right place. The teaching takes about eight minutes. Near the end, you will pause for one small writing practice, so have a pen nearby.

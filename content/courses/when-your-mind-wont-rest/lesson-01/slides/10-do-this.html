Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 10 of 12

Now it is your turn, and it is small. Take your pen. Name one matter your mind keeps returning to. Just one. Write it in a single sentence. Do not solve it. Do not argue with it. Do not pray it away yet. Simply name it, so it is on the page instead of only in your head. Then, if you want, read that sentence to God. You are not asking for anything. You are just showing him what is in the room. Pause the audio now and write your sentence. Press play when you are ready. ...

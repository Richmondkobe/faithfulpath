Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 5 of 12

First, let me say this clearly. Thinking is not the enemy. God gave you a mind. The Bible tells us to consider, to examine, to count the cost. Joseph planned ahead for a famine. That was good thinking. Good thinking goes somewhere. It leads to understanding, or to a decision, or to an action. And when it is done, it stops, and you can rest.

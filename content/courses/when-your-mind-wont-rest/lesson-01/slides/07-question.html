ASK ONE QUESTION

Where is this thought taking me?

If the honest answer is "back to where I started," you are circling.

Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 7 of 12

So here is the one question I want you to remember from this lesson. When you notice your mind running, ask: where is this thought taking me? If it is moving you towards an answer, a decision or a practical step, continue until you can name that next step. Then stop. If the honest answer is "back to where I started," you are circling. That is all you need to notice for now.

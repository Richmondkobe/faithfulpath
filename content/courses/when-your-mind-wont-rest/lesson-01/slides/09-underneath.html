WHAT MAY BE UNDERNEATH IT

"I am trying to feel completely certain before I allow myself to rest."

Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 9 of 12

Sometimes, underneath mental circling, we are trying to feel completely certain before we allow ourselves to rest. It is a rule we have never said out loud, and it will not describe everyone, but see if it fits you. Will they still like me? Will the job be safe? Did I make the right choice? Those questions have no final answer, so the mind keeps searching. And here is the surprising part: after a certain point, more thinking does not bring more clarity. It brings more noise. Imagine a friend who spent four hours wondering whether their pastor was upset with them. You would not say, "Give it four more hours." You would say, "You have thought enough for now. Ask him, decide when you will ask, or set the question down until you have new information."

MEET GRACE

Her body was in bed.
Her mind was still in meetings.

Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a woman I will call Grace. She loves God. She is good at her job. She is a caring mother. Nobody at church would guess what her nights are like. At ten o'clock she turns off the light. By quarter past, she has replayed a short conversation with her boss three times. By eleven, she has moved on to her son's exams, then to a bill that is not even late, then to something her sister said years ago. She prays. Then she opens her eyes, and the meeting starts again. Grace is not lazy. She is not a weak Christian. She is exhausted, and she is ashamed, because she thinks a stronger believer would just hand it all to God and fall asleep. I have sat with many people like Grace. Their problem is not weak faith. It is a mind that has learnt to run without a destination.

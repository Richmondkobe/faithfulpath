MENTAL CIRCLING IS DIFFERENT

It returns to the same question and produces nothing new.

Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 6 of 12

Mental circling is different. It looks like thinking. It feels like being responsible. But it goes around and around the same question and produces nothing new. You reach a conclusion, and twenty minutes later you are back at the beginning, as if the earlier thinking never happened. Nothing is decided. Nothing is released. It only stops when you are too tired to continue, or something distracts you.

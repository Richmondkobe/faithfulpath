Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 8 of 12

Let me put the two side by side. Useful thinking gathers facts. Circling repeats fears. Useful thinking moves towards action. Circling returns to the beginning. Useful thinking has a stopping point. Circling often seeks complete certainty. When the question concerns the future, another person's thoughts, or an outcome you cannot control, that certainty may not be available. Look at that last line, because it explains a lot. When you are circling, your mind is often not looking for more information. It is looking for a guarantee.

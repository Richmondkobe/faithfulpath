PRAYER

Father, you already know
what my mind keeps returning to.

I bring it to you as it is — unsolved.

When the cares of my heart are many,
let your comfort cheer my soul.

Amen.

Lesson 1 · When Your Mind Will Not Switch Off · Slide 12 of 12

Let us pray. Father, you already know what my mind keeps returning to. You know the meetings it holds without my permission. You know how tired I am. I bring it to you as it is, unsolved. I do not ask you to make me stop thinking. Teach me to think with you, and to rest in you while the questions are still open. When the cares of my heart are many, let your comfort cheer my soul. In Jesus' name. Amen. ... You may stop here for today. Nothing is lost if you do. When you are ready, mark the lesson complete.

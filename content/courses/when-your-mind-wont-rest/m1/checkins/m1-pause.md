# Module 1 pause: What am I noticing?

You have finished the five lessons of Module 1. Before moving on, take a few minutes to look back, kindly. These questions are private, optional and unscored. You may answer some, all or none of them, and you can continue to Module 2 whether or not you write anything here. Your answers are not monitored.

1. What have I noticed about my mind during this module?
2. What is one pattern I can now name?
3. Is anything I have noticed telling me to seek professional help? *(If yes, the Finding Help Where You Live page shows how to find support where you live. Deciding to contact someone is a good outcome of this module, not a failure of it.)*
4. What is one practice from these five lessons that I will carry forward?

You do not have to solve your whole life tonight. When you are ready, Module 2 begins with a short Pattern Finder that will suggest which lessons may be most helpful for what your mind has been doing.

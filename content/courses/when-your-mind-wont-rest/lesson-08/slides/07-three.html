THREE THINGS THAT LOOK ALIKE

Preparation

Your facts, your purpose, your opening words. Has an end. Leaves you ready to listen.

Bounded planning

A few realistic responses, held as possibilities. Responsible for hard conversations.

Rehearsal

Scripts the other person. Treats the script as evidence. Never ends.

Lesson 8 · Rehearsing Conversations That Have Not Happened · Slide 7 of 12

Not all thinking ahead is this kind of rehearsal. Three things look alike. Preparation concerns what belongs to you: your facts, your purpose, your opening words, your questions, your boundaries. It has a natural end point, and it leaves you ready to listen. Bounded planning considers a small number of realistic responses so you can stay clear, safe and flexible. For some conversations that is simply responsible: an interview, a disciplinary meeting, a talk with someone who commonly turns things around. Rehearsal is different. It scripts the other person, treats the script as evidence, and continues long after useful preparation has ended. The test is not whether you imagine a response. It is whether your preparation stays bounded, based on evidence, and flexible. Hannah's fortnight was none of those.

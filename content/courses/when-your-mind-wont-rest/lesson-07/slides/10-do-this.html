Lesson 7 · Replaying What You Cannot Change · Slide 10 of 12

Now it is your turn. Take your pen. Take the scene your mind returns to most, and write which one it needs: Repair, Learn, Continue or Release. Then write one sentence for it. If it is Learn, that sentence is the truth you will carry forward. If it is Release, write: this has been dealt with, and I hand it to God. If it is Repair, write only the first safe step, not the whole apology, and if the situation involves serious harm, a legal matter, or someone who has asked for no contact, write instead the name of the person you will ask for advice first, and send nothing today.

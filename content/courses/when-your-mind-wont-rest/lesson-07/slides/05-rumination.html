Lesson 7 · Replaying What You Cannot Change · Slide 5 of 12

Thomas's pattern is usually called rumination: replaying a past event, with regret, guilt or shame, without arriving anywhere new. It is hard for Christians to recognise because it feels virtuous. It feels like responsibility, like humility, above all like repentance. But look at the difference. Repentance moves towards truth and changed action. It names the wrong without minimising it, receives God's forgiveness, makes appropriate repair, and begins living differently. Rumination returns to the scene and produces no new truth, no repair, and no change. It keeps demanding suffering after suffering has stopped serving anyone.

MEET THOMAS

Forgiven five years ago.
Still at the kitchen table every night.

Lesson 7 · Replaying What You Cannot Change · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a man I will call Thomas. Five years ago he persuaded his wife to put most of their savings into a friend's business. Eighteen months later the business failed and the money was gone. Thomas apologised for the pressure his confidence had put on his wife. She forgave him within the year, told him so more than once, and meant it. By every visible measure, the matter was closed. But most nights, between switching off the light and falling asleep, Thomas went back to the kitchen table where he had made the case. Some nights he ran the scene a dozen times, and each time it ended the same way, because it had already happened. "I have confessed it to God more times than I can count," he told me. "And I still cannot stop going back there."

Lesson 7 · Replaying What You Cannot Change · Slide 3 of 12

Our verse is Philippians 3, verses 13 and 14, in the English Standard Version. "Forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus." It matters what Paul is forgetting. He had persecuted the church, and he says so elsewhere without flinching. So forgetting does not mean pretending nothing happened. He remembered. He simply did not live there. That is the posture this lesson is after. Not "pretend it did not happen," but "it happened, it has been dealt with as far as it can be, and I am not required to keep living in it."

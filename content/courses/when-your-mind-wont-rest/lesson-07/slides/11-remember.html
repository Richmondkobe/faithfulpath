Lesson 7 · Replaying What You Cannot Change · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember. It happened. It has been dealt with as far as it can be. I am not required to keep living in it. Paul remembered what lay behind. He simply refused to let it be the place he drew his identity from. Pray about the past as something already dealt with, not something still owed.

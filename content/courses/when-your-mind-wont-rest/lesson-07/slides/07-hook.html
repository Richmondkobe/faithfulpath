Lesson 7 · Replaying What You Cannot Change · Slide 7 of 12

Many people half believe this: "If I stop replaying it, I am letting myself off the hook. Feeling bad is the least I can do." Underneath is an assumption that continued suffering is a payment. Said plainly, that implies the forgiveness you received was not enough. Rumination never says that out loud. It simply keeps you at the kitchen table. And self-punishment does not make people more careful or more loving. Thomas's wife did not benefit from his sleepless nights; she had asked him to stop apologising. Letting yourself off the hook would mean pretending it did not happen, or refusing to make repair. Accepting that it has been dealt with is taking God at his word. Two qualifications: forgiveness does not automatically restore trust or remove consequences, and some people move on too quickly. This lesson is for those who have faced their responsibility and whose minds keep punishing them anyway.

Lesson 7 · Replaying What You Cannot Change · Slide 9 of 12

For Thomas, repair had been made and accepted years ago. The lesson was one sentence: I will not let my confidence do another person's deciding for them. There was nothing ongoing to continue. What remained was release: trusting that his confession had been heard the first time. So the practice is this. Notice the replay and name it: I am replaying. Ask what it is reaching for; often the answer is "to undo," and undoing is not available. Work through Repair, Learn, Continue and Release once, carefully, and keep the page. The next time the scene begins, show your mind the sentence, not the scene: repair is made, the lesson is written, my commitments are being kept, the rest is released. Then turn to the present. Do not run the exercise again, and do not assume the return of the feeling means God requires another confession. If the replay keeps intruding night after night, that is information, and it may be time to talk to someone.

NAME THE PATTERN · CONFIRMATION-SEEKING

Discernment

Uses the ordinary means. Gathers what can reasonably be gathered. Then decides.

Confirmation-seeking

Uses the same means and cannot stop. Asks the same question of one more person. Reads signs in both directions.

It is not seeking guidance. It is seeking the removal of risk, and guidance cannot provide that.

Lesson 10 · When Discernment Becomes Obsession · Slide 5 of 12

Discernment is a real discipline, and this lesson is not against it. It uses the ordinary means: Scripture, wisdom, trusted people, circumstances, and what you actually want. And it is bounded: it gathers what can reasonably be gathered, and then it decides. Confirmation-seeking uses the same means, but cannot stop. It asks the same question of another person, and another, hoping for a more certain answer. It reads a coincidence as a sign and the opposite coincidence as a counter-sign. It is not seeking guidance. It is seeking the removal of risk, and guidance cannot provide that. It is hard to spot because it looks devout, feels responsible, and postpones the cost of choosing. While she was still discerning, Rebecca did not yet have to give anything up.

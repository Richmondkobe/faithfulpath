Lesson 10 · When Discernment Becomes Obsession · Slide 6 of 12

"God has one right choice for me, and if I miss it I will be outside his will." Christians hold different views of how God guides, and I will not settle that here. But whatever your view, three things can be said with confidence. God is not a cruel examiner who condemns sincere believers for failing to decode a hidden message. God's providence is larger than our decisions; Scripture is full of people who chose badly, or without a clear word, and whom he led, corrected and used. And many decisions are between morally permissible options, where Scripture leaves real freedom to choose wisely, even though the options carry different consequences. Rebecca was choosing between two permissible cities. And on signs: an impression or a sermon is weighed, not obeyed automatically. If you are collecting signs in both directions, the signs are not deciding the matter.

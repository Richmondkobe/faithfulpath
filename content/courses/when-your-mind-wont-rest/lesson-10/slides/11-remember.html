God promised wisdom, not a guarantee. Deciding is an act of faith.

Lesson 10 · When Discernment Becomes Obsession · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember. God promised wisdom, not a guarantee. Deciding is an act of faith. You can make a decision with God rather than waiting for him to make it for you, trusting that if it proves unwise, he will remain faithful, and asking for the humility to recognise it and respond well.

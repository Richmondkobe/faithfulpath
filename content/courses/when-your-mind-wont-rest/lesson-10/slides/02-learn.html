Here is what you will learn today: how to tell discernment from confirmation-seeking, how to weigh the ordinary means God has given, and how to answer honestly whether you need more information or more courage. One thing first. If decisions large and small keep becoming impossible, if you feel unable to act without certainty that a choice is God's will, or you cannot stop reviewing a decision after it is made, please consider speaking with a mental-health professional who understands obsessive symptoms and respects Christian faith. That is not weak faith, and the support link is under this lesson.

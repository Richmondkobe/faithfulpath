Lesson 10 · When Discernment Becomes Obsession · Slide 9 of 12

Then answer which fits. I need specific information: name what is missing, where it can be found, and by when, and go and get it. I have enough information and I need courage: you may have reached the point where further discernment is no longer adding anything relevant, and unless circumstances materially change, what remains is the courage to decide, which is itself an act of faith. Or I need both: get the information, and know that when you have it the courage will still be required, so you do not mistake the next piece of information for the last one.

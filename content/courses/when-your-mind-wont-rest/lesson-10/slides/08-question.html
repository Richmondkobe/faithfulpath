Lesson 10 · When Discernment Becomes Obsession · Slide 8 of 12

The tool is one honest question. What information is genuinely missing, and what courage will still be required after I have it? First, notice and name it: I am seeking confirmation. Then take stock of the ordinary means, and include your desire. What you want is relevant information, to be weighed alongside character, responsibility and consequences. It is not divine authorisation, but the overthinker often discounts it entirely, and that is its own error. Once a suitable person has given a careful answer, write it down and use it, instead of asking the next person the same question.

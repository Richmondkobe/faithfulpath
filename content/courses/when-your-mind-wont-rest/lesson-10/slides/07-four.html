Lesson 10 · When Discernment Becomes Obsession · Slide 7 of 12

When your mind will not settle over a decision, there are four faithful responses. Act: you already have what you need; the moral question is clear or absent, counsel has been given, circumstances permit, you know what you want. Wait: there is a genuine reason, information you can obtain, a circumstance about to change, a person still to consult. Waiting is faithful when it has a reason and an end. Seek help: you need expertise you do not have, a doctor, a lawyer, a mature Christian who knows you. Surrender: the decision is not yours, or the outcome is beyond your control, or you have done all that can be done.

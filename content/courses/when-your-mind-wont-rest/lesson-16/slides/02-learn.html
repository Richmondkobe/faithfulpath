Here is what you will learn today: how to notice when prayer has become rehearsal, and a six-movement shape that lets a prayer come to rest, without limiting persistent prayer. Two things first. Prayers or phrases repeated until they feel right, or a prayer you cannot stop because stopping feels dangerous, deserve help from a professional who understands both faith and obsessive-compulsive symptoms. And being unable to pray at all is a common experience in depression and grief. It is not a spiritual verdict, and persistent low mood deserves a doctor's assessment.

Lesson 16 · Prayer That Does Not Become More Worry · Slide 6 of 12

Repeating a request is not evidence of unbelief. Jesus told a parable so that his disciples ought always to pray and not lose heart. In Gethsemane he prayed the same request repeatedly, and Paul pleaded three times about his thorn. The question is what the repetition is doing. Persistent prayer keeps asking without losing heart, open to God's will. Lament returns honestly to unresolved pain. Intercession carries an ongoing need day after day; Elizabeth's nightly prayer was intercession, and it was right. Compulsive repetition repeats in order to neutralise fear or make the prayer feel complete, driven by the sense that stopping is dangerous. The first three are healthy expressions of faith. The fourth may need professional help. The same words can belong to any of them. What distinguishes them is what they are for.

Lesson 16 · Prayer That Does Not Become More Worry · Slide 5 of 12

Some patterns are worth recognising. Prayer that addresses God but talks to itself: the words go upward while the mind rehearses and predicts, and adding "Lord" to the front of each sentence does not change what the sentence is doing. Prayer that circles without lament or trust: the psalms lament, but they do not rehearse imagined futures in ever-greater detail. Prayer that seeks a guarantee: "promise me she will be all right." God gives many firm promises, but not every desired outcome, and a prayer asking for what is not on offer cannot rest. Prayer that cannot come to rest. And prayer that replaces action or help, when what a situation needs is a phone call, a conversation, or a doctor.

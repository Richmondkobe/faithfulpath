A COMMON BELIEF

"If I decide wrongly, I will be outside God's will."

Lesson 18 · When Discernment Is Finished · Slide 6 of 12

Many people are held in place by one belief: "If I make the wrong decision, I will be outside God's will." Scripture gives a different picture. God's revealed will is about how we are to live: with justice, mercy, faithfulness, and love of God and neighbour. Within that there are usually several morally permissible options, and Scripture nowhere promises that God has secretly chosen one and hidden it. That does not make every option equally good. Wisdom is choosing well among permissible things, and a mistaken decision can cause real harm even in good faith. But it does not put you beyond God's reach.

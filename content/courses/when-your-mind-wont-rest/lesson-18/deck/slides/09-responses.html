Lesson 18 · When Discernment Is Finished · Slide 9 of 12

Then one of five responses is the answer. Act: what remains is courage; write what you will do and when. Wait for a defined reason: write what must happen first, because a wait without a trigger is delay wearing wisdom's clothes. Seek qualified help: write whom you will ask, and about what. Move to safety: where there is credible danger, contact emergency or specialist support, and do not announce a plan if that could increase the risk. This comes before everything else. Surrender: write what is not yours to decide. Miriam's sheet ended with act, and a date. Under question ten she wrote a sentence she had never let herself write before: "I am afraid that if I stop, I will find out I was only valued for what I did." That needed a longer conversation, and she had it with her pastor. But it was not a reason to lead the youth ministry for another year.

Lesson 18 · When Discernment Is Finished · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember. A decision is not proved wrong because it cost what you knew it would cost. Nor is it proved right; watch the consequences honestly. When the decision reopens in your mind, do not redo the sheet. Ask one question: has anything actually changed? A changed feeling may deserve brief attention, if it points to new facts, an overlooked obligation, pressure or danger. If not, the decision stands. This closes Module 3. You have learned to test a thought, to pray without rehearsing, to give tomorrow back, and to decide without waiting for certainty.

Here is what you will learn today: how to tell a decision that is missing something from one that is waiting for a feeling, and how to bring one decision to a named response. If the decision is about leaving a relationship in which you are harmed, controlled or threatened, it is a matter of safety, not courage. Plan it with a domestic-abuse service or another qualified professional before any step is taken, and sometimes before anything is said to family or church leaders. If the decision has serious financial, legal, medical or immigration consequences, a qualified adviser belongs in the process. And if even small everyday choices have become paralysing, describe that to a doctor; it is treatable. The support link is under this lesson.

Proverbs 3:5–6 (ESV)

Lesson 18 · When Discernment Is Finished · Slide 3 of 12

Our passage is Proverbs chapter 3, verses 5 and 6, in the English Standard Version. "Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths." "Do not lean on your own understanding" is not an instruction to stop thinking; Proverbs praises the person who plans and takes counsel. Use your understanding, but rest your weight on God. "Acknowledge him" means knowing and honouring him in all your ways; it is not a promise that God will announce the decision. And "he will make straight your paths" is confident covenant wisdom, not a technique for getting a preferred result. It does not tell you which fork to take. What follows is a pastoral application of these verses.

Now it is your turn. Take your pen. Choose the decision that has waited longest, and write it as one sentence. Then write what is actually stopping you. Then name the response that fits: act, wait for a defined reason, seek qualified help, move to safety, or surrender. If the answer is act and the decision is hard to undo, the next step is to arrange the review it needs, not to act tonight.

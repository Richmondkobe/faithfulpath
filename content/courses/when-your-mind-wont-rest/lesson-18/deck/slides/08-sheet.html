Lesson 18 · When Discernment Is Finished · Slide 8 of 12

The tool is the Faithful Decision Sheet: ten questions, answered in writing. One: what decision is actually before me? Two: is any option morally or legally ruled out? Three and four: what do I know, and what important information can still be obtained in reasonable time? If it would change the decision, getting it is the next step. Five: who is affected? A spouse, children or a dependent person may need to be consulted, not just informed. Six: what did counsel actually say, not what I hoped? Seven: what do I desire and fear? Desire is information, not divine authorisation. Eight and nine: what will each option require, risk and close, and can it be undone? A decision that is hard to reverse deserves more care, counsel and time. Ten: what is actually stopping me?

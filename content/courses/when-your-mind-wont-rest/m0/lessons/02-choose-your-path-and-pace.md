# Choose Your Path and Pace

There are several supported ways to use this course, and one may suit your present situation better than the others. You may choose one below or decide later.

Your choice changes only the suggested route shown to you. It never locks a lesson, and you can change paths at any time without losing anything you have written or completed.

## Full six-week journey

Take the foundation course over six weeks:

- Week 1: Lessons 1–5
- Week 2: Lessons 6–9
- Week 3: Lessons 10–12
- Week 4: Lessons 13–15
- Week 5: Lessons 16–18
- Week 6: Lessons 19–21

This is a brisk pace. You may read all the assigned lessons, but choose only one principal worksheet or practice each week. Completing every worksheet is neither required nor recommended.

The 30-day journey is best used afterwards, but it remains available at any time.

## Gentle self-paced journey

Complete one lesson every few days or one each week. There is no deadline.

The lessons build on each other, so following their order is recommended, but you remain free to open the lesson that most closely names what is happening. You may stay longer with any lesson that is especially relevant to you.

The book recommends twenty chapters over twenty weeks as a suitable pace. In the course, the Conclusion appears as a short twenty-first capstone lesson.

## Help for today

Use My Mind Is Restless Right Now to go directly to the lesson and practical resource that most closely match what is happening.

You can return to the complete foundation course from any lesson.

## Alongside counselling or pastoral care

You may use this course alongside pastoral care or counselling. Agree with the person supporting you what place the course should have in your work together and which lessons may be most relevant. It is not a syllabus, and you do not have to cover every lesson.

If you are receiving professional care, your clinician's guidance takes priority over anything in this course, including its exercises and suggested limits. You may want to show them the relevant material.

If a pastor, church leader or group facilitator is accompanying you, the separate Using This Course With Others section provides group and safeguarding guidance. Reading it does not qualify anyone as a counsellor or mental-health professional.

## 30-day practice journey

This path is designed for members who have completed the foundation course or read the book and want to practise its principles gently over time.

Prior completion is recommended but not required. Choosing this path opens the journey directly, and all thirty days remain available without calendar restrictions.

# It Is the Middle of the Night

You do not have to solve anything tonight.

If a concern has arrived in the dark, write it down briefly, choose when you will return to it, and place it into God's hands. That is the whole task.

Do not begin a full worksheet or another thought-analysis exercise tonight. I recommend returning to the teaching lessons during your next waking period.

## Night-Time Thought Download

**What is on my mind:**
Write one brief line for each concern that is already demanding your attention. Do not try to make the list complete.

**When I will look at this:**
Tomorrow during my next waking period / at a specific time I have chosen.

**A short prayer of handing over, if I can:**

If writing makes you more alert, or you find yourself getting up to check whether the note is complete, leave it for daytime.

If sleep does not come, that is not failure. If your sleep has been persistently poor, speak with a healthcare professional.

*"When the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul." (Psalm 94:19, ESV)*

---

**Need more support?** Open When This Course Is Not Enough. Opening that page does not send an alert or contact anyone for you.

**The one exception:** If there is immediate danger, an urgent medical concern or a safety need requiring action now, do not wait until morning. Open Finding Help Where You Live to locate appropriate services, and contact your country's emergency service or an urgent crisis service directly.

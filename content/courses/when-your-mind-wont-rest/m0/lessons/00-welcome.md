# Welcome: You Do Not Have to Solve Everything Today

*A short welcome from Pastor Richmond Kobe. A video or audio version will appear here when it is recorded; the text is the same.*

If you have come to this course, it may be because your mind keeps returning to a decision, a conversation, a person or a fear about what may happen. Perhaps it is worst at night. Perhaps you have prayed about it repeatedly and begun to wonder whether your restless mind says something troubling about your faith.

I created this course for you, and I want you to know three things before you begin.

First, you are not unusual. Over many years of pastoral ministry, in several countries, I have met people whose minds would not rest. Their restless thoughts were not evidence of weak character or deficient faith. Many were responsible and conscientious people who had been carrying too much for too long. Scripture speaks to this experience with honesty and tenderness.

Second, this course will not tell you simply to pray more and think less. Prayer and Scripture are central to every lesson, but you will not be told that anxiety is proof of unbelief or that a Christian who trusted God properly would never struggle with restless thoughts.

Third, this course has limits. It offers pastoral formation, not medical or psychological treatment. Understanding what your mind may be doing and practising faithful responses can help you relate to your thoughts with greater wisdom, honesty and compassion. But some difficulties require support from a doctor, counsellor, therapist or specialist service. In those situations, this course may accompany appropriate care, but it cannot replace it. Every lesson opens with a short note about when you may need more than this course, and there is a page called When This Course Is Not Enough. Please take time to read them. They are not fine print; they are part of the care offered by this course.

Take the lessons slowly. You do not have to complete every exercise. You may skip an exercise or stop if your distress increases. "Not today" is a legitimate answer.

Your journal and reflection answers are private and are not monitored. Writing that you need help does not alert Faithful Path or contact a support service. If you need immediate assistance, use the support information provided in the course.

This course does not promise to cure anxiety or guarantee a quiet mind. It will help you recognise what your restless mind may be doing, respond with biblical truth and practical wisdom, and begin building a quieter, more sustainable way of living with God.

You do not have to solve your whole life tonight.

May God, who knows what you need before you ask him, meet you here.

**Pastor Richmond Kobe**

# Before You Begin: A Personal Intention

Two questions, and only two.

If you wish, answer them in a sentence or two each, in your own words. There is nothing to rate and nothing to get right. Your answers are saved in your private journal so that you can read them again at the end of the course.

You may answer either question, both questions or neither.

**What brought you to this course?**

**What would you like to understand or practise?**

Your answers are private and are not monitored. Writing that you need help does not alert Faithful Path or contact a support service. You may edit or delete your answers at any time.

You do not need to decide whether your mind is "bad enough" for this course or whether you belong here. If your mind has been restless, you are welcome here. This course may be helpful on its own or alongside appropriate professional or pastoral support.

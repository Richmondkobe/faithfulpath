# My Mind Is Restless Right Now

You do not have to start at the beginning.

If your mind is circling right now and you need somewhere to begin, choose the description that comes closest to what is happening. It does not have to be a perfect match. You can return to the complete course from any lesson.

Each lesson and resource opens with any safety or suitability guidance that applies to it. Please read that short note before beginning. You may leave any worksheet undone.

**If this is happening…**

*The list below is generated from the `entries` above; it is reproduced here only so the page can be read as plain Markdown.*

- **My mind is predicting disaster** → Lesson 6, *When Your Mind Predicts Disaster* · Possibility, Probability and Present Reality
- **I am replaying something I cannot change** → Lesson 7, *Replaying What You Cannot Change* · Repair, Learn, Continue or Release?
- **I am rehearsing a conversation that has not happened** → Lesson 8, *Rehearsing Conversations That Have Not Happened* · What Was Said and What Did I Imagine?
- **I am trying to work out what someone thinks of me** → Lesson 9, *Trying to Read Other People's Minds* · Facts, Assumptions and Other Possible Explanations
- **I keep asking God for one more confirmation** → Lesson 10, *When Discernment Becomes Obsession* · Do I Need More Information or More Courage?
- **I am checking and re-checking** → Lesson 11, *Perfectionism and the Fear of Getting It Wrong* · Faithful, Wise or Perfect?
- **I am carrying everyone** → Lesson 12, *Carrying Everyone in Your Mind* · What Is Mine to Carry?
- **My prayers have become more worry** → Lesson 16, *Prayer That Does Not Become More Worry* · The Six-Movement Prayer
- **I am living in tomorrow** → Lesson 17, *Give Tomorrow Back to God* · Today, Later or Release?
- **A decision has been waiting a long time** → Lesson 18, *When Discernment Is Finished* · The Faithful Decision Sheet
- **I cannot rest until I know** → Lesson 19, *Peace Before Answers* · What Am I Waiting to Know Before I Permit Myself to Rest?
- **It is the middle of the night** → Open the night-time page. It offers only the Night-Time Thought Download and does not direct you into another thought exercise.

If choosing between these options becomes another form of checking, stop. You do not have to identify the perfect lesson. Choose one that seems close enough, return to the course home or open the support page.

None of these worksheets treats an anxiety disorder, depression, OCD or trauma. If a qualified professional is supporting you, their guidance takes priority over anything here.

If you may act on thoughts of harming yourself, cannot keep yourself safe or are in immediate danger, open **Finding Help Where You Live** and contact an emergency or crisis service directly now. Opening that page does not alert Faithful Path or contact help for you.

**Need more support?** Open When This Course Is Not Enough.

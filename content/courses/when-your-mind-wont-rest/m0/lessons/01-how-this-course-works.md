# How This Course Works

This course is built from my book *When Your Mind Won't Rest*. Lessons 1–20 each contain one chapter from the book, and Lesson 21 is drawn from its Conclusion. The lessons retain the book's order because the teaching develops across the course, although you remain free to open any lesson when you need it.

The four modules of the foundation course follow the book's four parts. An optional thirty-day practice journey is also available. It is best used after completing the foundation course or reading the book, but it is not locked.

## The shape of every lesson

Each lesson follows the same shape, so that you always know where you are.

- **What this lesson will do for you** — one sentence.
- **Before you begin** — any safety, repetition or suitability guidance that belongs before the teaching.
- **Key Scripture** — the lesson's central passage, read in its setting.
- **One truth to carry with you** — for a day when you have little capacity. If you read nothing else, read this.
- **The lesson** — the main teaching, including a shortened opening story and a common misunderstanding.
- **Read the complete chapter** — the full chapter from the book in one closed section. Open it when you want the complete treatment.
- **Pause and notice** — one gentle question, not a demand.
- **Guided prayer** — the chapter's prayer, with audio where it has been recorded.
- **Your next faithful step** — one concrete action from the chapter. You may choose privately: *I intend to try this*, *Not today* or *This is not appropriate for me*. None of these responses is wrong.
- **Journal reflection** — two or three questions for your private journal. Writing is optional.
- **Worksheet** — the chapter's exercise as a printable resource.
- **Closing declaration** — a few sentences you may read or say aloud if they are helpful.
- **If you need more support** — guidance specific to the lesson and a link to When This Course Is Not Enough.
- **Continue or rest here** — explicit permission to stop.

When you decide that you have finished for the day, use the **I have finished this lesson for today** button. Finishing a lesson does not mean that you have mastered its subject, completed its worksheet or carried out its suggested action.

## Your journal and worksheets

Everything you write in the journal is private to your account. Journal and reflection answers are not monitored, included in analytics or shown in administrator course-completion views. Writing that you need help does not alert Faithful Path or contact a support service. You may edit or delete your entries at any time, and you can download your journal as a PDF whenever you wish.

A few boundaries apply throughout the course:

- **Complete each thought-analysis worksheet once for one matter, and then stop.** Do not repeat it to make the answer feel certain or perfect. If you notice that you are repeating a worksheet to feel certain, put it down and use the support guidance in the relevant lesson. Some prayers and ordinary practices may be used again when the course specifically invites you to repeat them.
- **You do not have to complete every worksheet.** Exercises marked optional, and any exercise that touches memory or fear, may be left undone. "Nothing comes to mind" is a legitimate result. "Not today" is a legitimate answer.
- **Stop any exercise that increases your distress.** Use the support guidance if you need help deciding what to do next.
- **If a qualified professional is supporting you, their guidance takes priority** over anything in this course, including the exercises and their limits.
- **Do not begin a full worksheet in the middle of the night.** If a concern arrives in the dark, use only the Night-Time Thought Download and return to the lesson during the day.
- **Do not build a routine or write a rule of life in the middle of a crisis.** Keep what is helping you remain safe and supported, accept appropriate help, and return to those lessons later.
- **Never start, stop or change medication because of anything in this course.**

## Getting help at any time

Every course page carries two links: **Need more support?**, which opens When This Course Is Not Enough, and **My Mind Is Restless Right Now**, which takes you to the lesson and resource that most closely match what is happening right now.

Neither link is locked. Neither sends a message, alerts Faithful Path or contacts a support service.

If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical or crisis support, opening a course page is not enough. Use the emergency and crisis information on the support page to contact help directly.

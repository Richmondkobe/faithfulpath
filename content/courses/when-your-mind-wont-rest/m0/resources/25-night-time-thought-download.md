# Night-Time Thought Download

*Worksheet 25 in the printed toolkit · a general worksheet, for any night*

For when a concern arrives in the dark. A brief note only. Do not begin a full worksheet in bed.

**What is on my mind:** one brief line for each concern that is already demanding your attention; no sorting, no analysis. Do not try to make the list complete.

**When I will look at this:** tomorrow during my next waking period / at a specific time I have chosen.

**A short prayer of handing over, if I can:**

If writing increases alertness, or you find yourself getting up to check that the note is complete, leave this sheet for daytime. If sleep does not come, that is not failure; if sleep has been persistently poor, speak with a healthcare professional.

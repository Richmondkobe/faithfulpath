# Control, Influence or Surrender

*Worksheet 24 in the printed toolkit · a general worksheet*

A short version of the sorting that runs through the course, for a matter with many moving parts.

**The matter:**

**Control (what is mine to decide or do, now or at a named time):**

**Influence (what I can contribute to but not decide: a conversation I can have, help I can offer, an appropriate question I can ask, a referral I can make):**

**Surrender (what is neither mine to decide nor mine to influence, and belongs to God or to someone else):**

**Safety (anything here that involves credible danger, which comes before all of the above):**

**One thing from “control” to do today:**

This sheet maps onto the course’s five responses: control is act or wait for a defined reason; influence is share, offer, ask appropriately, or seek qualified help; surrender is release. Do not put under “surrender” what actually belongs under “seek help” or “safety.”

MEET RUTH

Eleven years in the church. Leads a home group.
Anxious for months, and ashamed of it.

Lesson 3 · Anxiety, Faith and Shame · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a woman I will call Ruth. She waited until the end of a church meeting, when almost everyone had gone home, before she came to me. She had been a member for eleven years. She led a home group. "Pastor, I need to ask you something, and I need you not to think less of me. I have been anxious for months. I know all the verses. I have cast my cares on him, over and over, and they keep coming back. So either the verses don't work, or I don't have real faith. And I don't know which is worse." Ruth was carrying two burdens. The first was anxiety. The second was shame about the anxiety. And the shame was cutting her off from the very people who could have helped her carry the first.

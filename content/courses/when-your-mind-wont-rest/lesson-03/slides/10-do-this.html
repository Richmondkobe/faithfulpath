Lesson 3 · Anxiety, Faith and Shame · Slide 10 of 12

Now it is your turn, and it is small. Take your pen. Write one sentence you have told yourself about your faith because of anxiety. Write it exactly as it sounds in your head, however harsh. Then rewrite it truthfully. For example, "If I trusted God, I would not feel this way" becomes "I feel afraid, and I am choosing to trust God with it." Or, "I should be able to pray this away" becomes "Prayer is where I bring this, not a switch that removes it." Do it once. Do not go back to check whether you got it right; your standing with God does not depend on a sentence. Pause the audio now and write your sentence. Press play when you are ready. ...

Here is what you will learn today. First, how to separate what you feel from what you choose. Second, how to hear the sentences that turn anxiety into shame, and test them against what God has actually said. One honest word before we start. In this lesson, "anxiety" means the ordinary experience of worry, fear and bodily alarm. If anxiety is persistent, or it is affecting your sleep, work or daily life, please also speak with a doctor or a mental-health professional. Faith and treatment are not rivals.

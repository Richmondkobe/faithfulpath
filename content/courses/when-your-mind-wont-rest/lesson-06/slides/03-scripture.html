Lesson 6 · When Your Mind Predicts Disaster · Slide 3 of 12

Our verse is Matthew 6, verse 34, in the English Standard Version. "Do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble." Notice that Jesus does not promise tomorrow will be trouble-free. He says the opposite: tomorrow will have its trouble, and so does today. This is an honest description of life, spoken by the one person who knew exactly what tomorrow held for him. Then he says today has enough in it. Israel gathered manna by the day. We ask for daily bread. Trying to live through every possible tomorrow in advance leaves us empty for today.

Lesson 6 · When Your Mind Predicts Disaster · Slide 2 of 12

Here is what you will learn today: how to name a prediction as a prediction, and how to separate three things dread mixes together: what is possible, what is probable, and what is actually happening now. One caution first. If there is evidence of real danger, abuse, violence, coercion or a medical emergency, do not use this lesson to persuade yourself the danger is probably imaginary. Move to safety and contact appropriate help. And if vivid images of disaster arrive suddenly and drive you to check or seek reassurance again and again, a mental-health professional can help; the support link is under this lesson.

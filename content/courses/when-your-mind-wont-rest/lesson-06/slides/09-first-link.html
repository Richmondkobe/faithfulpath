Lesson 6 · When Your Mind Predicts Disaster · Slide 9 of 12

When Esther wrote her chain down, link by link, she could see that five of her six links rested on nothing she knew. So here is the practice. Slow the chain down enough to see it. Name it: "I am predicting." Write the chain exactly as your mind produced it. Then ask the four questions of the first link only. If the first link does not hold, the rest of the chain has nothing to hang from. Ask them once, carefully, and then turn to the meal, the task, the person in front of you. If the prediction comes back an hour later, remind yourself briefly of your answers; do not run the whole exercise again. Running it repeatedly turns a tool for clarity into a ritual for reassurance.

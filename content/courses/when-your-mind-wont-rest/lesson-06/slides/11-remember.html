REMEMBER

My mind can imagine many tomorrows. I have been given only today.

Lesson 6 · When Your Mind Predicts Disaster · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember. My mind can imagine many tomorrows. I have been given only today. Bring the present reality, not the imagined future, to God. There is a kind of prayer that is only catastrophising with "Lord" attached to the front of every sentence; we will look at that in Lesson 16. For now: tell him what is actually true, ask him for what today requires, and leave tomorrow where he has put it.

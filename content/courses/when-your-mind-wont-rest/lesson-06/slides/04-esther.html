MEET ESTHER

4:30 pm: "Could you come and see me at eight tomorrow?"
8:00 pm: How long will my savings last?

Lesson 6 · When Your Mind Predicts Disaster · Slide 4 of 12

Let me tell you about a teacher I will call Esther. At half past four on a Thursday she got a short email from the head of her school: "Could you come and see me in my office at eight tomorrow morning? Thanks." By five she had decided she was being dismissed. By six, a parent must have complained. By eight she had worked out how long her savings would last and which subjects she might tutor. She slept for about three hours. At eight the next morning the head asked whether she would take on an extra class, because a colleague was going on maternity leave. The meeting lasted six minutes. "The worst part," Esther told me, "is that I knew while I was doing it. I knew I had no evidence. I did it anyway." If you recognise that sentence, this lesson is for you.

# Get Help Now

*Emergency and support page · contact information last checked 15 September 2026. Services, hours and numbers can change.*

**If you are in immediate danger, or you may act on thoughts of ending your life, use your country's emergency number now.** 112 in most of Europe · 911 in the United States and Canada · 999 in the United Kingdom · 000 in Australia · in Thailand, 1669 for a medical emergency and 191 for the police.

If you do not know your country's number, go to the nearest emergency department, or ask a trusted person nearby to help you contact the appropriate local emergency or crisis service. Contact police when police assistance is needed and it is safe to do so.

**To find a crisis line for your country tonight:** Find A Helpline (findahelpline.com) provides verified helpline information for more than 150 countries, by phone, text and online chat. The International Association for Suicide Prevention (iasp.info) also directs people seeking crisis support to Find A Helpline. Availability and hours vary by country.

**Other directories:** Befrienders Worldwide (befrienders.org) · Child Helpline International (childhelplineinternational.org) · Lila.help, for domestic abuse (lila.help).

**Some examples, correct when checked.** Thailand: medical emergency 1669; police 191; Department of Mental Health hotline 1323; Social Help Centre 1300; Samaritans Thailand 02 113 6789, press 2 for the English callback service, which is not a substitute for emergency assistance. United Kingdom and Republic of Ireland: Samaritans 116 123, free day or night. United States: call or text 988. Canada: call or text 9-8-8. Australia: Lifeline 13 11 14. New Zealand: call or text 1737.

**To find ongoing care**, the usual first step is a primary-care doctor, who can assess what is happening, consider whether physical factors may be contributing, discuss appropriate care and make a referral where necessary. Ask what is free or subsidised, what can be done by telephone or video, and whether a church or charity near you can help you reach it.

**A word about your church.** A pastor can be one of the best people to help you find and reach professional help. But pastoral care is not clinical treatment and should not be asked to take its place.

*This course does not monitor what you write. Journal entries, reflections and check-ins are not monitored, and writing that you need help does not alert Faithful Path or contact any of these services. If you need a person, contact one of them directly.*

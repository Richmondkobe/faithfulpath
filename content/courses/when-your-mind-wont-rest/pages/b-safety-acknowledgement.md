# Three Boundaries to Acknowledge

*Course orientation page · from "When This Course Is Not Enough"*

Please read and acknowledge these three boundaries before continuing with the foundation journey. They protect the purpose of the course and remind you where additional help is available.

- I understand this is pastoral formation, not medical or psychological treatment.
- I know where to find "When This Course Is Not Enough".
- I understand I can stop or skip any exercise.

*On the site, the course records only whether each statement has been checked and the acknowledgement date. The boxes are not a safety assessment, consent to treatment or a waiver of any right. Ticking them does not tell Faithful Path that you are safe, and it does not contact help. Nothing in the course is locked.*

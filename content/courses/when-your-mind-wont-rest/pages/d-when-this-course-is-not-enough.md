# When This Course Is Not Enough

*Emergency and support page · the short form. The full page is in the course.*

Please seek more than this course if any of the following describes you. Doing so is not a failure of faith, and it is not a failure of the course.

- **Thoughts of suicide or self-harm.** Contact appropriate support today, and emergency or crisis services now if you may act, cannot keep yourself safe or are in immediate danger. Do not remain alone.
- **Panic or urgent physical symptoms.** Do not assume that chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, fainting or sudden confusion is anxiety. Seek urgent medical assessment.
- **Severe disruption to daily life**, or sleep problems that are severe, persistent or worsening.
- **Trauma**, or a memory that returns as vivid images, nightmares or a sense of reliving.
- **Abuse, danger or a safeguarding concern**, for you or for someone in your care.
- **Severe or persistent anxiety or low mood.**
- **Pregnancy and the period after birth**, where thoughts are accompanied by an urge to act, or by confusion, hallucinations or loss of contact with reality: this is a medical emergency.
- **Obsessive or compulsive patterns**, including repeated checking, confessing or reassurance-seeking.
- **A course exercise that has become a problem**: compulsory, distressing, or impossible to stop.

If a qualified professional is already supporting you, their guidance takes priority over anything in this course, including the exercises and their limits.

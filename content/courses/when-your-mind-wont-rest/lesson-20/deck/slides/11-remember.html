Lesson 20 · A Rule of Life for a Restless Mind · Slide 11 of 12

One thing to remember. When you miss a practice, return without a reckoning. The trellis may change, or fall for a season, but God's faithfulness does not depend on your keeping it. Jonathan did not need a harder version of the tools he already had. He needed a small, ordinary rhythm that made the tools less necessary in the first place, and room to return to it gently when a busy season knocked it over. That is what this lesson has offered you: not one more thing to get right, but a place for everything else in this course to live.

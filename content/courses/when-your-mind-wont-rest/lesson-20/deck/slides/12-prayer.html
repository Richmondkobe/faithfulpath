PRAYER

Lord, build in me a small and gracious rhythm
for the life I actually have.

When I miss it, let me return without a reckoning.

The trellis may fall. Your faithfulness does not.

Amen.

Lesson 20 · A Rule of Life for a Restless Mind · Slide 12 of 12

Let us pray. Lord, build in me a small and gracious rhythm for the life and season I actually have. Let it turn me toward you, support my body, protect my attention, and keep me connected to safe people and appropriate care. Save me from using it to earn peace, measure my worth, or punish my own inconsistency. When I miss a practice, let me return without a reckoning. The trellis may change or temporarily fall. Your faithfulness does not. In Jesus' name. Amen. ... You may stop here for today. Nothing is lost if you do. When you are ready, mark the lesson complete.

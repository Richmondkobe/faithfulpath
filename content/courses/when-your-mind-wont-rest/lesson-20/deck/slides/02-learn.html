Here is what you will learn today: how to build a small, gracious weekly rhythm under five simple headings, and why we call it a trellis rather than a law. One caution before we begin. If a rule like this one ever becomes rigid, if missing an item produces disproportionate distress, or you feel you must complete items in a particular way or order for the day to feel safe, stop using it and speak with a qualified professional who can assess what is happening. And medication, if you take it, is never changed because of anything in this lesson. The support link is under this lesson.

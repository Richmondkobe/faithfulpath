Lesson 20 · A Rule of Life for a Restless Mind · Slide 7 of 12

A few honest limits, so the rule does not overpromise. Habits like a regular bedtime can support sleep, but they do not guarantee it, and they are not a treatment for insomnia; if sleep problems are severe or persistent, that is a conversation for a healthcare professional. Choose movement that is medically appropriate for your own body; ask your doctor if you are unsure, especially after a long period of inactivity. And on church: the aim is not attendance at any cost. Some readers are housebound, unwell or recovering from a harmful church experience. Seek safe and accessible community in whatever form your circumstances permit, and do not return to an unsafe congregation merely to satisfy a weekly rule.

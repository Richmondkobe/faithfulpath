Welcome to Lesson 20. Here is today's question. What happens to your tools when life gets too busy to use them? A good tool used only in crisis is still a good tool, but a life with nothing supporting it will keep producing crises. The teaching takes about eight minutes. Near the end, you will pause for one small writing practice, so have a pen nearby.

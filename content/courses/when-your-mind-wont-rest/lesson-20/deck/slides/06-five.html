Scripture, read steadily and unhurried, and safe, accessible Christian community.

Lesson 20 · A Rule of Life for a Restless Mind · Slide 6 of 12

Here are the five areas the rule is arranged under. Centre: prayer and communion with God, the thing everything else is arranged around — not necessarily long or early, but regular, at a time, place or cue the rest of the week knows to protect. Formation: steady, unhurried Scripture reading, and safe, accessible Christian community in whatever form your circumstances permit. Embodiment: sleep, movement and rest, supporting the body rather than treating a disorder. Boundaries: work and digital life, a limit you write for your own week and can actually keep. And Support: the people and professionals you can actually call on, arranged before a crisis, not during one.

A TRELLIS, NOT A LAW

A "rule of life" is not a legal code. It is a trellis: a simple structure that helps something living grow toward the light.

You will not build a commitment in every area. Establish one small central practice, then one or two supporting ones where they fit.

Lesson 20 · A Rule of Life for a Restless Mind · Slide 5 of 12

That word, "rule," can sound heavy, so it is worth pausing on it. A rule of life is not a legal code to be obeyed or broken. It comes from a word meaning a guiding measure, and the picture I want you to hold is a trellis: a simple structure that helps something living grow toward the light. It does not make the plant grow. It gives what is already alive somewhere to climb. And you will not build a commitment in every area of life at once. Establish one small central practice, then add one or two supporting ones where they genuinely fit your week.

Lesson 20 · A Rule of Life for a Restless Mind · Slide 8 of 12

Here is a belief worth naming: "If I miss a day, the whole rule has failed, and so have I." A rule built to earn peace or measure your worth has stopped being a trellis and become one more source of anxious monitoring, exactly what this whole course has been helping you set down. A trellis that has started to strangle the plant is not doing its work. So the rule includes, from the start, a grace clause: what you will do, gently, when you miss a line. The rule serves you. You do not serve the rule, and missing a day is information, not a verdict.

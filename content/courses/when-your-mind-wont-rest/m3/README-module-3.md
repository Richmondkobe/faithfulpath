# When Your Mind Won't Rest — Module 3 content (draft for review)

Module 3 — Renew Your Mind Without Fighting Yourself (Chapters 13–18). Course map v3 governs structure; conventions as in Modules 1 and 2.

## What is in this package

- `lessons/13…18` — the six teaching lessons in the approved template. All six carry a safety boundary, so each runs to roughly 1,060–1,250 words: intrusive and perinatal thoughts (13), depression and blame-the-sufferer teaching (14), compulsive monitoring and trauma-linked material (15), scrupulosity in prayer and inability to pray (16), real hardship, danger and persistent anxiety (17), decisions involving abuse, money, law, health or immigration (18).
- `resources/13…18` — the six worksheets from the chapter exercises, each opening with its own boundary box and carrying `resource_safety_required: true`. Worksheet 18 (The Faithful Decision Sheet) is marked **NOT FOR GROUP SHARING**, as the course map requires.
- `checkins/m3-pause.md` — the unscored Module 3 pause, "My faithful response", ending in the five responses.

## Round 1 corrections applied (15 Sept 2026)

- Outcomes reworded across all six so none promises categorical certainty ("recognise", "distinguish", "use a pastoral test").
- Lesson 13: postpartum psychosis now stated to affect the person who has recently given birth; "fighting it usually makes it worse" replaced with "repeatedly fighting, suppressing or neutralising it can make it more persistent or distressing"; "intrusive rather than testable" replaces "merely intrusive".
- Lesson 14: the course-wide two-stage suicide wording; "for those who belong to Christ, loss cannot place them outside his covenant care"; the action now excludes confronting an abusive, coercive or dangerous situation alone.
- Lesson 15: direct Night-Time Thought Download instruction in "Before you begin" and on the worksheet; "Established" now asks what reliable evidence supports a claim; the peace sentence reframed ("Paul places the promise of guarding peace before the instruction"); footer support wording made non-medicalising; "reread what you concluded" replaces "reread your verdict".
- Lesson 16: `audio: six-movement-prayer`; the inner-voice sentence rewritten ("does not require an inner impression, special message or sense of certainty"); a scrupulosity note added above the guided prayer; direct night-time instruction naming the Night-Time Thought Download.
- Lesson 17: `audio: give-tomorrow-back-to-god`; the action now begins with the urgency check and no longer implies an urgent item can wait for "today"; direct night-time instruction; "for those who belong to Christ, your Father will still be Father tomorrow".
- Lesson 18: the Move to safety wording rewritten throughout so it never reads as "leave now" — specialist domestic-abuse service from a safe device or place, an individual safety plan, no confronting or announcing, emergency services if danger is immediate; worksheet privacy stated in copy and front matter; the course-wide suicide boundary added; "pervasive indecision can accompany depression, anxiety or another difficulty".
- **Four/five responses:** the book develops this deliberately. Chapter 2 and Chapter 10 give four (act, wait, seek help, surrender); Chapter 18 adds Move to safety as the fifth. Lessons 16 and 17 therefore keep four, with a bridge sentence: the four ordinary responses apply after the urgency check, and where danger is present the prior response is to move to safety or seek urgent help. Lesson 18 names all five.
- Lesson lengths now 1,060–1,270 words. Lessons 17 and 18 remain slightly above 1,100 because their urgency check and five-response section were kept intact in preference to length, as the review asked.

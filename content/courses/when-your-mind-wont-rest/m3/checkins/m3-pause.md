# Module 3 pause: My faithful response

You have finished the six lessons of Module 3. Before moving on, you may find it helpful to bring one thing you are carrying through the whole of what this module has taught. This is optional, private and unscored. Your answers are not monitored, and you are never required to submit them to receive completion credit for anything.

Take one thought or decision, and write briefly:

1. **The thought or decision I have been carrying.**
2. **What is known.**
3. **What remains unknown.**
4. **What belongs to me now.**
5. **My next faithful action.**
6. **What I will release for now.**
7. **When, if necessary, I will review it.**
8. **Who can support me.**

Then name the response that fits, using the five the course has been building towards: **act**, **wait for a defined reason**, **seek qualified help**, **move to safety**, or **surrender**. If there is credible immediate danger, move to safety comes before everything else, and Finding Help Where You Live shows where to start.

Complete this once for one matter. If you find yourself returning to redo it because the answer did not bring the feeling you wanted, that is the pattern Lessons 10 and 18 describe, and the answer is to stop rather than to try again.

Module 4 turns from renewing the mind to building a quieter life: peace before answers, a rule of life for a restless mind, and the course's closing lesson.

# When Your Mind Won't Rest — Module 4 content (draft for review)

Module 4 — Build a Quieter and More Sustainable Life (Chapters 19–20 and the Conclusion). Course map v3 governs structure; conventions as in Modules 1–3, with every rule established in the earlier review rounds applied from the start.

## What is in this package

- `lessons/19` — Peace Before Answers (John 14:27). Safety: waiting for a serious answer, when to tell the clinical team, scrupulosity about assurance, and the rule that a wait concerning safety is a matter for action, not rest.
- `lessons/20` — A Rule of Life for a Restless Mind (Mark 1:35). Safety: no ambitious rule in acute crisis, rigidity as a sign to seek assessment, the rule as no substitute for care, and the medication rule.
- `lessons/21` — You Do Not Have to Solve Your Whole Life Tonight (Matthew 6:34), the capstone drawn from the Conclusion. Front matter carries `capstone: true`, `issues_certificate: true` and `certificate_requires: "Lessons 1-21 all marked finished"`. It has no worksheet; its guided prayer is the Final Prayer of Surrender, with the book's own introductory line for uncertain readers.
- `resources/19`, `resources/20` — the two chapter worksheets, each opening with its boundary box.
- `resources/26-personal-digital-boundary.md`, `resources/27-support-map.md` — the two general toolkit pages Chapter 20 refers to, attached to Lesson 20 so a member can print them without opening the whole toolkit.
- `checkins/m4-pause.md` — the Module 4 pause, written as a practical course review so it does not repeat Lesson 21's reflection, and ending with the "ready for the journey, or would rest be better?" question.

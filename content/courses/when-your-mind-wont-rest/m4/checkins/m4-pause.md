# Module 4 pause: Course review

You have finished the foundation course. Lesson 21 gathered it theologically; this page is the practical review, and it asks different questions so that you are not answering the same ones twice. It is optional, private and unscored, and it does not affect your certificate, which becomes available once all twenty-one lessons are marked finished.

1. **Which lesson helped me most?**
2. **Which practice will I keep?**
3. **Which practice was not appropriate or helpful for me?** *(A practice that did not suit you is information, not failure. Some are deliberately optional.)*
4. **Do I need any additional support?** *(If the help notes spoke to you more than once, this is the place to say so plainly. Finding Help Where You Live shows where to begin.)*
5. **Am I ready for the 30-day journey, or would rest be the better next step?**

That last question is a real question. The thirty-day journey is optional, it is not locked, and it will still be there in a month or in a year. Some people finish the foundation course and want to begin practising straight away. Others have had enough for now, and the right next step is to put the course down, keep the one practice they chose, and live an ordinary few weeks. Both are good answers.

Your answers here are not monitored. Writing that you need help does not alert Faithful Path or contact a support service.

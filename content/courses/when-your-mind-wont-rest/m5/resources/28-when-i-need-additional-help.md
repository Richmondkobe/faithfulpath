# When I Need Additional Help

*Worksheet 28 in the printed toolkit · Day 29*

> **Private.** An honest page. Completing it is not a failure; for some members it is what the course was for. Your answers are not monitored, and writing that you need help does not alert Faithful Path or contact a support service — if you need a person, use Finding Help Where You Live.

**Recently, has my worry or low mood been severe, persistent, rapidly worsening, or interfering with sleep, work, relationships or daily functioning?**

**Have any of the help notes in this course described me?**

**Have any of the practices become compulsory, produced significant distress, or felt impossible to stop?**

**Who will I contact, and when?**

---

*If you cannot keep yourself safe, do not wait for a routine appointment. Use Finding Help Where You Live now, and contact emergency or urgent crisis services if you may act on thoughts of harming yourself.*

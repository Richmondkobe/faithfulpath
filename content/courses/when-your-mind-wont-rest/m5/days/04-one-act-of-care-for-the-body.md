# Day 4: One act of care for the body

**Ordinary** · Week One: Notice

## Scripture

“Arise and eat, for the journey is too great for you.”

## Focus

Lesson 5. Sleep loss, illness, physical discomfort, irregular meals, stress or stimulants may contribute to a restless mind. They do not explain every case, but they are part of the story.

## Practice

No writing today. Do one ordinary thing for your body that you have been putting off: a proper meal, a walk, an earlier bedtime, a glass of water in the afternoon instead of another coffee. Nothing to analyse.

## Prompt

None. Let the day be the practice.

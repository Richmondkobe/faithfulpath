# Day 19: Today, later or release

**Worksheet** · Week Three: Release

## Scripture

“Seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness.”

## Focus

Lesson 17. Preparing for tomorrow is wisdom. Living in it before it arrives is not.

## Practice

Complete “Today, Later or Release?” for the tomorrow you have been living in most. Check first for anything urgent. Then sort: today, later with a trigger, or release for now with a written reason. Set a review date for the later list.

## Prompt

Which item have I been treating as actionable even though no responsible action is currently available?

# Day 6: Noticing triggers

**Action** · Week One: Notice

## Scripture

“Search me, O God, and know my heart.”

## Focus

Lessons 1-5 together. Ask God for honest awareness while noticing the recurring situations and states in which your mind circles most, without treating every trigger as morally significant.

## Practice

Look back over the week and use the toolkit’s “Common Triggers” worksheet lightly: tick what you have noticed (late evening, the phone, a particular person, hunger, an unanswered message, Sunday night). Then take one practical step today about the trigger that is easiest to change.

## Prompt

Which trigger cannot be changed and will have to be met differently?

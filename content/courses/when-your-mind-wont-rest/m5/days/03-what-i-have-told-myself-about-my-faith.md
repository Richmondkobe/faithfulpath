# Day 3: What I have told myself about my faith

**Worksheet** · Week One: Notice

## Scripture

“When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.”

## Focus

Lesson 3. Fear is an experience. Trust is a direction. The two can travel together.

## Practice

Complete “What Have I Been Telling Myself About My Faith?” Write the sentences you have believed about what your anxiety says about you as a Christian, and beside each, what Lesson 3 said instead.

## Prompt

Do I already know where the most damaging sentence came from? If not, I do not need to search for an origin.

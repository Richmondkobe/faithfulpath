# Day 28: Do one thing

**Rest** · Week Four: Practise

## Scripture

“He gives to his beloved sleep.” This verse presents rest as gift rather than achievement, and translators differ on whether God gives sleep to his beloved or gives to them while they sleep. It is not a promise that completing today’s practice will make sleep come.

## Focus

The rule begins.

## Practice

Do one line of your rule today, at its cue, and then rest. If it is your day of rest, let this be the rest.

## Prompt

One sentence, if one comes: what did I do today, and what did I let be?

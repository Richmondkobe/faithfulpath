# Day 1: What has been occupying my mind

**Worksheet** · Week One: Notice

## Scripture

“When the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul.”

## Focus

Lesson 1. Before anything can be tested or released, it has to be seen.

## Practice

Complete “What Has Been Occupying My Mind?” from Lesson 1. Write down, without editing, the things your mind has returned to most in the past week. Do not sort them yet.

## Prompt

Which of these surprised me by being on the list?

# Day 15: One thought, examined

**Brief** · Week Three: Release

## Scripture

“Take every thought captive to obey Christ.”

## Focus

Lesson 13. Second Corinthians 10 concerns arguments opposed to the knowledge of God, not a command to police every private thought; this exercise is a careful pastoral application. It is examination of a claim, and a response, not suppression.

## Practice

At one planned time during waking hours, choose one recurring thought that makes a testable claim and use the six movements once: notice, name, test, refuse, reorient, act. Do not monitor your mind through the day looking for a suitable thought.

## Prompt

Did the claim survive testing? What did “act” turn out to be?

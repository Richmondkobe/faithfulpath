# Day 17: The six-movement prayer

**Worksheet** · Week Three: Release

## Scripture

“Pour out your heart before him; God is a refuge for us.”

## Focus

Lesson 16. Prayer that has somewhere to go, so that it does not become more worry.

## Practice

Pray “The Six-Movement Prayer” in writing for the matter that has most turned your prayers into worry: become present, name the reality, pour out your heart, make your request, remember and discern, entrust the outcome. Then do what movement five said.

## Prompt

Which movement did I usually skip before today?

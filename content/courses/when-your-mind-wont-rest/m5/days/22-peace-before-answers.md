# Day 22: Peace before answers

**Worksheet** · Week Four: Practise

## Scripture

“Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you.”

## Focus

Lesson 19. Christ gave peace to his disciples before their answers came. He has not made yours conditional on knowing.

## Practice

Complete “What Am I Waiting to Know Before I Permit Myself to Rest?” Write the sentence, separate necessary monitoring from anxious watching, and choose one suspended thing to resume or adapt this week.

## Prompt

If the answer I am waiting for came, what would I then be waiting to know?

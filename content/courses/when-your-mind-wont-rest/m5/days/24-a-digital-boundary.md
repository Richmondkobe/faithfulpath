# Day 24: A digital boundary

**Action** · Week Four: Practise

## Scripture

“Look carefully then how you walk… making the best use of the time.”

## Focus

Lessons 15 and 20. Where the phone lives in the evening shapes where the mind lives at night.

## Practice

Look at the toolkit’s “Personal Digital Boundary” and choose one boundary you can keep this week: when work ends and how you mark it, where the phone lives in the evening, what you check before bed. Adapt it for anyone who may need to reach you. Put it into practice tonight.

## Prompt

What do I check last thing at night, and does it help me sleep?

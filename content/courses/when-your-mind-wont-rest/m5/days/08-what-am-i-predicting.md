# Day 8: What am I predicting

**Worksheet** · Week Two: Test

## Scripture

“Sufficient for the day is its own trouble.”

## Focus

Lesson 6. A prediction is not yet established; it is not the same as a fact.

## Practice

Take the matter your mind has predicted worst outcomes for and complete “Possibility, Probability and Present Reality”: what am I predicting, what evidence supports or challenges it, what is happening now, what does the present require.

## Prompt

Where in the chain did the evidence run out?

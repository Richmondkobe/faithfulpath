# Day 16: What is mine to carry

**Brief** · Week Three: Release

## Scripture

“Bear one another’s burdens… For each will have to bear his own load.”

## Focus

Lesson 12. Not every need you can see is yours to meet.

## Practice

Take one person you have been carrying in your mind. Using the five questions from Lesson 12, write which category they belong in: carry, share, offer, refer or release. Reduce only an unnecessary or unrequested action; do not withdraw from an established responsibility as an experiment.

## Prompt

If the answer was “release,” what am I releasing, and to whom does it belong?

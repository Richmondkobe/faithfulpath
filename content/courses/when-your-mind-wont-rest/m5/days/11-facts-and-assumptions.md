# Day 11: Facts and assumptions

**Brief** · Week Two: Test

## Scripture

“Love… believes all things, hopes all things.”

## Focus

Lesson 9. An observation is not an interpretation, and one event is not a pattern. Love is not gullibility, though: do not generate charitable explanations to dismiss an established pattern of deception, coercion or harm.

## Practice

Take one person whose mind you have been trying to read. Write what you actually observed, stripped of the motive you supplied. Write two other possible explanations, then stop. Decide: ask clearly, seek appropriate clarification, wait with ordinary respect, or let go.

## Prompt

Was this a single event or a pattern? What is the evidence for the answer?

# Day 10: Said and imagined

**Worksheet** · Week Two: Test

## Scripture

“If one gives an answer before he hears, it is his folly and shame.”

## Focus

Lesson 8. Prolonged rehearsal can replace the real person with a version written by fear. Bounded preparation may still be useful.

## Practice

Take one conversation you have been rehearsing and complete “What Was Said and What Did I Imagine?” Separate the two columns honestly. Decide: ask, not yet, no, or ask someone else. Keep any written imagined dialogue to ten lines, and stop if it becomes distressing.

## Prompt

How much of the conversation that has been troubling me actually happened?

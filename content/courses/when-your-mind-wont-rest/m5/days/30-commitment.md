# Day 30: Commitment

**Brief** · Review and Commitment

## Scripture

“He who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ.”

## Focus

Not a finish line. A beginning.

## Practice

From the “what helped” list, choose two or three practices to carry forward, and write where they live in your rule. Then pray the Final Prayer of Surrender, which is on this page below, or, if you are uncertain about belonging to Christ, pray only what you can say honestly and turn to the Start Here page “If You Are Not Sure You Belong to Christ”.

## Prompt

One sentence, to be read in a month: what do I want to remember from these thirty days when my mind will not rest?

## The Final Prayer of Surrender

*You may use the words that are true for you, adapt them or remain silent. An audio recording in Pastor Richmond's voice is available here.*

If you belong to Christ, you may pray the following words as an expression of trust. If you are uncertain, pray only what you can say honestly, and return to the Start Here page “If You Are Not Sure You Belong to Christ” without forcing certainty.

Father,

I have carried more than I can hold. I have taken up tomorrow’s trouble, yesterday’s regret, other people’s responsibilities and outcomes I cannot control. Some of it came from love, some from fear, and much of it became too tangled for me to separate alone. I bring it to you without pretending that I have sorted it perfectly.

Show me what faithfully belongs to me now. Give me courage to act where action is appropriate, patience where waiting has a real purpose, and humility to seek qualified help where I lack what I need. Where there is danger, lead me toward safety and toward people equipped to help me take the next possible step.

What lies beyond my role and control—the outcome, another person’s heart, the scan, the verdict, the past I cannot change and the future I cannot see—I entrust to you. When I take it up again tomorrow, receive it again without reproach.

I do not need to prove my faith by feeling calm. My hope rests in Christ, not in the condition of my mind. Give me the peace Christ gives his people: not a feeling I must produce, but the assurance that I am not left alone while questions remain unanswered.

When my mind circles again, remind me that its return is not failure. Help me notice what is happening, remember what is true, and return to the next faithful thing.

Sufficient for the day is its own trouble. Let this day be enough. Give me whatever rest is possible tonight, and if sleep does not come, keep me from turning wakefulness into condemnation.

In the name of Jesus, who prayed honestly in the garden, rested in the boat, and has not left his people orphaned.

Amen.

## The end of the journey

You do not have to solve your whole life tonight. You did not have to solve it in thirty days either. What you have done is notice, test, release and practise, and you can continue tomorrow with the Father whose faithfulness did not depend on how well you completed the journey.

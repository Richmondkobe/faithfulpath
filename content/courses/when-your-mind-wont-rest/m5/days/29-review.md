# Day 29: Review

**Brief** · Review and Commitment

## Scripture

“His mercies… are new every morning.”

## Focus

Look at the month as a whole, kindly.

## Practice

Read back over the month, using the tracker if you kept one. Write three short lists. What helped: the practices that gave your mind somewhere to go. What did not: the practices that were not for you, or not for this season; leave them without guilt. What became difficult: any exercise you kept redoing to feel certain, or any routine that produced distress when missed. Then complete the toolkit’s “When I Need Additional Help” worksheet honestly.

## Prompt

Did this month show me that I need more than this course? If so, what is the next step, and who will I contact?

> Note. If a practice repeatedly became compulsory, produced significant distress, interfered with daily functioning, or felt impossible to stop, contact a qualified mental-health professional. A single uncomfortable or overextended exercise does not diagnose a condition. And if the help notes spoke to you throughout the month, please treat the “When I Need Additional Help” worksheet as the most important page of the plan. Contacting a doctor, a qualified professional or another appropriate service is not a failure of the thirty days. For some readers, it is what the thirty days were for.

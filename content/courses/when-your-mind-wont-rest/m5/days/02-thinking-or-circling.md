# Day 2: Thinking or circling

**Brief** · Week One: Notice

## Scripture

“For everything there is a season… a time to keep silence, and a time to speak.”

## Focus

Lesson 2. Not all repeated thinking is overthinking. Some of it is planning, discernment or problem-solving.

## Practice

Choose one item from Day 1 that returned frequently and run it through the five questions of “Healthy Thinking or Mental Circling?” Leave the other items for later chapters or for professional discussion.

## Prompt

If this was circling, what was the thinking hoping to achieve that it could not?

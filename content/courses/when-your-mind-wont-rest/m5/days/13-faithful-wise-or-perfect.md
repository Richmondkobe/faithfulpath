# Day 13: Faithful, wise or perfect

**Action** · Week Two: Test

## Scripture

“He who observes the wind will not sow.”

## Focus

Lesson 11. Flawless certainty and protection from every criticism are not available. Responsible excellence should not be confused with fear-driven perfectionism.

## Practice

Choose one low-risk task you have been over-checking or delaying. Do it faithfully, to the standard it actually requires, and stop. No worksheet today. If you have OCD, do not drop any checks abruptly; choose a task your professional would consider appropriate.

## Prompt

What did “done faithfully” look like, and what did stopping feel like?

# Day 12: Information or courage

**Brief** · Week Two: Test

## Scripture

“If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God.”

## Focus

Lesson 10. James promises that God gives wisdom generously; it does not promise that every decision will be answered by a private sign. Christians understand impressions and signs differently, but none should bypass Scripture, evidence, consent, safety or relevant expertise.

## Practice

Take one matter you have been seeking confirmation about. Write which of these is true: I need more information; I need more courage; both; neither, because the decision or outcome is not mine. If information is genuinely missing, write where it can be obtained.

## Prompt

How many times have I asked the same question, and what was the asking doing?

# Day 23: The six-movement prayer, again

**Brief** · Week Four: Practise

## Scripture

“For God alone my soul waits in silence.”

## Focus

Lesson 16. Repetition is not unbelief. The shape is there to help prayer rest, not to limit it.

## Practice

Pray the six movements, in your head or on paper, for a different matter from Day 17, or return to the same unchanged need as an act of persistent prayer or lament. Stop only if you notice that you are repeating the structure to neutralise fear, to obtain certainty, or to make the prayer feel correct.

## Prompt

Was today’s prayer persistence, lament, intercession, or an attempt to neutralise fear?

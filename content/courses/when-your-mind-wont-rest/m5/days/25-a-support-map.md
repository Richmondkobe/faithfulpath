# Day 25: A support map

**Brief** · Week Four: Practise

## Scripture

“Two are better than one… For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow.”

## Focus

Lessons 12 and 20. A restless mind isolates. Support should be arranged before it is needed.

## Practice

Sketch the toolkit’s “Support Map”: the people who support you, listen wisely, share appropriate burdens or help you reach professional care; the professionals you have or may need; the Christian community available to you. If there is a safe person on it, mark what, if anything, you would be comfortable telling them this week. Disclosure is optional and only ever to safe people.

## Prompt

Who on this map have I been contacting only in crisis?

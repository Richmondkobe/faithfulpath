# Day 18: From false reassurance to biblical truth

**Brief** · Week Three: Release

## Scripture

“Be transformed by the renewal of your mind.”

## Focus

Lesson 14. Romans 12 describes the transformation of judgement, desire, conduct and communal life in response to God’s mercy. Truthful self-talk is one application of it, not the whole meaning.

## Practice

Write one reassurance you have been giving yourself (“it’ll be fine,” “God won’t let that happen”). Beside it, write what Scripture actually says, in context. One pair only; the full worksheet is in the Course Journal and Practical Toolkit.

## Prompt

Is what God has actually said harder or easier to rest on than what I had been telling myself?

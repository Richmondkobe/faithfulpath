# Day 9: Replaying what I cannot change

**Brief** · Week Two: Test

## Scripture

“Forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead.”

## Focus

Lesson 7. Rumination and repentance are not the same thing.

## Practice

Take one past event your mind keeps returning to. Write one line: does it need repair, learning, continuing or release? If repair, note only whether repair is safe and appropriate; do not plan it today. The full “Repair, Learn, Continue or Release” worksheet is in the Course Journal and Practical Toolkit for when you have time.

## Prompt

Have I been confessing a regret as though it were a sin?

# Day 27: My rule for this season

**Worksheet** · Week Four: Practise

## Scripture

“He would withdraw to desolate places and pray.”

## Focus

Lesson 20. A trellis, not a law.

## Practice

Complete the rest of “My Peace-Supporting Weekly Rhythm”: one practice toward God, one for the body, one boundary, one form of support, the grace clause, and a review date. Tie each to a time, a place or a reliable cue; shift workers, carers and people with fluctuating health do not need a fixed clock time. Keep it to half a page.

## Prompt

Could I keep this in a week when everything else went wrong?

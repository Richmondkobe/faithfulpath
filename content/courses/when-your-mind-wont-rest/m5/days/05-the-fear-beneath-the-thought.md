# Day 5: The fear beneath the thought

**Brief, optional** · Week One: Notice

## Scripture

“I pour out my complaint before him; I tell my trouble before him.”

## Focus

Lesson 4. Sometimes the surface thought is not the whole story. Sometimes it is.

## Practice

Take one circling item and ask “If this happened, what would it mean to me?” no more than twice. Treat any answer as a possibility to consider, not a diagnosis, a recovered memory or a revelation from God. If no deeper sentence comes, write that; it is a legitimate result. Stop if the exercise increases distress.

## Prompt

Does this item need a practical response, a response to a deeper fear, both, or neither?

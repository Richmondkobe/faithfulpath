Here is what you will learn today: how to tell an observation from an interpretation, how to consider other explanations, and how to choose whether to ask, wait or let go, rather than acting on a conclusion the evidence does not support. One thing to say clearly at the start. This lesson is about stories built from too little evidence. Where there is repeated unkindness, broken promises, contempt or control, that is not a story; it is a pattern, and it is a reason to seek help, not to keep interpreting.

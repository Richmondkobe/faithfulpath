Lesson 2 · Thinking, Worrying or Overthinking? · Slide 10 of 12

Now it is your turn. Take your pen. Choose one subject your mind returned to today. Just one. Ask the five questions once, and write a yes or a no beside each. Producing a clear action? Learning anything new? Within my control? Already decided? Helping me obey God today? If the honest picture is that nothing new is happening, you may say this once: "The season for working on this has ended for today." Then turn to one ordinary thing: a dish to wash, a person to speak to, a walk. The words do not have to bring relief before you move on. Pause the audio now and ask your five questions.

THE MAIN TRUTH

The difference is the fruit, not the subject.

Lesson 2 · Thinking, Worrying or Overthinking? · Slide 6 of 12

Here is the main truth. The healthy four and the circling three are not separated by subject. You can reflect on a conversation, or ruminate on it. Plan for a meeting, or worry about it. Same subject. What separates them is the fruit. Jesus said you know a tree by its fruit. So ask: what is this thinking producing? Reflection produces understanding. Planning produces steps. Worry, rumination and obsession produce more of themselves. That is why "Is it important?" and "Am I being thorough?" cannot tell you if your thinking is healthy. Daniel's decision was important and he was thorough, and he was stuck, because he measured his thinking by how serious it was, not by what it produced.

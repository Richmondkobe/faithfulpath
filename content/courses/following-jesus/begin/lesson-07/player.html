I do not know what to say when I pray. Do my simple prayers count?

Read the Scripture · Matthew 6:5–8

“And when you pray, you must not be like the hypocrites. For they love to stand and pray in the synagogues and at the street corners, that they may be seen by others. Truly, I say to you, they have received their reward. But when you pray, go into your room and shut the door and pray to your Father who is in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will reward you.” Matthew 6:5–6 · ESV

“And when you pray, do not heap up empty phrases as the Gentiles do, for they think that they will be heard for their many words. Do not be like them, for your Father knows what you need before you ask him.” Matthew 6:7–8 · ESV

Prayer can be simple, because your Father knows what you need.

and forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors.

Many churches add: “For yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory, forever. Amen.” (in a footnote in the ESV)

Understand the Teaching

The short answer

Yes, your simple prayers count. God is not impressed by long words, and he is not put off by short ones. What matters is the heart, not the length. Christian prayer is the honest response of God’s children to their Father: praising him, trusting him and bringing everything to him.

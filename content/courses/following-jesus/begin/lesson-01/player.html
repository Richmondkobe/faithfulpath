The good news in one sentence

Jesus, the promised King, has come. He died for our sins and rose again, so that through him God now offers forgiveness, reconciliation with God, new life and hope to everyone who turns to him and trusts him.

Prayer

Father, thank you for the good news of Jesus.

Thank you that you did not wait for us to fix ourselves, but loved us while we were still sinners.

Thank you that Jesus is the promised King, who died for our sins and rose again.

Teach me to turn from my own way and trust Jesus more fully.

Help me to understand this good news, live in its truth and share it clearly.

In Jesus’ name. Amen.

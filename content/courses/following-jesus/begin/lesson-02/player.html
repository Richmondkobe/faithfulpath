Understand the Teaching

No.

You do not have to fix your life first.

God comes to us in grace. We turn to him and trust Jesus, and we receive what God freely gives. Our changed lives are the fruit of his welcome, not the price of it.

Thank you that I do not have to make myself good enough before coming to you.

I turn from my own way and from the things I know are wrong.

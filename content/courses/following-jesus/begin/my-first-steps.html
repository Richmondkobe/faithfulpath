A word about completing Begin

What this means

Completing Begin means you have worked through all eight lessons, in a group or on your own. It marks the completion of this first course; it does not mean you have finished growing or that completing the course qualifies you to lead, teach or counsel others. Following Jesus is lifelong, and the next stage is Book 2, Establish.

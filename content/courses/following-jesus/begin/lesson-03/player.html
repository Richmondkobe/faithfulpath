Understand the Teaching

The short answer

To be born again means that God himself gives a person new life. When we receive Christ, God does something in us that we could never do for ourselves: by his Holy Spirit he gives us new life, makes us new, and begins a lifetime of growth.

1 · From Above

“But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God, who were born, not of blood nor of the will of the flesh nor of the will of man, but of God.” John 1:12–13 · ESV

2 · By the Spirit

“But when the goodness and loving kindness of God our Savior appeared, he saved us, not because of works done by us in righteousness, but according to his own mercy, by the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit.” Titus 3:4–5 · ESV

regeneration = rebirth or renewal: the giving of new life

John: born from above · Paul: regeneration and renewal → God’s life-giving work

3 · Made New

“Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come.”2 Corinthians 5:17 · ESV

4 · Born to Grow

“Like newborn infants, long for the pure spiritual milk, that by it you may grow up into salvation.”1 Peter 2:2 · ESV

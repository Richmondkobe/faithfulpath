Understand the Teaching

The short answer

Following Jesus is personal, but it was never meant to be private. When God makes us his children, he brings us into his family. No church is perfect, but we belong and grow together.

Biblical priorities and wise signs

  • Centred on Jesus; the gospel clearly proclaimed
  • Teaches the Bible faithfully
  • Prays
  • Loves people, including newcomers and the struggling
  • Handles money responsibly and openly
  • Leaders serve, are accountable, and do not stand above Scripture

Understand the Teaching

The short answer

Yes, it matters how we live, and yes, following Jesus can be costly. But neither of these turns salvation into something we earn. Jesus is not only the one who saves us; he is the risen King. We follow him because we have been loved and welcomed, not to earn his love.

1 · Jesus Is Lord

“Why do you call me ‘Lord, Lord,’ and not do what I tell you?” Luke 6:46

A King who gave his life for his people

2 · Count the Cost

“If anyone comes to me and does not hate his own father and mother… he cannot be my disciple.”Luke 14:26

deliberately stark language about supreme loyalty

“Whoever loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me.”Matthew 10:37

Not contempt for family: first place for Jesus

3 · Obey From Grace

“If you love me, you will keep my commandments.”John 14:15

the response of love, not the price of love

“For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation for all people, training us…”Titus 2:11–12

Grace is not only a gift at the beginning; it is a teacher for the whole journey.

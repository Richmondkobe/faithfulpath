Where It Points

The Spirit poured out across God’s people

The Short Answer

The Holy Spirit is God, present in every believer. He gives power to witness to Jesus, grows the fruit of Christlike character, and gives gifts for the good of others.

We welcome his work gladly, and we test every claim by Scripture.

Understand the Teaching

Welcome and test

“Do not quench the Spirit. Do not despise prophecies, but test everything; hold fast what is good.”1 Thessalonians 5:19–21 · ESV

A genuine work of the Spirit:

  • points to Jesus
  • does not contradict Scripture
  • builds people up
  • produces the fruit of the Spirit

Test with wise counsel in the church, not alone

Experiences are never the proof of salvation; our assurance rests on Christ

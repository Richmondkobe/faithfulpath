The Short Answer

The world is beautiful because God made it good. It is broken because humanity turned away from God. And every person, without exception, is made in God’s image.

Two things to say clearly

1 · Genesis 3:16describes a consequence of the Fall. It is never permission to dominate, harm or silence women.

Your private page

My Foundations in Following Jesus

These are reflection points, not requirements for earning God's acceptance or permission to continue. You may move into Grow while still working on them. Simple words are enough.

My beliefsfrom Lessons 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

In simple words: who is God, who is Jesus and what did he do, and what does salvation include? What does it mean that every person bears God's image, and that human sin has brought brokenness into God's good world?

My life with Godfrom Lessons 1 · 8

Choose one passage and work through it with the four questions. Then name the rhythm of Bible reading, prayer and worship you will keep.

My life in the Spiritfrom Lesson 6

How is the Spirit present with every believer? Name one fruit he is growing in you, and one way you can use what God gives you to serve or encourage others.

My churchfrom Lessons 7 · 9

What is your next step with baptism, the Lord's Supper, belonging or serving? Your next step may simply be to keep attending, observing and asking questions.

If church leadership has been unsafe for you, choose someone outside that situation to talk with.

My hopefrom Lesson 10

What do Christians expect when Christ returns, and how does that hope change the way you live now?

If you are still exploring, you are welcome to write what Christians believe, beginning, “This is what Christians believe about…”. No one will ask you to say publicly whether you have become a Christian.

What you write is saved to your account and is not shared with your group or other learners.

Before you move on

Look outward

Think of one way you have served or encouraged someone during this course. You may keep the person's name and circumstances private.

When you have finished looking through My Foundations, choose the response that best fits today. Your choice does not change your completion of Establish.

A word about completing Establish

What this means

Completing Establish means you have completed the Establish stage of the Following Jesus pathway: you have worked through all ten lessons, in a group or on your own. It records participation and completion. It does not mean that you are a mature Christian, or that you are qualified or approved to lead, teach, mentor or counsel others. Following Jesus is lifelong. The next stage is Book 3: Grow.

Keep walking with him

“Therefore, as you received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in him, rooted and built up in him and established in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving.” Colossians 2:6–7

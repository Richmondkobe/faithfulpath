Read the Scripture · Romans 5:1–2, 5

“Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Through him we have also obtained access by faith into this grace in which we stand, and we rejoice in hope of the glory of God.” “…God’s love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit who has been given to us.” Romans 5:1–2, 5 · ESV

“…while we were still weak, at the right time Christ died for the ungodly.” “…but God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.” “For if while we were enemies we were reconciled to God by the death of his Son…” Romans 5:6, 8, 10 · ESV

The Short Answer

Salvation means that God rescues us from sin and its results, for a new life with him, now and forever.

Saved fromthe guilt and power of sin · separation from God · final judgement and death
Saved forpeace with God · his family · growing freedom from sin · good works · a renewed creation

past · present · future: all of it rests on his grace

Part 1 · past

Declared Right

“…justified by his grace as a gift, through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.”Romans 3:24

“For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God.”2 Corinthians 5:21

Not earned · received by faith, as a gift

Part 2 · past and present

Welcomed as Children

“…so that we might receive adoption as sons… So you are no longer a slave, but a son, and if a son, then an heir through God.”Galatians 4:5, 7

“…heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ…”Romans 8:17

Every believer, women and men alike

Part 3 · present

Being Made Holy

“…are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another.”2 Corinthians 3:18

“…work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who works in you…”Philippians 2:12–13

A lifetime of growth, by the Spirit

Understand the Teaching

Fruit, not price

“For by grace you have been saved through faith… not a result of works… created in Christ Jesus for good works…”Ephesians 2:8–10

“…faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead.”James 2:17

Holiness is becoming like Jesus, not keeping extra human rules as though they make us right with God

Part 4 · future

Kept for Glory

“…much more shall we be saved by him from the wrath of God… much more, now that we are reconciled, shall we be saved by his life.”Romans 5:9–10

“…who by God’s power are being guarded through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.”1 Peter 1:5

Nowpeace with God
Nowhis family
Nowbeing changed
Futurea sure hope

Our hope rests on God’s grace and keeping power, not on a flawless record

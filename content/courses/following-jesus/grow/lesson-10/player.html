The council and the test

The Short Answer

God guides his people as they trust him, walk in his word, seek wise counsel, and test what they hear before taking a humble step.

Part 1

Trust the Lord

“For all who are led by the Spirit of God are sons of God.”Romans 8:14

“The heart of man plans his way, but the LORD establishes his steps.”Proverbs 16:9

“If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God…”James 1:5

Part 2

Walk in His Word

“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”Psalm 119:105

No hidden “perfect” choice to find or ruin God's plan

Never guided to deny Christ or to do what Scripture forbids

Part 3

Seek Wise Counsel

“Without counsel plans fail, but with many advisers they succeed.”Proverbs 15:22

Counsel informs a decision; it does not take it away

Part 4

Test and Take a Step

“Do not despise prophecies, but test everything; hold fast what is good.”1 Thessalonians 5:20–21

A decision may be wise without certainty that God privately revealed it

Part 4

Decide humbly, keep walking

“If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that.”James 4:15

Peace may help, but it is not the final test

Anxiety alone does not mean the decision was wrong

Christians Differ

How does God speak today?

Many expectthe Spirit to guide through prophecy, impressions, dreams and visions, as well as Scripture
Acts 2:17–18 · 16:9–10 · 1 Corinthians 14:1–5
Others emphasiseScripture, wisdom and God's ordering of circumstances, and are cautious about impressions
Hebrews 1:1–2 · 2 Timothy 3:16–17

Across historic Christian traditions: claimed guidance must not contradict Scripture; the Spirit leads God's people

No claimed word may be used to control another person

Remember

We trust the Lord, walk in his word, seek wise counsel, and test what we hear before taking a humble step.

Trust the Lordguidance begins with relationship; ask God for wisdom, and trust him when the way is unclear

Walk in His Wordmuch of God's will is clear; within it, God gives freedom and wisdom

Seek Wise Counseldecide with a few wise people; counsel informs, but does not take, the decision

Test and Take a Stepweigh every claimed word; then decide humbly, and let God redirect you if needed

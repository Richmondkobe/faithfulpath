The Short Answer

We confess our sin honestly and make things right where we can. We forgive others, releasing revenge to God. We seek reconciliation where it is possible and safe. And we protect ourselves and others with healthy boundaries.

Part 1

Agreeing with God about our sin, without excuses

“I acknowledged my sin to you… and you forgave the iniquity of my sin.”Psalm 32:5

“…if I have defrauded anyone of anything, I restore it fourfold.”Luke 19:8

Part 2

Forgive Freely

“…forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you.”Ephesians 4:32

“Never avenge yourselves, but leave it to the wrath of God…”Romans 12:19

Not: it didn't matter · forget it · excuse it · no justice

“Freely” does not mean quickly, easily or under pressure

Part 3

Reconcile Where Possible

“If possible, so far as it depends on you, live peaceably with all.”Romans 12:18

Part 4

Protect With Boundaries

“…Jesus on his part did not entrust himself to them…”John 2:24

A boundary protects what God has entrusted to you · wise care, not revenge

“I will not lend more money.” · “If shouting begins, I will end the conversation.”

Where there is control or abuse: specialist advice and a safety plan first

Four Different Things

Not the same thing

Forgivenessreleasing revenge, entrusting justice to God · one person can take this step

Reconciliationsome restoration of the relationship · needs truth and willingness from both

Trustconfidence based on proven reliability · earned over time, not owed

Safetyprotection from further harm · comes first, especially for children and vulnerable people

Christians Differ

Does forgiveness depend on repentance?

Some teachfull forgiveness is given when the wrongdoer repents; the wronged person still gives up bitterness and revenge
Luke 17:3–4
Others teachbelievers should forgive even without repentance, as Jesus prayed for those crucifying him
Mark 11:25 · Luke 23:34

Across historic Christian traditions: forgiveness is distinct from restored trust; trust is not owed and should reflect repentance, safety and changed behaviour

No one is required to remain in harm

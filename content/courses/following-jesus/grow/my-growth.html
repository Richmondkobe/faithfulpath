My Growth in Following Jesus

These are reflection points, not requirements for earning God’s acceptance or permission to continue. You may move into Multiply while still working on them. They are prompts for reflection, not a maturity score: no one is expected to have finished growing in any of them. Simple words are enough. You do not need to answer every prompt. Use the ones that help you reflect today.

Grow by gracefrom Lessons 1 · 2

In simple words, how does grace both save us and change us, and how do we begin again after failure? Name one truth that helps you test what shapes your thinking.

Grow in characterfrom Lessons 3 · 4

Name one part of the Spirit’s fruit you have been focusing on, and one healthy way you bring your emotions to God or seek help when you need it.

Grow in relationshipsfrom Lessons 5 · 6 · 7 · 9

Explain the difference between forgiveness, reconciliation, trust and safety. Name one way you are showing Christ’s love in family, friendship or church, one truth about honouring God with your body and relationships that you want to remember, and one step you have taken towards peace.

Forgiving someone does not mean you must return to an unsafe situation. If a relationship is unsafe, see the Support Page: Safety and Support: Start Here.

Grow in everyday lifefrom Lessons 8 · 10

Name one way you are working with integrity or practising generosity, and one decision you have weighed with Scripture, counsel and prayer.

Keep going through hardshipfrom Lesson 11

Write or say one honest lament, or one hope you hold in suffering.

You may stop at any point. If this brings up overwhelming feelings, pause. You may talk with someone you trust or use the Support Pages.

Keep a rhythmfrom Lesson 12

Review your rhythm. What is small enough to keep in this season? Then think of one person you have encouraged or supported during Grow. You may keep the person’s name and circumstances private.

If you are still exploring, you are welcome to write what Christians believe, beginning, “This is what Christians believe about…”. No one will ask you to say publicly whether you have become a Christian.

What you write is saved to your account and is not shared with your group or other learners. If you wish, you may share one part with your mentor or group.

Before you move on

Choose a response

When you have finished looking through My Growth, choose the response that best fits today. Your choice does not change your completion of Grow.

A word about completing Grow

What this means

Completing Grow means you have completed the Grow stage of the Following Jesus pathway: you have worked through all twelve lessons, in a group or on your own. It records participation and completion. It does not mean that you are a mature Christian, or that you are qualified or approved to lead, teach, mentor or counsel others. Following Jesus is lifelong. The next stage is Book 4: Multiply.

The Short Answer

We can bring our pain, protest and questions honestly to God. Doubt can sit alongside real faith. Our hope rests on God's presence now and on the resurrection to come. And we keep going together, held by God and by his people.

Christians Differ

Healing and the cross

Some teachhealing is provided through Christ's death; pray in faith for healing today (some believe it is promised to every believer now, others do not)
Isaiah 53:4–5 · Matthew 8:16–17 · James 5:14–15
Others agreeGod heals today, but full healing belongs to the resurrection; God does not always heal now
Romans 8:23 · 2 Corinthians 12:7–10

God's rule and suffering

Some emphasiseGod's sovereign rule over all things and his ability to bring good through suffering; God is not the author of evil
Genesis 50:20 · Romans 8:28
Others emphasiseGod's opposition to evil; much suffering is what God permits in a fallen world, not what he directly causes
Luke 13:1–5 · James 1:13

Romans 8:28: God works in all things for good, not “all things are good”

Across historic Christian traditions

God is able to heal, and Christians pray for the sick · seeking appropriate medical care is consistent with faith

Continuing illness does not prove weak faith

God is good · evil is real · God will one day end suffering

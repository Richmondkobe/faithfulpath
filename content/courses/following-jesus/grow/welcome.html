Welcome

You are very welcome here

Welcome to the third stage of the Following Jesus pathway. Begin helped you take your first steps: the gospel, new life in Christ, and first steps in Scripture, prayer and church. Establish helped you understand what Christians believe and how that truth shapes life. Grow asks a more personal question: how does Jesus reshape my character, relationships, decisions and ordinary life?

Over twelve lessons we look at grace and growth, renewing the mind, the character of Christ, emotions and wounds, forgiveness and reconciliation, loving others, the body and relationships, work and money, words and conflict, guidance and decisions, suffering and doubt, and a sustainable rhythm of life with God.

Grow is about becoming like Jesus over time. It is not a self-improvement programme, and it is not a ladder to climb to earn God’s love. It begins where all Christian growth begins: with grace.

If you are starting with Grow, you are very welcome. If the Christian gospel is new or unclear to you, we recommend watching Begin, Lesson 1 first. It explains the good news of Jesus that everything in Grow builds on. If you are still exploring who Jesus is, you are welcome too.

Every practice is an invitation, not a test. No one will ask you to read aloud, pray aloud or share your personal story. You can take Grow on your own, with a friend or mentor, or with a church group; one lesson a week works well.

If you or someone else needs support or urgent help at any point, start with the Support Page “Safety and Support: Start Here”.

Inside each lesson

Five movements

  1. 1Read the ScriptureThe main passage, read aloud, with the first question: what does it say?
  2. 2Understand the original meaningOne Hebrew or Greek word, only where it really helps the main passage.
  3. 3Explore the backgroundThe setting of the passage, and where it connects in the Bible’s larger story.
  4. 4Understand the teachingA short answer and four memorable parts, with a fair note where Christians differ.
  5. 5Practise what you have learnedA short story, common mistakes, this week’s assignment, and a prayer.

On each lesson page

Six simple parts

  1. 1Watch and listenA narrated lesson of about 15 to 18 minutes, with illustrations and captions.
  2. 2Transcript and ScriptureRead the full transcript or check the Bible references whenever you need to.
  3. 3This week’s assignmentOne practical step to help you put the lesson into practice, often with a short line for each of the four parts.
  4. 4Seven-day reading planOne short passage a day, with a question to guide you. If you miss a day, simply begin again the next day.
  5. 5Talk about itThree questions for your group, a mentor or a friend.
  6. 6Go deeperOptional: notes where Christians differ, practical help, the full chapter from the book, and a worksheet.

Some lessons also include a Look back at earlier lessons and a short “Pause and seek help” note with a link to the Support Pages.

When you have watched the teaching, tap “I have completed this lesson”. You can do the assignment and reading plan during the week.

The twelve lessons

What you will learn

  • Lesson 1Grace-Fuelled GrowthTitus 2:11–14 and Philippians 2:12–13
    about 18 min    I know I am saved by grace. So why should I try hard to change? And what do I do when I keep failing?
  • Lesson 2Renewing Your Mind With TruthRomans 12:1–2 and Philippians 4:8
    about 16 min    My mind is full of noise: my phone, the news, other people's opinions and my own worries. How does God change the way I think?
  • Lesson 3Developing the Character and Fruit of ChristGalatians 5:16–25 and 2 Peter 1:3–8
    about 16 min    I have been a Christian for a while, but I still lose my temper, worry and think mainly about myself. How does God actually change my character?
  • Lesson 4Emotions, Wounds and Knowing When to Seek HelpPsalm 42 and 1 Kings 19:1–8
    about 15 min    Christians are supposed to have joy and peace. So why do I feel so low, anxious or hurt? Is something wrong with my faith?
  • Lesson 5Confession, Forgiveness, Reconciliation and BoundariesMatthew 18:15–22 and James 5:16
    about 17 min    Someone hurt me badly, and I have hurt others too. Do I have to forgive? Does forgiving mean pretending nothing happened or letting them back into my life?
  • Lesson 6Loving Others in Family, Friendship and ChurchJohn 13:34–35 and Micah 6:8
    about 15 min    Loving God feels easier than loving the people around me: my family, my difficult church members, my neighbours. What does Christian love actually look like?
  • Lesson 7Honouring God With Your Body and Relationships1 Corinthians 6:12–20; 7:7–9; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–8
    about 16 min    My culture, my friends and my own desires pull me in different directions about sex, dating and marriage. Does God care about my body? And is there hope for someone with my past?
  • Lesson 8Work, Money, Generosity and Everyday FaithfulnessColossians 3:23–24 and 1 Timothy 6:6–10, 17–19
    about 16 min    Most of my week is spent working and worrying about money. Does God care about my job and my bank account, or only about church?
  • Lesson 9Words, Anger, Conflict and PeacemakingJames 1:19–20 and Ephesians 4:25–32
    about 15 min    I say things I regret, especially when I am angry or online. And there is a conflict in my life I keep avoiding. How do Christians handle disagreements well?
  • Lesson 10Guidance, Decisions and Testing “God Told Me”Proverbs 3:5–6; Acts 15:28; 1 John 4:1
    about 16 min    I have a big decision to make, and someone at church says God told them what I should do. How do I know God's will? How do I know if a word is really from God?
  • Lesson 11Suffering, Doubt, Lament and PerseverancePsalm 13 and 2 Corinthians 4:7–18
    about 17 min    I have prayed and believed, but the suffering has not stopped. Some days I am not even sure God is listening. Is it wrong to feel this way? How do I keep going?
  • Lesson 12A Sustainable Rhythm of Life With GodMark 1:35 and Acts 2:42
    about 15 min    I have learned so much, but my life is busy and tiring. How do I keep walking with God for the long term, not just in bursts of enthusiasm?

On your own or together

Two ways to take Grow

On your own

  • Watch the teaching
  • Read the transcript and Scripture references
  • Do the week’s assignment
  • Follow the seven-day reading plan
  • Optional: Go deeper

In a church group

  • Review last week’s teaching
  • Discuss the main passage
  • Answer the three discussion questions
  • Share how the assignment went, only if you wish
  • Pray together and prepare for the next lesson

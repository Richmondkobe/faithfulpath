The Short Answer

Long-term faithfulness grows from small, steady patterns: time alone with God, life together with his people, rest that trusts him, and a rhythm that fits your season of life, kept with grace.

Part 1

Time Alone With God

“…Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.”John 15:5

Not necessarily early, long or silent · returning regularly

Part 2

Life Together

“…let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together…”Hebrews 10:24–25

“Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly…”Colossians 3:16

Part 3

Rest as Trust

“The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath.”Mark 2:27

“Come away by yourselves to a desolate place and rest a while.”Mark 6:31

“…for he gives to his beloved sleep.”Psalm 127:2

We stop because we are creatures, not God

The Short Answer

Jesus calls us to love one another as he has loved us. That love honours family wisely, makes us true friends, and moves us to do justice and show mercy to our neighbours.

Remember

We love as Jesus loved, honour our families wisely, become true friends, and do justice and love mercy.

Love as Jesus Lovedpractical, self-giving love that welcomes without favouritism and rejoices with the truth

Honour Your Familyrespect, gratitude and care; not unlimited obedience; distance for safety is not dishonour

Be a True Friendloyal, honest, kind and freely chosen, never controlling

Do Justice and Love Mercybe a neighbour to anyone in need, speak up for the vulnerable, respect people's dignity

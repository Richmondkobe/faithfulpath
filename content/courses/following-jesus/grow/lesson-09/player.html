I say things I regret, and I keep avoiding a conflict. How do Christians handle disagreements well?

Read the Scripture · James 1:19–20

“Know this, my beloved brothers: let every person be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger; for the anger of man does not produce the righteousness of God.”James 1:19–20 · ESV

Read the Scripture · Ephesians 4:29, 32

“Let no corrupting talk come out of your mouths, but only such as is good for building up, as fits the occasion, that it may give grace to those who hear.”Ephesians 4:29 · ESV

“…forgiving one another, as God in Christ forgave you.”Ephesians 4:32 · ESV

The Short Answer

Believers listen before they speak. They speak the truth in love, with words that build up. They can be angry without sinning. And they take wise, safe steps to make peace.

Part 1

Quick to Listen

“If one gives an answer before he hears, it is his folly and shame.”Proverbs 18:13

“Can you help me understand what concerns you?” · “So you are saying…?”

Listening is not the same as agreeing

Part 2

Speak Truth in Love

“How great a forest is set ablaze by such a small fire!”James 3:5

“There is one whose rash words are like sword thrusts, but the tongue of the wise brings healing.”Proverbs 12:18

No lying, gossip, slander or mockery, including online

Part 2

Hard truths, spoken with grace

“A soft answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.”Proverbs 15:1

No one is responsible for another person's threats or abuse

“Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt…”Colossians 4:6

Correcting in public or in private? See Go deeper.

Part 3

Angry Without Sinning

“Be angry and do not sin; do not let the sun go down on your anger…”Ephesians 4:26

“…he looked around at them with anger, grieved at their hardness of heart…”Mark 3:5

The question is what anger leads us to do

pause · pray · name it · address the issue, not the person

Part 4

Make Peace

“Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God.”Matthew 5:9

Where appropriate and safe · respect boundaries where there was serious harm

“If possible, so far as it depends on you, live peaceably with all.”Romans 12:18

Part 4

When believers still disagree

“…to agree in the Lord.”Philippians 4:2

“…a sharp disagreement…”Acts 15:39

“…he is very useful to me for ministry.”2 Timothy 4:11

Peace is not pretending, and never giving in to abuse

This Week

Pause before you speak, and take one small, safe step towards peace

  1. Read James 1:19–20 and Ephesians 4:25–32
  2. Write one short line for each of the four parts
  3. Pause and pray before speaking or posting when upset
  4. Privately, one ordinary, safe conflict: own your part, one small step
  5. Look back at Lessons 3, 5, 6 and 7, and follow the reading plan

“As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace: whoever speaks, as one who speaks oracles of God; whoever serves, as one who serves by the strength that God supplies— in order that in everything God may be glorified through Jesus Christ.” 1 Peter 4:10–11 · ESV

The Short Answer

Every believer receives gifts from God's grace. We are different parts of one body. Our gifts are for serving others in love. And we discover and grow our gifts through faithful, healthy service.

Remember

God gives every believer gifts by grace; we are different parts of one body; we serve in love; and we grow through faithful, healthy service.

Grace-Given Giftsgifts are grace, not rewards; God also gives practical skills

Different Parts, One Bodyevery member belongs, whatever they can do; no gift makes anyone more important

Serve in Lovegifts without love are empty; serve for others' good and God's glory

Grow Through Faithful Servicestart small, agree the details, ask and notice, and rest when needed

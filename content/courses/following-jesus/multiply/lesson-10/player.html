Multiply · Lesson 10

I've been asked to lead a small group, or I'd like to start one.

How do I lead without dominating, keep people safe, and help others lead too?

Read the Scripture · Mark 3:13–15

“And he went up on the mountain and called to him those whom he desired, and they came to him. And he appointed twelve (whom he also named apostles) so that they might be with him and he might send them out to preach and have authority to cast out demons.” Mark 3:13–15 · ESV

Read the Scripture · 2 Timothy 2:1–2

“You then, my child, be strengthened by the grace that is in Christ Jesus, and what you have heard from me in the presence of many witnesses entrust to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also.” 2 Timothy 2:1–2 · ESV

Understand the Original Meaning

Entrust · Greek

paratithēmi

to entrust, to hand something over into someone's care

“…entrust to faithful men…” (2 Timothy 2:2)

“Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!” (Luke 23:46) · “…they committed them to the Lord…” (Acts 14:23)

Not just passing on information, but entrusting what we have received.

The Short Answer

Stay close to Jesus first. Lead as a servant, not a boss. Share leadership, keep people safe and welcome accountability. And entrust the work to others, step by step, so that healthy groups can multiply.

Part 1

Called to Be With Him

Scripture · prayer · rest · church life: a sustainable rhythm

“…the apostles' teaching and the fellowship… the breaking of bread and the prayers.”Acts 2:42

The group exists to help people follow Jesus

Part 2

Lead as Servants

“…whoever would be great among you must be your servant…”Matthew 20:26

“…not domineering over those in your charge, but being examples to the flock.”1 Peter 5:3

Everyone takes part · questions, not lectures · quieter voices welcomed · focused on Christ

“I don't know; let's find out” is always allowed

Part 3

Shared, Safe and Accountable

In Scripture, leadership is usually shared

More than one leader · clear agreements · safeguarding · trustworthy oversight

Leaders receive support and correction · not monitoring members' lives

Never a closed circle loyal to one person

Part 4

Entrust to Others

Christians Differ

How quickly should groups multiply?

Some emphasiserapid multiplication of groups and new leaders
Acts 2:47 · Acts 16:5
Others emphasiseslower, deeper formation of leaders first
1 Timothy 3:6, 10 · 5:22

Acts describes growth, rather than commanding speed

2 Timothy 2:2 is the clearer passage on preparing others

Across historic Christian traditions: faithfulness, character and safety matter more than speed or numbers

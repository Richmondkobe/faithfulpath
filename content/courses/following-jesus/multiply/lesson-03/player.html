The Short Answer

Tell what the Lord has done for you. Use a simple shape if it helps. Tell the truth, without exaggerating your past or your present. And let Jesus, not you, be the hero of the story.

Part 1

What He Has Done

“One thing I do know, that though I was blind, now I see.”John 9:25

“Come and hear, all you who fear God, and I will tell what he has done for my soul.”Psalm 66:16

Part 4

Point to Jesus

“For what we proclaim is not ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, with ourselves as your servants for Jesus' sake.”2 Corinthians 4:5

Christians Differ

Is a remembered moment of conversion expected?

Some emphasisea clear moment of turning to Christ, usually remembered
Acts 9:1–19 · Acts 16:30–34
Others emphasisecoming to faith gradually, or trusting Christ from childhood
2 Timothy 1:5; 3:14–15 · Acts 16:13–15

Across historic Christian traditions: what matters most is present trust in Christ, not how dramatic one's story is or whether one remembers an exact date

This Week

Prepare a short, honest story that points to Jesus

  1. Read Mark 5:1–20 and Acts 26:1–29
  2. Write one short line for each of the four parts
  3. If you are comfortable, write your story (about three minutes), then a thirty-second version · nothing painful or private
  4. If you wish, practise it with a trusted believer, and listen to theirs

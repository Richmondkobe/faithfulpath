Someone I know has just started following Jesus and has asked me to help them.

Where do I start, and how do I help without controlling them or getting out of my depth?

The Short Answer

Care for a new believer gently and patiently. Encourage them to walk worthy of God, without taking control of their life. Learn the basics of following Jesus together. And point them to Christ and his church, not to yourself.

Part 1

Gentle Like a Mother

“…not only the gospel of God but also our own selves…”1 Thessalonians 2:8

Listen · be patient · people grow at different speeds

Stand alongside them when others hold back

Part 2

Encourage Like a Father

“…to walk in a manner worthy of God…”1 Thessalonians 2:12

“Not that we lord it over your faith, but we work with you for your joy…”2 Corinthians 1:24

Qualities of care, not fixed roles · a mentor supports another adult

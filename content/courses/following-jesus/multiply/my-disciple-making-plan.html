Your private page

My Disciple-Making Plan

Complete this privately during the final week, taking more time if you need it. It is a plan for faithful, healthy, unpressured disciple-making, not a test or a commitment to results. Simple words are enough. Work through the six parts at your own pace. If a part is not appropriate or safe for you, you may leave it and return later, or choose “This is not appropriate or safe for me” below.

You do not have to show anyone the names or circumstances of the people included. Initials or short descriptions are enough.

Three people I will pray forfrom Lesson 1

Name three people you will pray for regularly. Pray for their good, whether or not faith is ever discussed. Initials or a short description are enough.

This is not a list of targets. Do not record private information about anyone, do not report their responses, and do not keep approaching someone who has declined.

One person I may invitefrom Lessons 4 · 8

If it is appropriate and safe, name one person you may invite to read a Gospel with you, to join a group or to come to church, without pressure or conditions.

Do not record private information about this person. An invitation is freely given and freely declined. If they say no, your care for them continues without pressure.

A first conversation planfrom Lessons 3 · 4 · 8

Prepare so that you are ready: one or two sentences of your story, a simple explanation of the gospel, and one short passage you could read with someone.

In a real conversation, listen first, and use only what answers the person’s question.

A supporting leader or mentorfrom Lessons 7 · 9

Name a mature believer or leader who can give you advice, help you review your boundaries, and support you when something is beyond your role. Agree how often you will meet.

Your mentor supports you. They do not control anyone else’s discipleship, and they do not receive private information about others without permission. If leadership in your church does not feel safe, choose someone outside it.

Agreed boundariesfrom Lessons 1 · 9

Write down where and how you will meet safely, the limits of confidentiality, your church’s safeguarding contact, and what you will refer to others. Note any situations where someone depends on you, in which you will not start faith conversations.

A 90-day next stepfrom Lessons 6 · 7 · 10

Choose one step for the next ninety days: walking with a new believer for a season, helping in a group, serving in your gifting, or simply continuing to pray and care.

Continuing to pray and care is a faithful step.

What you write is saved to your account and is not shared with your group, your mentor or other learners. If you wish, you may review parts of it with a trusted mentor or leader.

Before you move on

Choose a response

When you have finished looking through your plan, choose the response that best fits today. Your choice does not change your completion of Multiply.

A word about completing Multiply

What this means

Completing Multiply means you have completed the Multiply stage of the Following Jesus pathway: you have worked through all ten lessons, in a group or on your own. Completing all four courses, Begin, Establish, Grow and Multiply, means you have completed the Following Jesus pathway. It records participation and completion. It does not mean that someone is a mature Christian, or qualified or approved to lead, teach, mentor or counsel others, or to work with children or adults at risk; churches remain responsible for discernment, preparation, safer recruitment and safeguarding. Following Jesus is lifelong, and every disciple keeps growing by grace.

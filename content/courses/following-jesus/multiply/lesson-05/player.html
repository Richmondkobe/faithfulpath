Understand the Original Meaning

Reasoned · Greek

dialegomai

to reason or discuss

“So he reasoned in the synagogue… and in the marketplace every day with those who happened to be there.” (Acts 17:17)

“…he reasoned with them from the Scriptures…” (Acts 17:2)

He did not avoid questions, or simply preach at people. He reasoned with them.

The Short Answer

Listen to the question before you answer. Answer truthfully and graciously. Be honest about the limits of what you know. And point people back to Jesus, inviting them to look at him for themselves.

Part 1

Listen to the Question

“If one gives an answer before he hears, it is his folly and shame.”Proverbs 18:13

What is the person really asking, and why?

“What makes you ask?” · “What have you heard about that?” · “Can you tell me more about what you believe?”

Part 2

Answer With Grace

“…kind to everyone… correcting his opponents with gentleness.”2 Timothy 2:24–25

“…eyewitnesses…” (Luke 1:2) · “…most of whom are still alive…” (1 Corinthians 15:6)

The goal is not to win, but to help the person see Jesus

Honest About Limits

“I don't know, but I'd like to find out.”
“Christians think about that in different ways.”

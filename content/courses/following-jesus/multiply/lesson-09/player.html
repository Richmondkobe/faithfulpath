The Short Answer

Listen well. Keep confidences, but never promise secrecy when someone may be in danger. Protect the vulnerable, and never conceal abuse. And know your limits: referring someone to the right help is love, not abandonment.

Part 1

Listen Well

“The purpose in a man's heart is like deep water, but a man of understanding will draw it out.”Proverbs 20:5

Full attention · don't interrupt · don't rush to fix · “So what I'm hearing is…”

Listening is not investigating

Part 2

Keep Confidence Wisely

“…he who is trustworthy in spirit keeps a thing covered.”Proverbs 11:13

Never promise absolute confidentiality

“I'll keep what you tell me private, unless I'm worried that you or someone else might be hurt. Then I'd need to get help, and I'll tell you what I'm doing.”

A child's concern: get appropriate safeguarding help promptly

Part 3

Protect the Vulnerable

“Open your mouth for the mute, for the rights of all who are destitute.”Proverbs 31:8

“Let the children come to me; do not hinder them…”Mark 10:14

Particular protection · without assuming anyone lacks independence

Pass on concerns and facts through the proper route · do not investigate

Forgiveness does not prove that someone is safe, or restore positions of trust

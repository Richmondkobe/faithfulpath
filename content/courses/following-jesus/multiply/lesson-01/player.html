The Short Answer

As the Father sent Jesus, Jesus sends his followers. The Spirit empowers ordinary believers to bear witness. We begin where we already live. And we love people freely, with gentleness and respect, leaving the results to God.

Part 1

Sent by Jesus

Every follower belongs to Christ's mission, in different ways

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations…”Matthew 28:19 · spoken to the eleven

“…we are ambassadors for Christ, God making his appeal through us.”2 Corinthians 5:20

“…proclaim the excellencies of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light.”1 Peter 2:9

Part 2

Empowered by the Spirit

John 20:22 · Acts 1:8: mission and the Spirit belong together

“…they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and continued to speak the word of God with boldness.”Acts 4:31

“I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth.”1 Corinthians 3:6

Part 3

Witnesses Where We Live

family · work · school · neighbourhood · online

“Many Samaritans from that town believed in him because of the woman's testimony…”John 4:39

“…let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven.”Matthew 5:16

Where witness is dangerous, a quiet, faithful life is also witness

Part 4

Love Without Pressure

“…always being prepared to make a defense to anyone who asks you for a reason for the hope that is in you; yet do it with gentleness and respect…”1 Peter 3:15–16

“The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor.”Luke 4:18

Truthful words and genuine love, never pressure

Each person is free, and remains worthy of love and respect

Christians Differ

Is every believer called to evangelise?

Some emphasiseevery believer is called to share the gospel actively
Matthew 28:19–20 · Acts 8:4 · 1 Peter 3:15
Others emphasisesome have the gift of evangelism; all are faithful witnesses, ready to explain their hope
Ephesians 4:11 · Acts 21:8

Across historic Christian traditions: every believer bears witness to Christ, some are especially gifted for evangelism, and all should support the spread of the gospel

## Amendments to make while copying

Two changes were agreed after the pages were approved and are not in the
conversation text:

**Lessons 10 and 19** — both tell readers to contact a specialist
forced-marriage service before acting. Those services are office-hours only in
every country. Add to the "Need support?" section of each:

> Specialist forced-marriage services generally operate in office hours. Outside
> those hours, contact a 24-hour domestic abuse service, or — where there is
> immediate risk, including being taken abroad — the police or emergency
> services. In the UK the Forced Marriage Unit has an out-of-hours number:
> 020 7008 1500, asking for the Global Response Centre.

**Every "Finding Help Where You Live" link** should point at the course's own
support page (`05-finding-help.md`), not at the public resources page. The
country lists are shared; the surrounding guidance is not.

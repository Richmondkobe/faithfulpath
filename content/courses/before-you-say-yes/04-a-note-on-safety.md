# A Note on Safety

Most of this course is about the ordinary work of dating well: knowing yourself,
choosing wisely, working on real difficulties, deciding with clear eyes. It
assumes two people of goodwill who can speak freely to each other. For most
learners, that assumption holds.

For some it does not.

If you are afraid of the person you are dating; if you have been threatened, hit,
sexually pressured, followed, tracked, isolated from people who care about you,
or controlled through money; if you have to manage your words and behaviour to
avoid their reaction; or if you are being pressured into a relationship or a
marriage by your family or community — then the ordinary guidance in this course
is not what you need first.

Working on the relationship, slowing down, having an honest conversation, going
to joint counselling: every one of those assumes safety. Where safety is absent,
they can make things worse.

So before you go further, know where the safety guidance is. **Lesson 6** names
the patterns that are not ordinary difficulty. **Lesson 10** addresses family and
community coercion, including forced marriage. **Lesson 19** covers ending a
relationship safely, including digital safety and what to do when the other
person will not accept the ending. **Finding Help Where You Live** gathers all of
it and lists specialist services by country. It is linked from every page of this
course and you never have to complete a lesson to reach it.

If any of this describes you, go to those pages first. If you think you may be
monitored, use a safer device where you can — one the other person has not
accessed and cannot currently access. And if you are in immediate danger, stop
reading now. Contact the emergency service where you are if it is safe to do so,
or move to the safest place available to you.

This course is not a substitute for help.

**Before you continue, please confirm:**

- I understand that the ordinary exercises in this course may not be appropriate
  where fear, abuse, coercion or surveillance is present.
- I understand that I should not confront anyone using a worksheet from this
  course if doing so could put me at risk.
- I know how to reach the support page without completing any lesson, and I
  understand that what I write here is saved to my account — so if someone may
  have access to my account, device or browser, I should use a safer device or
  not record it here at all.

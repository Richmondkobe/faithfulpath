# Before You Say Yes

**A Christian Course for Dating Wisely, Recognising Red Flags and Discerning Whether to Continue**

---

Learn to understand your relationship patterns, recognise character and warning
signs, establish healthy boundaries, and decide — with biblical wisdom, honest
evidence and wise counsel — whether to continue, slow down or walk away.

**This course will help you see your relationship more clearly. It will not
choose a partner for you, predict the future or produce a score telling you
whether your relationship passes.**

Twenty lessons, each with a practical tool, drawn from the book *Before You Say
Yes* by Pastor Richmond Kobe. Work through it at your own pace. Nothing is
locked, nothing is timed, and you can change your route at any point.

## What is in the course

**Start Here** — six short pages: what this course will and will not do, what it
means to belong to Christ, how the course works, choosing your route, a note on
safety, and where to find help.

**Module 1 — Before You Start Dating.** Why you want a relationship, what
"equally yoked" actually asks of you, and knowing yourself before you choose
someone else.

**Module 2 — Choosing Wisely.** Attraction and discernment, character and
charisma, the warning signs Christians spiritualise, and the quiet green flags
that matter.

**Module 3 — Dating With Your Eyes Open.** Boundaries, physical intimacy, family
patterns, money, and what a person's past does and does not tell you.

**Module 4 — Discernment.** Testing a claim that God has spoken, deciding whose
counsel deserves weight, whether two lives can be built into one, and the
hardest case — two good people who may not be right for each other.

**Module 5 — Continue, Slow Down or Walk Away.** Continuing with wisdom, slowing
down deliberately, ending well and safely, and the fear that no one else will
come.

**Module 6 — Before You Say Yes to Engagement.** What engagement is, what must be
true before it, and the questions to work through first.

## How this course works

**The tools are central.** Each lesson includes a reflection, checklist or
worksheet. Reading the lessons alone will give you ideas; completing the tools
honestly will give you an account of what you have actually observed.

**Your answers save to your account** and are not shared by the course with the
person you are dating. You decide what to communicate. However, someone who can
access your account, device or browser may be able to see what you write. If
that could place you at risk, use a safer device where possible or leave
sensitive fields blank.

**Nothing is scored.** No tool produces a verdict, a percentage or a
recommendation. Where a tool offers options at the end, it is showing you the
paths, not choosing one.

**"Not yet" is a legitimate conclusion**, at every point in this course.

**Support is always available.** The support page is linked from every page and
never requires you to complete a lesson to reach it.

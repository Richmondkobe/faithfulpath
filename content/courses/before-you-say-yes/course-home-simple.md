# Before You Say Yes

**A Christian Course for Dating Wisely, Recognising Red Flags and Discerning Whether to Continue**

---

Twenty short lessons you can listen to, drawn from the book *Before You Say Yes*
by Pastor Richmond Kobe. Each one is a recording of about six to eleven minutes,
with one truth to remember, one question to sit with, and one step to take.

> **Listen. Think. Take one step.**

If you would rather read than listen, every lesson has its transcript, and the
longer written version of each one is a tap away from the lesson it belongs to.

**This course will help you see your relationship more clearly. It will not
choose a partner for you, predict the future or produce a score telling you
whether your relationship passes.**

## What is in the course

**Start Here** — four short pages: welcome, how to use the course, choosing
where to begin, and safety and support.

**Module 1 — Start With Yourself.** Why you want a relationship, whether your
faith and theirs point the same way, and knowing yourself before you choose
someone else.

**Module 2 — Learn What to Look For.** Attraction and wisdom, character rather
than charm, the red flags Christians sometimes excuse, and the green flags that
really matter.

**Module 3 — Date With Your Eyes Open.** Boundaries without guilt, talking
honestly about physical boundaries, family patterns, money, and understanding
their past wisely.

**Module 4 — Make a Wise Decision.** What to do when someone says "God told me",
whose advice to trust, whether you can build a life together, and the hardest
case — two good Christians who may be wrong for each other.

**Module 5 — Choose Your Next Step.** When it is wise to continue, when to slow
down, when it is time to walk away, and the fear that no one else will come.

**Optional Engagement Section.** Whether you are ready to discuss engagement,
the questions to answer before it, and what comes next.

After each of the first four modules there is a check-in: a short recording and
a few questions to notice what you have seen so far. Module 5 ends with My Next
Faithful Step.

## How this course works

**Listen. Think. Take one step.** Each teaching lesson begins with a short
recording. Then one main question — you can think about it privately or write
your answer, and writing is always optional. Then one small, practical step. Not
a list of homework; one thing you can actually do.

**Go deeper, if you want to.** Many lessons have an optional workbook that
breaks bigger questions into short screens. Use it now, later, or not at all. It
is there to help you, not to test you.

**Nothing is scored.** No lesson will tell you whether your relationship passes
or fails. When a page offers choices, it is showing you possible paths. It is
not choosing for you.

**"Not yet" is always an acceptable answer**, at every point in this course.

**Your answers belong to you.** The course will not share them with another
person, and nobody is entitled to demand that you show them. Some ordinary
answers may be saved to your account so you can return later. Safety-related
answers and conversation guides are never saved.

However, someone who can access your phone, computer or account may be able to
see your saved answers. If that could put you at risk, do not write them here.
Use a safer device if you can, or think about the questions without writing.

**Support is always available.** The support page is linked from every page and
never requires you to complete a lesson to reach it. **Leave this page →** is in
the corner of every page if you need to go somewhere else quickly.

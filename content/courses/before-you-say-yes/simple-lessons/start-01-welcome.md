## What this course will help you do

Learn how to date wisely, notice healthy and unhealthy patterns, set clear boundaries, and decide whether your next step is to continue, slow down, seek help, or walk away.

## One promise

**This course will help you see your relationship more clearly. It will not choose a partner for you, predict your future, or give you a score telling you whether your relationship passes.**

## Audio script and transcript

*(About four minutes. Read slowly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed "Read the full transcript" section.)*

Welcome to Before You Say Yes. I'm Pastor Richmond Kobe, and I'm glad you are here.

Before we begin, let me tell you plainly what this course is, and what it is not.

You are probably here because there is a decision somewhere in front of you. Perhaps you are wondering whether you are ready to date at all. Perhaps you are in a relationship, and something has been bothering you that you cannot quite name. Perhaps other people have strong opinions about your relationship, and you are no longer sure which voice is your own. Or perhaps engagement is being discussed, and you feel pressure to be certain when you are not.

Here is my promise to you. This course will help you see your relationship more clearly. It will not choose a partner for you. It will not predict your future. And it will not give you a score telling you whether your relationship passes or fails.

What it will do is help you look honestly at what you have actually seen, not just what you have hoped. It will help you notice the pressures that shape your choices. And it will help you consider your feelings and the evidence in the light of Scripture and wise advice, so you can take your next step with your eyes open.

I have served as a pastor for more than twenty years, in several countries. I have sat with many people before, during, and after their relationships. When I share from my own experience, I will tell you. When the Bible speaks directly, I will quote it. And when I apply a passage beyond its original setting, I will say so, because you should always be able to tell the difference between what God has said and what I think follows from it.

Two more things. This is not a premarital course. It is a dating course. And it is not a replacement for qualified help. If there is ever fear, control, pressure, or danger in your relationship, the course will tell you where to go first.

Each lesson is short. Most audio lessons are about five to twelve minutes. You will think about one main question and take one practical step. Many lessons also include an optional workbook if you want to go deeper.

Please take it slowly. Nothing is timed or graded, and there is no prize for finishing quickly. Some lessons are heavier than they first appear. You may stop and return whenever you need to.

Let's begin.

## Before you start

**This course is for:**

- Christians who are single and want to prepare well
- people who have just started dating
- people who are unsure about a relationship
- people in relationships where engagement is being discussed

**This course is not:**

- a premarital course — if you become engaged, *Talk Before You Marry* is the natural next step
- a replacement for qualified help — where there is fear, control, pressure, or danger, the course will point you to specialist support first

## Not sure you belong to Christ?

This course assumes you are a Christian. But if you are not sure what that means, or whether it is true of you, you are welcome here.

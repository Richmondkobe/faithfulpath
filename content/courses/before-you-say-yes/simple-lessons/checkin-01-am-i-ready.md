## Audio script and transcript

*(About three minutes. Read slowly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed “Read the transcript” section.)*

You have reached the first check-in in this course.

In Lesson 1, you asked why you want a relationship. In Lesson 2, you considered what it means to share the same faith and direction. In Lesson 3, you thought honestly about who you are and what you are looking for.

This page is not a test. It will not tell you whether you are allowed to date. It is simply a place to stop, look back, and notice what has become clearer.

Here is one question to consider:

What do you understand now that you did not understand before?

*[Pause for five seconds.]*

Next, you will choose the statement that best describes where you are right now. None of the choices is better than the others, and you can change your answer at any time.

You may feel ready to begin or continue dating thoughtfully. You may want to strengthen one area before moving forward. You may need to speak with someone wise before making a decision. Or you may simply need more time.

“Not yet” is always an acceptable answer in this course.

In the next module, you will learn how to look carefully at another person’s character, habits, and patterns. You will consider whether their actions match their words. In a healthy relationship, paying attention is normal and wise.

But if you are afraid of the person you are dating, please hear this clearly: fear is not a sign that you need to try harder or complete more lessons. Go first to **Find Help Where You Live** and speak with someone who understands relationship abuse and safety.

Let us pray.

Father, thank you for what you have shown me so far. Help me to be honest about where I am. Give me patience when I need to wait, courage when I need to move forward, and humility when I need help. Lead me one faithful step at a time. In Jesus’ name, amen.

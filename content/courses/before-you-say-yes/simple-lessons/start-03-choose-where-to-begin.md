## Audio script and transcript

*(About two minutes. Read slowly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed "Read the transcript" section.)*

People come to this course from different places, so you do not have to begin with Lesson 1. Let me make this simple. There are four starting points.

If you are single and want to prepare well, start with Lesson 1. The first lessons are about you, and they are especially helpful before there is a particular person involved.

If you have just started dating someone, start with Lesson 4. It will help you see the difference between attraction and wisdom. You can return to Lessons 1 to 3 whenever you like.

If you are unsure whether to stay in your relationship, begin with the Safety and Support page and then go to Lesson 6. Lesson 6 will help you tell the difference between ordinary problems and serious warning signs.

If engagement is being discussed, begin with the Safety and Support page and then go to Lesson 13. That is where the decision lessons begin.

You can change your starting point at any time. No lesson is locked, and you do not need to rush.

One more thing: a starting point is not a shortcut around something important. If a later lesson refers to an earlier lesson that you have not completed, return to it before making a major decision — unless the safety guidance says that lesson is not right for your situation.

If you are unsure which route fits, start with Lesson 1 and continue in order.

# Before You Say Yes — Simple Course Build Notes (Addendum)

Agreed 19 September 2026. This addendum explains how to build the **simple, audio-first version** of the course from the files in `simple-lessons/`.

**How to use this file:** the original `before-you-say-yes-course-build-notes.md` (17 September 2026) still applies. **Where this addendum and the original disagree, this addendum wins.** Every page file also has its own implementation note at the bottom; for that page's screens, **the page's own note is the final authority.**

---

## 1. The two layers

The course now has two layers.

- **Layer 1 — the simple lesson.** The main learning experience: audio, one truth, one Scripture, one question, one step, and a prayer (inside the audio). Written for a high-school reading level.
- **Layer 2 — "Go deeper."** The optional workbook, shown only when the learner taps **Open the workbook**, plus **Read the book chapter**, which opens the original detailed lesson file (for example `lesson-01-why-do-you-want-a-relationship.md`).

The original detailed lesson files are **not deleted.** They become the "Read the book chapter" pages. Keep them in the repository.

## 3. Lesson page template (replaces original §9)

Every teaching lesson uses this order:

1. **Before you begin** notice — only on Lessons 6, 9, 10, 13 and 19 (with support and exit links); Lesson 20 opens with the crisis notice instead
2. Today's question
3. Listen — audio player (see §4)
4. One truth to remember
5. Key Scripture, with two or three plain sentences
6. Optional page panel (some lessons only — for example the tables in Lessons 6, 8, 15 and 17)
7. Audio script and transcript — **inside a closed "Read the transcript" section**
8. Pause and think — one question, optional **Write my answer** button (no answer box on Lessons 6 and 19)
9. One step for today
10. What would you like to do next? — Continue · Stop here for today · Open the workbook · Read the book chapter
11. Need support?
12. Go deeper — the workbook, hidden until the learner taps **Open the workbook**

**Prayers:** every lesson now ends its audio with a short spoken prayer. This **replaces** the original rule "written prayers only in Lessons 6, 13, 16, 19 and 20; no audio prayers."

---

## 4. Audio and transcripts

- Each page has a large **Listen** button showing the approximate length (for example "Listen — about 7 minutes").
- Player controls: play, pause, back 15 seconds, speed.
- The transcript is **closed by default** and opens only when the learner chooses.
- Stage directions in scripts such as *[Pause for five seconds.]* are for recording only. Do not display them as spoken text in the transcript.
- Recording lengths range from about 2 minutes (Start Here 3) to about 12 minutes (Lesson 19).

---

## 5. Workbooks ("Go deeper")

- Hidden until the learner taps **Open the workbook.**
- Each workbook is split into **screens**. Show one screen at a time. **No screen has more than five prompts.**
- Support saving and returning over several days, with **no prompt to finish** and **no reminders on a schedule.**
- Where a screen says "Repeat this screen" (for example Lesson 8 Screen 2, Lesson 14 Screens 1 and 3), the learner must be able to add more entries.
- Nothing in any workbook is scored, counted, totalled, or summarised.

## 6. Saving rules — the most important changes

### 6.1 Three kinds of screen

| Kind | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Ordinary private screen** | May save to the learner's own account. It is not shared with another person by the course and is never used to score or label the learner. It may still be visible to anyone who can access the learner's device or account. |
| **Non-saved screen**        | Answers exist only on the page for that session. **Never stored, reflected, summarised, counted, exported, or included in analytics, notifications, downloads, or "View my earlier answers."** |
| **Read-only screen**        | Has **no input fields at all.** Text to read or think about only.                                                                                                                              |

### 6.2 Conversation guides replace joint worksheets (replaces original §5)

The original rule — "the shared section is entered by the account holder after both agree the wording" — **no longer applies.** Every part meant for two people is now a **non-saved conversation guide**:

- No shared response fields.
- The learner answers their own private screens first and chooses what to say aloud.
- The interface must never suggest that the other person has access to the account.
- Every conversation guide carries the gate: only use it if both people can speak freely and safely.

Conversation guides: Lesson 7 (Screens 6–8) · Lesson 8 (Screen 4) · Lesson 9 (Screens 3–5, 7) · Lesson 10 (Screens 6–8) · Lesson 15 (Screen 5) · Lesson 17 (Screen 10) · Lesson 18 (Screens 3, 4, 15) · Engagement 2 (Screen 16).

### 6.4 Rules that still apply everywhere (from original §4)

- Never store that a learner chose a safety path, a planned safety action, or a date or time for one.

- Account numbers, passwords, card details, and document numbers are never requested or storable anywhere.
- The monitoring-privacy note appears where each page places it, and before any download, print, email, or share.

## 7. Other interface rules

- **"View my earlier answers."** Check-ins and later lessons may refer back to earlier answers. **Never display earlier answers automatically.** Use a learner-initiated control, shown after the monitoring-privacy note. Never show answers from a non-saved screen.
- **Choices on check-ins and closing pages.** Ordinary choices may be reflected above the Continue button ("Your current choice: …") and changed at any time. **Safety routes are shown separately from ordinary choices, never saved, and display the support route locally for that session only.**
- **Start Here 3 route choice.** Show four navigation cards (radio-style, not checkboxes). Store only an **opaque internal identifier.** Never expose a descriptive label (such as "unsure whether to stay") anywhere. No lesson is locked by a route.
- **Start Here 4.** One button: **I understand — continue.** No tick boxes. **Find help where you live** and **Leave this page** stay visible **above** the button.
- **Leave this page and Find help where you live** remain persistent on every page, as in original §3.
- **Lesson 18.** The review workbook is now called **Slow Down and Review** (not "90-Day Relationship Review"). Never send a review-date reminder framed as a deadline.
- **Testing vocabulary.** Do not use "test period," "probation," "prove," or "perform" in interface copy or reminders for Lessons 17 and 18.
- **Lesson 20.** Crisis guidance must stay at the top of the lesson page, in Need support, and at the top of the workbook. Never hide it behind an expander or a completion step.
- **Completion record.** Label may never use "ready," "prepared," or "certified." "Completed Before You Say Yes" is acceptable.
- **Talk Before You Marry.** Mentioned on Start Here 1 and Engagement 3 as plain text. Make it a link only when that course exists.

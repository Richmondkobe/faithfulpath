name abuse, fear, leaving, coercion, forced marriage or safety concerns.

## Lesson page order (addendum §3)

Before you begin (Lessons 6, 9, 10, 13, 19 only; Lesson 20 opens with the crisis
notice) · Today's question · Listen · One truth to remember · Key Scripture ·
optional panel · transcript, closed by default · Pause and think (no answer box
on Lessons 6 and 19) · One step for today · What would you like to do next? ·
Need support? · Go deeper.

Every lesson's audio now ends with a spoken prayer, replacing the original
written-prayers rule.

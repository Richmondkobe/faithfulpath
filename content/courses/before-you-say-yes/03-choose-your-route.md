# Choose Your Route

Learners arrive here at very different points. Choose the description that fits
you now. You can change route at any time, and the course will remember where
you are.

### I am not dating yet

Start Here → Lessons 1–3 → **Am I Ready to Date?** → Lessons 4–7 → **What Have I
Actually Observed?**

Then pause. Return to Module 3 when dating becomes relevant. The first seven
lessons are about you and about what you are looking for, and they are most
useful before there is a particular person attached to the questions.

### I have recently started dating

Start Here → Lessons 1–3 → **Am I Ready to Date?** → Lessons 4–7 → **What Have I
Actually Observed?** → Lessons 8–12 → **Patterns, Not Isolated Impressions** →
Lessons 13–16 → **What Does the Evidence Require?**

Take the later lessons as the relationship develops rather than all at once. Do
not race ahead: much of what they ask you to weigh will not exist yet.

### I am deciding whether to continue

A Note on Safety → Lesson 6 → Lessons 12–16 → **What Does the Evidence
Require?** → Lessons 17–20 → **My Next Faithful Step**

Lesson 6 comes first because it distinguishes ordinary difficulty from patterns
that are not difficulty at all. Seek counsel as you go.

### We are considering engagement

A Note on Safety → Lessons 13–16 → **What Does the Evidence Require?** → Lessons
17–20 → **My Next Faithful Step** → Module 6

Module 6 is the engagement threshold itself, and it assumes the discernment work
in Lessons 13–16.

---

> **A route is a starting point, not a shortcut around something important.** If
> a later lesson refers to a tool you have not completed, return to it before
> making a major decision — unless its safety guidance says that completing the
> tool would not be appropriate for your situation.

If none of these fits, or you are not sure, take the complete course in order.
And if what brought you here is a specific worry rather than a stage — a red flag
you noticed, pressure from family, a relationship moving very fast — use **Help
Me With My Relationship Right Now** on the course home instead.

# How This Course Works

Eight things to know before you start.

**The tools are central to the course.** Each lesson includes a reflection,
checklist or worksheet. Read a lesson, then do its tool, then move on. If you
read all twenty lessons in a weekend and complete none of the tools, you will
have some ideas. If you complete the tools honestly, you should have a clearer
account of the evidence, the unanswered questions and any decision now in front
of you.

**Complete most tools alone first.** Several can afterwards be compared with the
person you are dating, and each tool says which. Do not skip the alone step.
Writing alone first makes it easier to notice your own response before it is
shaped by someone else's expectations.

**A worksheet records your present perception. It is not an infallible verdict.**
Examine what you write alongside observed facts, Scripture and wise counsel.

**Your answers are private, and they should stay that way unless you decide
otherwise.** Completing a tool alone does not mean every answer must later be
shown to the person you are dating. Follow the instructions attached to each
tool, and never share a worksheet with someone who may use it to pressure, punish
or monitor you. Nobody is entitled to your answers because they demand them.

If someone may have access to your account, device or browser activity, do not
record anything here that could place you at risk. Go first to *A Note on Safety*
and use a safer device if one is available.

**No tool produces a score.** None of them will tell you whether your
relationship passes. Where a tool offers options at the end, it is showing you
the paths, not choosing one for you.

**"Not yet" is a legitimate conclusion.** These tools cannot guarantee an
immediate decision, and they are not designed to. Sometimes the honest answer is
that you do not yet know enough, and knowing that is real progress.

**Use this course with counsel.** It will ask you repeatedly to bring what you
are seeing to wise, independent people — people who can speak freely to you
without depending on your relationship, your family or your church leadership
structure. Lesson 14 is about building that circle. This course is not a
substitute for it and works far better alongside it.

**Do not rush several demanding lessons in one sitting.** Some lessons will tell
you explicitly when it may be wise to stop afterwards. One lesson in an evening
is a good ordinary pace, but you may move more slowly whenever you need to.

Your answers save as you go, so you can leave a lesson and return to it. If you
are unsure where to begin, the next page will help you choose a route.

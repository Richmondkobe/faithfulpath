## Progress display

**35 course-content pages**, excluding the course home. The home page is not
page 1 of 36.

Progress shows position, never completion pressure. No percentage bar tied to a
target, no streak, no reminder to finish. A learner on the *I am not dating yet* route who stops after **What Have I
Actually Observed?** has completed that route, not abandoned the course.

Where a learner has chosen a route, progress is shown against that route rather
than against all 35 pages. Route progress may read `Page X of Y on your route`.
Do not describe finishing a shorter route as completing all 35 course pages. If
the learner changes route, recalculate the route position without deleting work
already completed.

Route lengths, excluding the course home and the optional routing page:

| Route | Pages |
|---|---|
| I am not dating yet | 15 |
| I have recently started dating | 26 |
| I am deciding whether to continue | 13 |
| We are considering engagement | 14 |
| The complete course | 35 |

The first two totals assume *Start Here* means all six Module 0 pages.

## The four routes

Each route names the pause pages it reaches. Routes are starting points, never
restrictions: no page is locked, and a learner may leave their route at any time.

### I am not dating yet

Start Here → Lessons 1–3 → **Am I Ready to Date?** → Lessons 4–7 → **What Have I
Actually Observed?**

Then pause. The first seven lessons are about you and about what you are looking
for, and they are most useful before there is a particular person attached to
the questions. Return to Module 3 when dating becomes relevant.

### I have recently started dating

Start Here → Lessons 1–3 → **Am I Ready to Date?** → Lessons 4–7 → **What Have I
Actually Observed?** → Lessons 8–12 → **Patterns, Not Isolated Impressions** →
Lessons 13–16 → **What Does the Evidence Require?**

Take the later lessons as the relationship develops rather than all at once.
Much of what they ask you to weigh will not exist yet.

### I am deciding whether to continue

A Note on Safety → Lesson 6 → Lessons 12–16 → **What Does the Evidence Require?**
→ Lessons 17–20 → **My Next Faithful Step**

Lesson 6 comes first because it distinguishes ordinary difficulty from patterns
that are not difficulty at all. Seek counsel as you go.

### We are considering engagement

A Note on Safety → Lessons 13–16 → **What Does the Evidence Require?** → Lessons
17–20 → **My Next Faithful Step** → Module 6

Module 6 is the engagement threshold itself, and it assumes the discernment work
in Lessons 13–16.

## Standing notice beneath the routes

> **A route is a starting point, not a shortcut around something important.** If
> a later lesson refers to a tool you have not completed, return to it before
> making a major decision — unless its safety guidance says that completing the
> tool would not be appropriate for your situation.

## Persistent elements

On every page of the course:

- **Finding Help Where You Live** in the navigation
- **Leave this page** — persistent while scrolling, opens the neutral
  destination immediately, never claims to erase history
- The support line: *Are you afraid, under pressure or unsure whether you are
  safe? Get support.*

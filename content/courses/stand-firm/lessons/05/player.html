Lesson 5 · Chapters 7–8 · Part 2: The Armour of God

The Breastplate of Righteousness and the Shoes of Peace

What steadies my heart when I feel accused or unsettled?

Imagine…

Trying to earn God’s acceptance · feeling crushed when we fall short

Ephesians 6:14–15

“Stand therefore, having fastened on the belt of truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness,Ephesians 6:14 (ESV)

and, as shoes for your feet, having put on the readiness given by the gospel of peace.”Ephesians 6:15 (ESV)

Covering the centre, keeping your footing

The breastplate Is he really for me?Do I really belong to him?Am I accepted, or only tolerated?
The shoes Studded for grip on rough groundYou cannot stand firm if your feet keep slipping.

We should not press every detail of the picture.

God wears it first

“He put on righteousness as a breastplate…”Isaiah 59:17

The righteousness that protects us begins with God himself, not with us.

“Readiness”

readiness

preparation · being prepared

Firm footing
Ready to go and share

The source: “the gospel of peace”

Point 1

The breastplate: righteousness received, then lived

The righteousness God gives
The righteous life we live

Both are biblical, and they belong together.

“For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin,2 Corinthians 5:21

so that in him we might become the righteousness of God.”2 Corinthians 5:21

Our standing rests on Christ, not on our performance.

Righteousness lived

“…put on the new self, created after the likeness of God in true righteousness and holiness.”Ephesians 4:24

We do not live rightly in order to be accepted. We live rightly because we have been accepted.

“Are we to continue in sin that grace may abound? By no means!”Romans 6:1–2

Grace does not make holiness optional. It makes holiness possible.

A welcome given, not earned

“Bring quickly the best robe, and put it on him…”Luke 15:22

Acceptance given, not earned

Answering the accuser

How can someone like you call herself a Christian?

“Who shall bring any charge against God’s elect? It is God who justifies.Romans 8:33

Who is to condemn? Christ Jesus is the one who died—more than that, who was raised—who is at the right hand of God, who indeed is interceding for us.”Romans 8:34

He points away from us to God and to Christ.

Look to your Advocate.

Imagine… Priya learns to rest

She should apologise — from security, not from fear.

“Do you serve because you are loved, or so that you will be loved?”

Her service becomes a response to a verdict already given.

Point 2

The gospel of peace gives us firm ground

“Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.”Romans 5:1

Peace that holds in trouble

“In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart; I have overcome the world.”John 16:33

Feeling anxious or unsettled does not mean that you have lost your peace with God.

Not the absence of trouble, but security in Christ while trouble is present

Point 3

Ready to carry the gospel of peace

“For he himself is our peace…”Ephesians 2:14

“How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him who brings good news, who publishes peace…”Isaiah 52:7

With gentleness and respect

1 Peter 3:13–17 · written to believers under pressure

“…always being prepared to make a defense to anyone who asks you for a reason for the hope that is in you; yet do it with gentleness and respect…”1 Peter 3:15

Prepared
A conversation, not an attack
Gentleness and respect

Now she can speak of grace.

Bringing it together

We stand on what Christ has given — his righteousness and peace with God — and we carry that peace to others.

  • 3 · Ready to carry the gospel of peace

Because Christ has made me right with God, I have peace with him, and…

Share it with a trusted believer.

Reflect: Do I serve because I am loved, or so that I will be loved?

Remember

“Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.”Romans 5:1

I am accepted because of Christ. I stand on the peace he has made, and I will be ready to share it.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Father, thank you that my standing with you does not rest on my performance, but on Jesus,

who died for me, rose again and now intercedes for me.

When accusation comes, help me to look to my Advocate.

Where I have sinned, give me honesty to confess it and grace to make it right.

Thank you that through Christ I have peace with you.

Give me firm footing when life is unsettled,

and prepare me to share the good news with gentleness and respect.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.

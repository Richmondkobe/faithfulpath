Together and Free

What freedom do I have in Christ, and who will walk in it with me?

The freedom every believer has in Christ

Not earned by our performance · received by grace, through faith in Christ

The man among the tombs · Mark 5

“…sitting there, clothed and in his right mind…”Mark 5:15 (ESV)

“Go home to your friends and tell them how much the Lord has done for you…”Mark 5:19 (ESV)

Christ has complete authority over every evil power.

An extraordinary account, not the pattern for every struggle

What is clear

Christians differ about some aspects of spiritual oppression and prayer for freedom. Our focus here is our belonging to Christ and walking faithfully with trustworthy believers.

  • Believers belong to Christ
  • We resist the devil; we do not fear him
  • Nightmares or dread, by themselves, do not show spiritual oppression

We do not walk alone

Free to serve one another in love · Galatians 5:13

“When they were released, they went to their friends…”Acts 4:23 (ESV)

“…they lifted their voices together to God…”Acts 4:24 (ESV)

Why we need one another

“Now the full number of those who believed were of one heart and soul…”Acts 4:32 (ESV)

“Therefore, confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed.”James 5:16 (ESV)

Not telling everyone: choosing someone trustworthy

A real church, not a perfect one

  • Centred on Christ
  • Teaches Scripture faithfully
  • Welcomes accountability
  • Cares for its members

Online sermons support fellowship; they do not replace it.

Point 3

Walking in freedom

“…many of those who were now believers came, confessing and divulging their practices…”Acts 19:18 (ESV)

Not a required procedure for everyone, but a helpful pattern

A helpful pattern

Confess: bring past involvement into the light, before God and a trusted leader.

Renounce: turn away, and declare your trust in Christ alone.

Let go: a free choice about things you own and used for occult practice or protection. Ordinary cultural and family belongings are not in view.

“…walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh.”Galatians 5:16 (ESV)

Letting go is not a ritual that buys protection, but a choice to stop relying on those things.

When someone asks for focused prayer

  • With consent: free to stop at any time
  • With dignity: no shouting, restraint or filming
  • Two mature leaders, under the church’s care
  • Followed by discipleship

Prayer and medical care go together.

Imagine… · Lucia walks in freedom

She already belongs to Christ. Letting go of the charms is one way she chooses to stop relying on them.

On difficult nights, she remembers that she belongs to Christ.

Two misunderstandings

Not every persistent struggle needs deliverance ministry.

Strong Christians are not those who need others least.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Lord Jesus, thank you that you have delivered me from the domain of darkness and brought me into your kingdom. Thank you that there is no condemnation for those who are in you.

Forgive me for the times I have tried to stand alone. I turn from every practice and allegiance that opposes you, and I give myself fully to you.

Lead me to trustworthy believers, and help me to be faithful among them. Help me to share my burdens, and to carry the burdens of others.

Fill us with your Holy Spirit, so that together we may stand firm. Give me courage to tell others how much you have done for me. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

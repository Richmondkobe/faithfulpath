What spiritual warfare is

A demon behind every problem
The devil as an old-fashioned idea

Neither view comes from the Bible.

Spiritual warfare is the Christian’s struggle to remain faithful to Christ amid the pressures of the world, the desires of the flesh and the opposition of the devil.

Point 1

People are not the enemy

“We do not wrestle against flesh and blood.”Ephesians 6:12

Slower to hate, quicker to pray

Wrongs can still be named, and sometimes confronted or reported.

Seeking safety and help can be a faithful response.

Slower to hate · quicker to pray · careful to fight the right battle

Real, but not everywhere

“Your adversary the devil prowls around like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour.”1 Peter 5:8

a mechanic
an honest conversation
a humble apology

They still call for prayer.

Four questions for reflection

Prompts, not a test

1 · My own heart
Is there sin in me that I need to confess or turn from?

2 · The world around me
Am I being shaped by pressures, values or voices that pull me away from God?

3 · Truth and lies
Is there a thought, accusation, fear or temptation pressing on me that contradicts what God says?

4 · Ordinary needs
Is there a practical, physical, relational or medical need to care for as well?

More than one can be true at the same time.

Imagine… the four questions with Simon

  • My own heart → an apology after an argument
  • The world around me → less attention to voices that feed fear
  • Truth and lies → remembering that he belongs to Christ
  • Ordinary needs → arranging the car repair

He does not need one explanation for everything.

Together, the questions suggest several possible faithful steps.

Bringing it together

Spiritual warfare is real, but not behind every problem.

  • 1 · People are not the enemy
  • 2 · The world, the flesh, the devil — and ordinary needs
  • 3 · We stand together

Christ calls us to stand, not to fear.

This week’s practice

1. Choose one difficulty in your life right now.

2. Work slowly through the four questions in prayer.

You do not need to settle whether this is a spiritual attack before taking a faithful step.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Lord Jesus, thank you that you came to destroy the works of the devil, and that you have triumphed over every power of darkness.

Thank you that I do not have to live in fear.

Give me wisdom to see my life clearly:

to confess my own sin, to resist the pull of the world,

to recognise the lies of the enemy, and to care for the ordinary needs in front of me.

Where I have treated people as my enemies, forgive me and teach me to love.

When the battle is long, help me to keep standing.

Make me strong in you and in the strength of your might. Amen.

Lesson 6 · Chapters 9–10 · Part 2: The Armour of God

The Shield of Faith and the Helmet of Salvation

How do I keep trusting God when the outcome is still uncertain?

Imagine…

The thoughts feel like burning arrows.

Ephesians 6:16–17a

“In all circumstances take up the shield of faith, with which you can extinguish all the flaming darts of the evil one;Ephesians 6:16 (ESV)

and take the helmet of salvation…”Ephesians 6:17 (ESV)

Point 1

The shield: faith in God, not faith in faith

thyreosGreek · Ephesians 6:16

a large shield, shaped like a door

“…extinguish all the flaming darts of the evil one…”Ephesians 6:16

“In all circumstances…”

Faith in God, not faith in faith

“Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen.”Hebrews 11:1

“I believe; help my unbelief!”Mark 9:24

The object of our trust matters more than its size.

Imagine… · Answering the darts

Maybe God doesn’t hear me.

“The eyes of the LORD are toward the righteous and his ears toward their cry.”Psalm 34:15

Her feelings may not change straight away. But she has something to hold up.

Faith that remembers

“I will remember the deeds of the LORD; yes, I will remember your wonders of old.”Psalm 77:11

He did not spare his own Son (Romans 8:32).

Point 2 · Hebrews 11

Faith when the outcome is uncertain

“…who through faith conquered kingdoms, enforced justice, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions, quenched the power of fire, escaped the edge of the sword…”Hebrews 11:33–34

“Others suffered mocking and flogging, and even chains and imprisonment.”Hebrews 11:36

“And all these, though commended through their faith, did not receive what was promised, since God had provided something better for us, that apart from us they should not be made perfect.”Hebrews 11:39–40

Some received particular deliverances, but none had yet received the complete fulfilment towards which God’s promises pointed. Their shared hope reached beyond their immediate circumstances.

Continuing hardship is not failed faith

Their faith was not measured by how quickly their circumstances changed.

Continuing hardship does not mean failed faith.

Imagine… · Esi’s faith and Esi’s timetable

God’s promises are sure. Our own expectations need to be held more humbly.

Locking shields

She no longer feels that she is holding up her shield alone.

Two mistakes about faith

Mistake 1: enough faith always secures the outcome we want.

Mistake 2: any doubt means faith has failed.

Faith is not the absence of struggle. It is holding on to God in the middle of it.

Point 3

The helmet: salvation past, present and future, and the hope it gives

“…for a helmet the hope of salvation.”1 Thessalonians 5:8

“…who died for us so that whether we are awake or asleep we might live with him.”1 Thessalonians 5:10

Salvation in three tenses

  • We have been saved. “For by grace you have been saved through faith.” Ephesians 2:8
  • We are being saved. “…to us who are being saved it is the power of God.” 1 Corinthians 1:18
  • We will be saved. “…salvation is nearer to us now than when we first believed.” Romans 13:11

The helmet of assurance

“…no one will snatch them out of my hand.”John 10:28

“…that you may know that you have eternal life.”1 John 5:13

Our assurance rests on Christ, not on the strength of our feelings.

The helmet of hope

“…born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead…”1 Peter 1:3

We may grieve, but not as those who have no hope (1 Thessalonians 4:13).

Christian hope is not positive thinking. It rests on the risen Lord.

Bringing it together

Faith in God and the hope of salvation hold us even when the outcome is uncertain.

This week’s practice

1. Write down one doubt that keeps returning.

2. Beside it, write a promise from Scripture that speaks to it.

3. Then write a short prayer asking honestly for what you need, while trusting God without demanding a particular result or timetable. ✦

For example: Father, please provide work for our family. Help us take wise steps and trust your care while we wait.

Remember

“Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen.”Hebrews 11:1

My faith may feel small, but my God is great and faithful. My assurance rests on Christ, not on my feelings. My hope is anchored in the risen Lord. I will hold up the shield of faith, and I will not stand alone.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Father, thank you that my faith does not rest on my own strength, but on you. You are faithful, even when I struggle to trust.

When doubts and fears come like burning arrows, help me to hold up the shield of faith and to set your promises against them.

Where my faith is weak, help my unbelief. Teach me to trust you when I do not know how things will turn out.

Thank you that by grace I have been saved, that you are still at work in me, and that one day my salvation will be complete.

Lift my eyes to the living hope of the resurrection, and surround me with your people. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

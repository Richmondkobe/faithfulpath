Lesson 3 · Chapter 4 · Part 1: Understanding the Battle

How the Enemy Works

How do I recognise the enemy’s schemes when they are happening?

Imagine…

Something is slowly pulling Mei away from peace.

2 Corinthians 2:10–11

“Anyone whom you forgive, I also forgive. Indeed, what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, has been for your sake in the presence of Christ,2 Corinthians 2:10 (ESV)

so that we would not be outwitted by Satan; for we are not ignorant of his designs.”2 Corinthians 2:11 (ESV)

Point 1

A verse about forgiveness

“…turn to forgive and comfort him, or he may be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.”2 Corinthians 2:7

“…reaffirm your love for him.”2 Corinthians 2:8

Two dangers

A repentant man crushed by despair
A church that becomes hard and divided

Both would serve the enemy’s purposes.

Unforgiveness · discouragement · quiet division

Point 2

Five patterns at a glance

Lies Accusation Temptation Fear Division

Not a complete list: a useful starting point

Lies

“a liar and the father of lies.”John 8:44

“Did God actually say, …?”Genesis 3:1

“…even Satan disguises himself as an angel of light.”2 Corinthians 11:14

The answer to lies is truth: God’s Word, understood in context and held together with other believers.

Accusation

“…and Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him.”Zechariah 3:1

The Lord rebukes the accuser, and the filthy garments are replaced with clean ones.

“the accuser of our brothers”Revelation 12:10

Temptation

We are tempted by our own desires. The enemy can exploit them, but he cannot make us choose sin.James 1:14

“…with the temptation he will also provide the way of escape…”1 Corinthians 10:13

There is always a faithful path.

Fear

Not every fear is a spiritual attack.

“…God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control.”2 Timothy 1:7

“Resist him, firm in your faith…”1 Peter 5:9

Division

“…do not let the sun go down on your anger, and give no opportunity to the devil.”Ephesians 4:26–27

A careless word · a hurt left unspoken · a story we tell ourselves

Point 3

Conviction is not accusation

The difference cannot be judged simply by how painful or specific a thought feels.

The better question

Does the thought truthfully identify wrongdoing and point towards repentance and God’s mercy?

Or does it turn our failures into a verdict that we are beyond his grace?

Godly grief draws us to the cross. Condemnation pushes us away from it.

Not God’s verdict over her

“There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.”Romans 8:1

A real wrong may still need confession and, where possible, repair — as forgiven people.

Imagine… Mei’s way back

Honesty · prayer · humility · forgiveness

Not every story ends this well

Some conflicts take a long time. Some relationships need wise boundaries.

Ordinary faithfulness stands against the enemy’s designs.

Eyes on Christ

We learn how the enemy works so that we can recognise his designs. Then we turn our full attention back to Jesus.

Bringing it together

Truth, grace and forgiveness equip us to resist the enemy’s schemes.

Remember

I will hold to the truth, receive God’s forgiveness, and seek peace with God’s people. My eyes are on Christ.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Lord Jesus, thank you that you know every scheme of the enemy, and that you have already triumphed over him.

Give me wisdom to recognise lies, and the courage to hold on to your truth.

When I hear accusation, remind me that there is no condemnation for those who are in you.

When I am tempted, show me the way of escape. When I am afraid, help me to stand firm in faith.

Where there is hurt or division in my relationships, give me humility to seek peace and grace to forgive.

Keep my eyes on you. Amen.

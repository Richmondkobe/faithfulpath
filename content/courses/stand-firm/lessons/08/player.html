Lesson 8 · Chapter 13 · Part 3: Standing Firm in Everyday Life

Is This a Spiritual Attack?

How do I test spiritual claims and respond wisely when life is difficult?

Imagine…

What if all these problems are an attack?

1 John 4:1–3

“Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God, for many false prophets have gone out into the world.1 John 4:1 (ESV)

By this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God, and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God.1 John 4:2–3 (ESV)

This is the spirit of the antichrist, which you heard was coming and now is in the world already.”1 John 4:3 (ESV)

Point 1

What John was talking about: testing teachers by their confession of Christ

dokimazeteGreek · 1 John 4:1

“test”, as metal is tested to see whether it is genuine

“…that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh…”1 John 4:2

A wider principle, drawn carefully

Not every spiritual claim should be believed.

“Do not despise prophecies, but test everything; hold fast what is good.”1 Thessalonians 5:20–21

Point 2

Testing spiritual claims today

  • 1 · Does it agree with the gospel?
  • 2 · What is its fruit? “You will recognize them by their fruits.” Matthew 7:16
  • 3 · Is money involved in a troubling way?
  • 4 · Does it draw you towards God’s people, or away from them?
  • 5 · Does it bring you to Christ, or make you dependent on a person?

These questions help us assess a claim. Passing them does not, by itself, prove that a particular prediction or message came from God.

Feelings are not the test

An immediate feeling of comfort or fear is not enough.

Truthful correction can be uncomfortable. Manipulative reassurance can feel comforting.

Imagine… · Testing what Tunde heard

  • It claimed a curse without establishing it.
  • It made spiritual help conditional on payment.
  • It pointed to a stranger’s prayer, not to Christ and trustworthy counsel.

Curses and Christ

“Like a sparrow in its flitting, like a swallow in its flying, a curse that is causeless does not alight.”Proverbs 26:2

A proverb, not a formula

“He disarmed the rulers and authorities…”Colossians 2:15

Family patterns, and belonging to Christ

Painful patterns call for repentance where needed, healing, wise help and the support of God’s people.

A believer does not need to buy protection or live in fear. They belong to Christ.

Point 3

Why certainty about the cause is often unnecessary

Scripture asks us to test claims, walk in truth, resist fear and act faithfully.

The four questions are for reflection, not a test that diagnoses a difficulty.

The four questions for Tunde

More than one answer is true

More than one answer is true at once.

Trust Christ · resist fear · act wisely · seek help

The value of wise counsel

“Where there is no guidance, a people falls, but in an abundance of counselors there is safety.”Proverbs 11:14

Two errors to avoid: dismissing real spiritual opposition · seeing it behind everything

If the claim comes from your own leader, seek counsel from another trustworthy Christian who can assess it independently.

Imagine… · Tunde finds clarity

His problems do not disappear immediately, and fearful thoughts may return. But he now has people to turn to and practical steps to take.

He knows whom he belongs to, and he is not facing his difficulties alone.

Testing is not unbelief

Testing is not unbelief. It is obedience to Scripture.

“…test everything; hold fast what is good.”1 Thessalonians 5:21

Bringing it together

We test every spiritual claim by Christ and Scripture, and we can respond faithfully without being certain of the cause.

  • 1 · Test teachers by their confession of Christ
  • 2 · Test claims today; believers belong to Christ
  • 3 · Act faithfully without certainty of the cause

Remember

I will not believe every spirit. I will test what I hear by the truth of Christ. I belong to him, and I will walk in wisdom, not in fear.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Lord Jesus, thank you that I belong to you, and that nothing can separate me from your love.

Give me wisdom to test every spiritual claim by your truth. Keep me from both carelessness and fear.

When I do not understand what is happening in my life, help me to confess my own sin, resist lies, care for practical needs and seek wise counsel.

Protect me from voices that would exploit or frighten me, and draw me closer to you and to your people. Amen.

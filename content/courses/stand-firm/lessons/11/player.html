Lesson 11 · Chapters 18–19 · Part 4: Living in Victory

Guarding Your Heart and Passing Faith On

How do I keep my heart, and help someone else’s faith grow?

Imagine…

Tomas knows something is wrong. How did I get here?

Proverbs 4:20–27

My son, be attentive to my words; incline your ear to my sayings. Let them not escape from your sight; keep them within your heart. For they are life to those who find them, and healing to all their flesh.Proverbs 4:20–22 (ESV)

Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life. Put away from you crooked speech, and put devious talk far from you.Proverbs 4:23–24 (ESV)

Let your eyes look directly forward, and your gaze be straight before you. Ponder the path of your feet; then all your ways will be sure. Do not swerve to the right or to the left; turn your foot away from evil.Proverbs 4:25–27 (ESV)

Point 1

What the Bible means by “heart”, and how hearts drift

lēbHebrew · Proverbs 4:23

“heart”: the inner centre of a person

“…out of the abundance of the heart his mouth speaks.”Luke 6:45 (ESV)

“…from it flow the springs of life.”Proverbs 4:23 (ESV)

If the spring is clouded, the stream will be too.

A father’s instruction · Proverbs 4

“…keep them within your heart.”Proverbs 4:21 (ESV)

What we listen to · what we say · what we look at · where we go

The heart shapes the whole of life, and our daily choices shape the heart.

How hearts drift

“…we must pay much closer attention to what we have heard, lest we drift away from it.”Hebrews 2:1 (ESV)

“…let anyone who thinks that he stands take heed lest he fall.”1 Corinthians 10:12 (ESV)

Not to make us anxious, but to make us honest

God guards our hearts too

“And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.”Philippians 4:7 (ESV)

It is God’s peace that guards the heart.

Vigilance, not fear

Point 2

Rhythms that keep the heart

“…very early in the morning, while it was still dark…”Mark 1:35 (ESV)

“Come away by yourselves to a desolate place and rest a while.”Mark 6:31 (ESV)

“Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits…”Psalm 103:2 (ESV)

A heart that is known by others is harder to lose in secret.

Brief prayers, listening to Scripture, accepting help. Being exhausted is not spiritual failure.

Faithful over the long haul

“…let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, looking to Jesus…”Hebrews 12:1–2 (ESV)

“My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.”Psalm 73:26 (ESV)

“I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.”2 Timothy 4:7 (ESV)

Not a perfect record, but a life that keeps returning to Christ to the end

Imagine… · Tomas turns back

Turning again towards the springs of life

Point 3

Passing faith on

lēbābHebrew · Deuteronomy 6:5–6

“heart”: the fuller form of lēb

“You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart…”Deuteronomy 6:5 (ESV)

“You shall teach them diligently to your children…”Deuteronomy 6:7 (ESV)

Faith in ordinary moments

“…when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise.”Deuteronomy 6:7 (ESV)

Available · honest · ready to point to God

“We were Pharaoh’s slaves in Egypt. And the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.”Deuteronomy 6:21 (ESV)

Our answers can begin with what God has done.

A sincere faith passed on · Lois, Eunice and Timothy

“…a faith that dwelt first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice and now, I am sure, dwells in you as well.”2 Timothy 1:5 (ESV)

“…the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.”2 Timothy 3:15 (ESV)

Encouraging faith across generations

A calling for every believer

“…my beloved child…”2 Timothy 1:2 (ESV)

A grandparent who prays · a friend who reads Scripture with someone · a Sunday school teacher · a youth leader

“We will not hide them from their children, but tell to the coming generation the glorious deeds of the LORD…”Psalm 78:4 (ESV)

Without fear, with honest questions

“…he who is in you is greater than he who is in the world.”1 John 4:4 (ESV)

“In peace I will both lie down and sleep; for you alone, O LORD, make me dwell in safety.”Psalm 4:8 (ESV)

Listen first · admit what you do not know · look for answers together

Questions are not the enemy of faith.

Faithful, not in control

“Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it.”Proverbs 22:6 (ESV)

A wise proverb, not an unconditional guarantee

Called to be faithful, not to control the outcome

“…while he was still a long way off…”Luke 15:20 (ESV)

This week’s practice

Choose one daily rhythm that guards your heart, and keep it all week.

1. The rhythm I will keep each day is… (time with God before your phone, a prayer of thanks each evening, a psalm on the way to work)

2. When and where I will keep it…

Tomas: “Each morning before work, I’ll read Scripture and pray, with my phone in another room.”

3. One person whose faith I can encourage, and one simple way…

“On Thursday I’ll ask a friend from church how I can pray for them.”

Want a short daily guide? The 7-day Battle Plan is on the lesson page.

Remember

“Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life.”Proverbs 4:23

I will guard my heart, for from it flow the springs of life. I will keep running with endurance, looking to Jesus. I will speak of his faithfulness to the next generation, and trust him with their story.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Father, you know my heart better than I do. Show me where I have drifted, and give me grace to turn back to you.

Help me to listen to your word, to speak what is true, to keep my eyes on what is good and to walk in your ways. Guard my heart and mind with your peace.

Deepen my love for you, so that my life points others to you. Help me to speak of your faithfulness in ordinary moments, and to trust you with what I cannot control.

Keep me faithful to the end. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

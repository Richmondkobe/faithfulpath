Lesson 4 · Chapters 5–6 · Part 2: The Armour of God

Be Strong in the Lord and the Belt of Truth

Where does my strength to stand come from?

Imagine…

You are a fraud.

Ephesians 6:10–14a

“Finally, be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might.”Ephesians 6:10 (ESV)

“Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.”Ephesians 6:11 (ESV)

“For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.”Ephesians 6:12 (ESV)

“Therefore take up the whole armor of God, that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand firm.”Ephesians 6:13 (ESV)

“Stand therefore, having fastened on the belt of truth…”Ephesians 6:14 (ESV)

Point 1

Strong in the Lord: strength that is not our own

“…be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might.”Ephesians 6:10

“…the immeasurable greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his great might that he worked in Christ when he raised him from the dead…”Ephesians 1:19–20

When our strength runs out

Lord, I don’t have anything left.

“He gives power to the faint, and to him who has no might he increases strength.”Isaiah 40:29

Exhaustion does not necessarily mean we have failed to trust God.

Point 2

Whose armour is it?

“He put on righteousness as a breastplate, and a helmet of salvation on his head…”Isaiah 59:17

“Righteousness shall be the belt of his waist, and faithfulness the belt of his loins.”Isaiah 11:5

God’s gift to every believer

The armour is God’s own, and he gives it to every believer in Christ.

Not a reward to earn · not only for a few

“…put on the Lord Jesus Christ…”Romans 13:14

The soldier, held lightly

“…the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet the hope of salvation.”1 Thessalonians 5:8

Belt of truthBreastplate of righteousnessShoes: gospel of peaceShield of faithHelmet of salvationSword of the SpiritPraying at all times

Nothing here only a few special Christians can reach.

Our part

Put on · Take up · Stand

God provides, and we respond.

The armour is not a ritual that must be performed correctly.

Point 3

The belt of truth: truth we believe and truth we live

perizōsamenoiGreek · Ephesians 6:14

“having fastened on” · literally “having girded your waist”

“Stand therefore, having fastened on the belt of truth…”Ephesians 6:14

Ready for action

“…with your belt fastened, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand.”Exodus 12:11

The belt of truth is not a decoration. It makes us ready to stand and move.

Two sides of truth

Truth God has revealed: “I am the way, and the truth, and the life.”John 14:6

Truth we live: “Therefore, having put away falsehood, let each one of you speak the truth with his neighbor, for we are members one of another.”Ephesians 4:25

The two belong together.

The truth that sets us free

“If you abide in my word, you are truly my disciples, and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”John 8:31–32

“So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.”John 8:36

Imagine… · Two kinds of untruth

Shading the truth · a false verdict

His dishonesty needs repentance and repair, but it does not put him beyond Christ’s forgiveness.

“…you delight in truth in the inward being…”Psalm 51:6

Imagine… · Kevin takes off the mask

Truth is worth the cost.

Some situations need legal or professional advice. Consequences do not mean we are beyond God’s mercy.

Speaking the truth in love

“…speaking the truth in love…”Ephesians 4:15

Truthfulness is not cruelty. God’s truth, not our feelings, is the final measure.

Bringing it together

Our strength is the Lord’s, and truth — believed and lived — holds everything in place.

  • 2 · Whose armour is it? God wears it first and gives it to us
  • 3 · The belt of truth: truth we believe and truth we live

Remember

“If you abide in my word, you are truly my disciples, and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”John 8:31–32

My strength is not my own. I am clothed with what God provides. I will abide in Christ’s word, live honestly, and speak the truth in love. In the Lord, and with his people, by his grace I will stand.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Lord Jesus, I confess that I often try to stand in my own strength, and I grow tired.

Thank you that the power that raised you from the dead is at work in those who believe.

Thank you that the armour you give is your own.

Help me to abide in your word. Where I have shaded the truth, forgive me, and give me courage to make it right.

Where I have believed false verdicts about myself, help me to hear what you say.

Teach me to speak the truth in love, and keep me close to your people. Amen.

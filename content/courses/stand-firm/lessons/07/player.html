Lesson 7 · Chapters 11–12 · Part 2: The Armour of God

The Sword of the Spirit and Praying at All Times

How do I use God’s Word and prayer in the battle?

Imagine…

Is my prayer life second-class?

Ephesians 6:17b–20

“…and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God,Ephesians 6:17 (ESV)

praying at all times in the Spirit, with all prayer and supplication. To that end, keep alert with all perseverance, making supplication for all the saints,Ephesians 6:18 (ESV)

and also for me, that words may be given to me in opening my mouth boldly to proclaim the mystery of the gospel, for which I am an ambassador in chains, that I may declare it boldly, as I ought to speak.”Ephesians 6:19–20 (ESV)

Point 1

The sword of the Spirit: the Word given by the Spirit, as Jesus used it

rhēmaGreek · Ephesians 6:17

“word”, often something spoken

“All Scripture is breathed out by God…”2 Timothy 3:16

It is what God has said.

A Word that searches us

“For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword…”Hebrews 4:12

Not a weapon against people · not a charm

As Jesus used it

“It is written…” (Matthew 4:4) · “Again it is written…” (Matthew 4:7) · “For it is written…” (Matthew 4:10)

Not magic words: God’s Word, understood and obeyed

The Word in the hardest hours

“My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”Matthew 27:46 · Psalm 22:1

“Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!”Luke 23:46 · Psalm 31:5

“This is my comfort in my affliction, that your promise gives me life.”Psalm 119:50

How we take up the sword

  • What is happening in this passage?
  • What does it reveal about God and his work?
  • What response does it call for?

“I have stored up your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you.”Psalm 119:11

“…be doers of the word, and not hearers only…”James 1:22

Point 2

Praying at all times in the Spirit

Not another piece of armour: many see prayer as the way the whole armour is worn.

all times · all prayer · all perseverance · all the saints

At all times

Ongoing conversation with God

“So I prayed to the God of heaven. And I said to the king…”Nehemiah 2:4–5

In the Spirit

Prayer led and empowered by the Holy Spirit

“…the Spirit helps us in our weakness. For we do not know what to pray for as we ought, but the Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words.”Romans 8:26

Christians differ

Some Christians understand praying in tongues as one expression of praying in the Spirit.

“I will pray with my spirit, but I will pray with my mind also…”1 Corinthians 14:15

In the gathered church: interpretation, so that others are built up.

Others understand Ephesians 6:18 as Spirit-led prayer without seeing tongues specifically in view.

“Do all speak with tongues?”1 Corinthians 12:30

This course holds: a genuine gift of the Spirit · “…do not forbid speaking in tongues.” (1 Corinthians 14:39)

Never limited to tongues · no believer’s prayers are second-class

Point 3

Keep alert, for all the saints

“Watch and pray that you may not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.”Matthew 26:41

Perseverance is not wearing God down. It is continuing to trust him.

For all the saints, and also for me

When you pray for a struggling believer, you help hold up their shield.

“…an ambassador in chains…”Ephesians 6:20

Pray for courage, faithfulness and opportunities to make Christ known.

Imagine… · Ama learns to pray

God hears your prayers in your own language just as much as anyone else’s. Start where you are.

Two misunderstandings about prayer

Prayer is not a technique for controlling outcomes. It is speaking with our Father.

“…your Father knows what you need before you ask him.”Matthew 6:8

Every believer is invited to pray in dependence on the Spirit.

Bringing it together

God’s Word and prayer are how we stand, and how we help others stand.

This week’s practice

1. Choose one verse for your current struggle.

2. Each day, read it in its context: What is happening? What does it reveal about God? What response does it call for?

3. Then turn it into a short prayer for yourself and for one other believer. ✦
“Lord, you have said… so I ask you… for me and for…”

For example: Father, your Spirit helps us in our weakness. Help me and my friend pray when we do not know what to say.

Remember

“I have stored up your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you.”Psalm 119:11

God’s Word is living and active. I will let it search my heart, store it in my mind, and stand on what God has said. I will not stand in my own strength. I will pray at all times in the Spirit, watching, persevering and standing with God’s people.

Prayer

Let us pray.

Father, thank you that I can come to you at any time. Thank you for your Word, living and active.

Holy Spirit, search my heart through it, and lead me to the grace of Jesus.

Help me to read, understand, remember and obey what you have said.

Help me in my weakness, especially when I do not know what to pray. Teach me to pray at all times, not only in emergencies.

Keep me alert and give me perseverance. Help me to pray for my brothers and sisters, and give me boldness to make Christ known. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

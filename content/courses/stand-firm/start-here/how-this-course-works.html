How This Course Works

Four parts, twelve lessons

The course follows the four parts of the book Stand Firm.

Part 2 · The Armour of God4 Be Strong in the Lord and the Belt of Truth · 5 The Breastplate of Righteousness and the Shoes of Peace · 6 The Shield of Faith and the Helmet of Salvation · 7 The Sword of the Spirit and Praying at All Times
Part 3 · Standing Firm in Everyday Life8 Is This a Spiritual Attack? · 9 Submit, Resist and Worship · 10 Together and Free
Part 4 · Living in Victory11 Guarding Your Heart and Passing Faith On · 12 Until He Comes: Stand Firm

In Part 2 you will read Ephesians 6:10–20 straight through, a few verses each week, one piece of the armour at a time.

The picture you will see

From the first lesson, the believer on screen wears the whole armour of God. As we study each piece, it is shown in gold, and it stays gold. The picture shows what we have explored together. God’s whole provision is available to you in Christ from the beginning. The armour is God’s gift. It is not earned, and nothing is added along the way.

What is in each lesson

Every lesson page has the same three parts.

  1. Watch and Learn. A slide lesson of about 15 to 20 minutes, with narration, illustrations and subtitles. Each lesson begins with a story or question, then opens Scripture, explains the passage, and ends with a practice, a declaration and a prayer. A transcript sits underneath.
  2. Reflect and Practise.
    • This week’s practice: one small step, with a short prompt to write your answer.
    • The 7-day Battle Plan (optional): five to ten minutes a day to Read, Reflect, Pray and Declare.
    • Your declaration card: one verse and one statement of trust, to keep on your phone or print.
    • Reflect: a few questions, private and optional.
  3. Go Deeper (optional). The book chapters, a second story, questions for a small group, and a look back at the last lesson.

At the end of each lesson, press I have completed this lesson. Completion records that you took part. The practice, the Battle Plan and the reflections are never required.

About the declarations

Each lesson has a short declaration. Declarations express trust in God and commitment to follow him. Saying them is not a technique for securing protection or guaranteeing an outcome.

Your pace

  • One lesson a week is the best rhythm: time to think, pray and practise.
  • Take the lessons in order. Part 1 prepares for everything after it. All lessons stay open, so you can return to any lesson at any time.
  • Read the passages in your own Bible as well. The course is meant to lead you into God’s Word, not replace it.

Your notes are private

What you type on a lesson page is for you. It is not saved or sent anywhere, so write and print it, or use your own notebook.

With a group or a church

You can take this course on your own, with a friend, with your family or in a small group. Spiritual warfare is not meant to be fought alone. Leaders can use the Leader’s Guide: a 60-minute plan for each of the twelve sessions, with discussion questions and pastoral guidance.

At the end of the course

After Lesson 12 you can write My Stand Firm Plan: one truth you stand on, the piece of armour you most need, your daily rhythm of Word and prayer, the people you stand with, and one person you will help to stand.

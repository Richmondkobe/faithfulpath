How This Course Works

One lesson a week, at your own pace

Concerns, Care and Reporting

One lesson a week

Each lesson takes about 20 minutes to watch, with a little more time to reflect and a practical action for the week. We recommend one lesson a week, in order: the first three lessons are about you, and they prepare you for the practical craft that follows.

All the lessons are open. If you are facing a hard conversation this week, you can go straight to Lesson 7.

Urgent concerns come first. Urgent concerns should be addressed promptly, not held for the relevant lesson.

Allow about 20–30 minutes for each lesson and reflection, plus time for the week’s action. Repeat a week when needed.

What you will find on each lesson page

  1. Watch and Learn — a narrated lesson of about 20 minutes, with slides, subtitles and a transcript. Scripture is read slowly, and its background and original words are explained.
  2. Reflect and Practise — five questions to reflect on, this week’s action, and, in most lessons, a practical sheet for real ministry (for example, a Role and Decision Agreement to fill in with your pastor or ministry head).
  3. Go Deeper (optional) — a seven-day reading plan, the book chapter, questions to talk about with a leaders’ group or mentor, and a look back at an earlier lesson.

At the end of the course, My Leadership Plan helps you bring the ten lessons together into a few practical commitments.

Your reflections are private

Your reflection answers are private and optional. You can write on the page and print your answers, or use your own notebook. Your written answers are not saved to your account. Print or copy them before leaving the page. The course saves only your lesson-completion progress. You can complete every lesson without answering any of them.

Keep sensitive ministry information — pastoral details, safeguarding matters or passwords — in your church’s proper secure system, never in a reflection or on a worksheet.

Better with someone else

If you can, work through the course with a friend who also leads, a mentor, or the person who appointed you to your role. Share only what is appropriate. Several lessons will encourage you to talk with your pastor or ministry head, because a course cannot know your church.

Completing the course

When you mark a lesson complete, it records that you took part. It is not a test, and nothing is scored. When all ten are complete, My Leadership Plan shows the date you finished.

If something concerns you

If you have a concern about harm, abuse or misconduct, you do not need to wait for a lesson or to confront the person concerned first. Use the appropriate reporting route.

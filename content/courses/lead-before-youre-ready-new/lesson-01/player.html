Lead Before You’re Ready

You are not required to be ready. You are required to be willing, teachable, and honest about what you do not know.

What do I do with the feeling that they chose the wrong person?

Feeling inadequate does not, by itself, mean you were wrongly chosen.

A unique call, and an ordinary one

The Lord’s anger was kindled after “please send someone else.” And he still gave Moses Aaron.

Moses’ call at the bush was unique in Israel’s history.

Your call will usually come through a need, a nudge, and the confirmation of people who know you.

Point 1

The feeling you’re having is normal

“Do not say, ‘I am only a youth’… Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you, declares the Lord.”Jeremiah 1:7–8 (ESV)

Acts 4:13

“They recognized that they had been with Jesus.”Acts 4:13 (ESV)

Formal status is not the whole measure of a leader. That does not make training unnecessary.

Willingness is a beginning

Willingness opens the door.

Character, preparation, competence and supervision help you walk through it.

Your uncertainty can keep you teachable. Let it also show you what you need to learn.

Point 4

What “called” does and does not mean

Calling is not the same as feeling certain.

God frequently speaks through his people, not only through our emotions.

Not permission to stay as you are

Calling is not permission to stay as you are.

Being called does not download the skills.

Not permission to bypass the training, supervision or safeguarding the role requires.

Bringing it together

  1. The feeling you’re having is normal
  2. Our sufficiency is from God
  3. Character matters greatly, and every role needs its own skills
  4. Calling is not certainty, and not permission to stay as you are

This week’s action

1. Write down three things you do not know how to do in this role.

2. Take the list to the person who put you in the role, or to whoever oversees your work.

3. Ask them to help you with one of the three. Do it this week.

You are not confessing failure. You are learning from the people God has put around you.

Remember

“But I will be with you.”Exodus 3:12

“Our sufficiency is from God.”2 Corinthians 3:5

God has not abandoned his people. Depend on his grace, seek his wisdom, and ask for help.

Prayer

Lord God, you called Moses when he felt unready, and you promised, “I will be with you.”

Thank you that my sufficiency is from you, and not from myself.

Where I am afraid, give me courage. Where I am unprepared, make me teachable.

Grow in me the character this work needs, and give me humility to ask for help.

Help me to serve the people you have entrusted to me, for your glory and not my own.

In Jesus’ name. Amen.

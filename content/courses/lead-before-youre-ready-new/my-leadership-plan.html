My Leadership Plan

Lead Before You’re Ready · printed

You have reached the end of the course, but not the end of learning. None of us arrives. This page helps you look back over the ten lessons, choose a few realistic commitments for your own leadership, and name your next step. Take your time. Write as much or as little as you like.

“And I am sure of this, that he who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ.”PHILIPPIANS 1:6 (ESV)

Paul was encouraging believers that God would continue his good work among them. As we finish this course, our confidence rests in God’s faithfulness, not in having mastered leadership.

Looking back: the ten steps
  1. 1
    Called, Not Qualified
    Our sufficiency is from God; calling is not certainty, and not permission to stay as you are
  2. 2
    Leading Yourself First
    A modest fixed time with God, a trusted person, and real rest
  3. 3
    Knowing Your Role
    Delegated authority is entrusted, limited and accountable
  4. 4
    Building a Volunteer Team
    Choose prayerfully, ask clearly, and let people say no
  5. 5
    Helping Volunteers Stay and Grow
    Prepare, send and listen; pass on skills in four steps
  6. 6
    Running a Meeting People Want to Attend
    One page, finish on time, and make room for every voice
  7. 7
    The Conversation You’re Avoiding
    Go to find out, not to deliver a verdict
  8. 8
    Criticism, Control and Ongoing Pastoral Needs
    Assess criticism, address over-centralised responsibility, support ongoing care
  9. 9
    Burnout and Boundaries
    Recognise exhaustion early, respect your limits, and seek rest and support
  10. 10
    Growing the Next Leader
    Entrust what you received to faithful people who can teach others

Private and optional. Skip any you like.

1 · My calling (Lessons 1 and 3)

When you began, you may have felt that they chose the wrong person. What has God shown you since about his call and your role?

2 · My own soul (Lessons 2 and 9)

What will help you keep walking with God, and keep going without wearing out?

3 · My team (Lessons 4 to 8)

Think of the people you lead. What is the next good step for them, or for one of them?

4 · The next leader (Lesson 10)

Who might you help to grow, or what will you look for?

A commissioning prayer

Lord Jesus, you called me before I was ready, and you have been faithful.

Thank you for all you have taught me in these lessons.

Strengthen me by the grace that is in you.

Keep my soul close to you, and keep me from wearing out.

Help me to serve the people you have given me with honesty, patience and love.

Give me eyes to see the next leader, and the humility to make room for them.

Finish the good work you have begun in me.

In Jesus’ name. Amen.

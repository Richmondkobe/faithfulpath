Lesson 2 · Book chapter 2 · Part 1: Start With Yourself

Leading Yourself First

Who is tending my own soul while I tend everyone else’s?

The vineyard you forget to tend

“They made me keeper of the vineyards, but my own vineyard I have not kept!”Song of Songs 1:6 (ESV)

You can produce spiritual food for others all the time, and eat almost none of it yourself.

1 Timothy 4:12–16

“Let no one despise you for your youth, but set the believers an example in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.1 Timothy 4:12 (ESV)

Until I come, devote yourself to the public reading of Scripture, to exhortation, to teaching. Do not neglect the gift you have, which was given you by prophecy when the council of elders laid their hands on you. Practice these things, immerse yourself in them, so that all may see your progress.1 Timothy 4:13–15 (ESV)

Keep a close watch on yourself and on the teaching. Persist in this, for by so doing you will save both yourself and your hearers.”1 Timothy 4:16 (ESV)

A young leader in Ephesus

1 Timothy 1:3 — Timothy left in Ephesus to deal with false teaching

Paul’s charge to Timothy. We apply it, carefully, to every leader.

The original meaning

ἔπεχε

epeche1 Timothy 4:16

to pay close attention — and keep paying it

“on yourself”your life and conduct
“and on the teaching”what you teach

Both.

Point 1

Life and teaching together

Not a ranking. Both matter.

“…you will save both yourself and your hearers.”1 Timothy 4:16 (ESV)

Persevering in the faith. Christ alone saves.

Point 2 · Jesus made room to be with the Father

“And rising very early in the morning, while it was still dark, he departed and went out to a desolate place, and there he prayed.”Mark 1:35 (ESV)

Even when the need is real

“But he would withdraw to desolate places and pray.”Luke 5:16 (ESV)

Your ministry is not too urgent for it either.

The apostles shared the work

“It is not right that we should give up preaching the word of God to serve tables.”Acts 6:2 (ESV)

“But we will devote ourselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word.”Acts 6:4 (ESV)

Point 3 · Integrity is mostly private

“Behold, you delight in truth in the inward being, and you teach me wisdom in the secret heart.”Psalm 51:6 (ESV)

“Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.”Psalm 51:10 (ESV)

Repentance deals with the inside

Repentance addresses conduct and harm, not merely reputation.

Repentance never replaces truth, accountability or action to address harm.

“Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life.”Proverbs 4:23 (ESV)

After preaching to others

“But I discipline my body and keep it under control, lest after preaching to others I myself should be disqualified.”1 Corinthians 9:27 (ESV)

Paul took seriously that a preacher could fail to live what he preached.

Point 4

What this looks like on a Tuesday

  • Keep some Bible reading for your own growth
  • A fixed time, not a hopeful intention
  • A trustworthy person who can ask honest questions
  • Confidentiality has limits where someone may be at risk of harm.

Rest, and noticing strain

Protect a regular period of genuine rest, and agree how urgent needs are covered.

tirednesscynicismisolationsmall dishonesties

Tiredness is not a moral failure. It is a signal to pay attention.

Bringing it together

  1. Keep watch over your life and your teaching
  2. Make room to be with the Father, even when the need is real
  3. Integrity is mostly private; repentance deals with the inside
  4. A modest fixed time, a trusted person, and real rest

“Keep a close watch on yourself and on the teaching.”

Watch it happen: a realistic week

Mon · Wed · Fri, 6:30–7:00 — reading Mark slowly. Saturday evening off; urgent matters covered.

First Thursday of the month: coffee with an older friend.

One example, not a rule.

Fixed, modest and protected.

Reflect, on your own, after the lesson

Five questions are waiting on the lesson page.
Take them at your own pace.

To start you thinking: When did I last read the Bible with no intention of using it for anyone else?

Date: ____________

This week’s action

1. Put one protected appointment for prayer and personal Bible reading in your calendar this week.

2. Make it modest enough to keep.

3. Then contact one trustworthy person and ask about meeting monthly.

Protect the prayer appointment before you fill your calendar with ministry tasks.

Remember

“Keep a close watch on yourself and on the teaching.”1 Timothy 4:16

“Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life.”Proverbs 4:23

Receive from God’s word yourself, not only prepare it for others. Tend your own vineyard.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you made room to be alone with the Father, even when everyone was looking for you.

Teach me to do the same.

Where I have been feeding others and starving myself, feed me again by your word.

Create in me a clean heart, and keep me true in private as well as in public.

Give me one trustworthy friend, and the humility to be honest with them.

In your name. Amen.

Burnout and Boundaries

How do I keep leading without wearing out — and without making those closest to me pay the bill?

Leaders erode before they collapse

For many leaders, exhaustion develops gradually rather than dramatically.

Dreading Sunday · short with those you love · prayer like paperwork · relief at the thought of it ending

Burnout can affect even deeply committed and conscientious leaders. It is not proof of laziness or weak faith.

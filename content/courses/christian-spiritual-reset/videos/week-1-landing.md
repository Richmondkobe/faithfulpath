Welcome to your first week home.

This is the soft week.

Expect a period of adjustment, and do less than you may feel tempted to do.

Your essential tasks are simple: keep the rhythm you chose on Day 3, imperfectly; sort the cards you brought home; and tell your support person that you are back.

If you are still tired, that does not automatically mean something went wrong. Your body and mind may need more time to recover from the demands you were carrying.

But if the tiredness becomes severe, continues to deepen, prevents you from functioning, or affects your safety, read Lesson 27 and seek appropriate help. That does not wait for the calendar.

Do not announce major conclusions this week. Do not redesign your life. Do not write your rule of life yet.

Notice what happened without forcing yourself to interpret everything.

Psalm 23 is your psalm for this week. Read it again at home, and notice whether you hear anything differently now.

Keep your rhythm. Sort your cards. Have one honest conversation.

Land gently.

I will see you next week.

This is the week when the cards begin to leave the pile.

Have one Share conversation using the three sentences from Lesson 25:

"This is the burden."

"This is how I have been carrying it alone."

"This is the specific help I am asking for."

Specific requests are easier to understand and answer than general appeals.

Next, work through the Jethro questions in your journal. Be especially honest with the first one: "What am I doing that only I can do?"

That list is often shorter than it first appears.

If you are testing something significant, meet with your Eli—your mature, trustworthy adviser—this week. Ask them to help you examine it, not simply confirm it.

Then complete the two-week check on your rhythm.

Is it realistic? Can it survive an ordinary, difficult week? If not, make it smaller. Do not abandon it merely because the first version was too ambitious.

Some things you attempt this week may not work. Someone may decline your request. An appointment may be weeks away. You may miss your rhythm.

That is information, not failure.

A delayed appointment can still be booked. A refused request can lead to a different conversation. A missed rhythm can be resumed.

Psalm 62 is your psalm for this week:

"For God alone my soul waits in silence."

Do not attempt everything.

Do the next small, responsible thing.

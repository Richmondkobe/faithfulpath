This is the final week of the plan and the week of your review.

If you left Session Six, on forgiveness, for this month, it may belong here—but only if its conditions are met.

You are rested and settled. The harm is not continuing. You want to explore what release might mean without forcing a particular outcome.

If you are uncertain, use the short prayer and leave the rest. Session Six may not be right for this month, or even this year. Choosing not to force it is a legitimate decision.

Forgiveness is not reconciliation, restored trust, or returning to danger.

If you are testing a major direction, revisit your Testing Worksheet now that time has passed. Consider what Scripture, the facts, your condition, and your advisers have shown you.

Day 30 is not a decision deadline.

On Day 30, sit down for an hour with your journal, your cards, and the intention you wrote before the retreat.

You are not there to grade the month. You are there to see it clearly.

Begin with grace received. Where did you notice help, kindness, provision, truth, rest, or God's presence? Begin there before evaluating what you accomplished.

Then record the four outcomes this course was designed to help you carry home:

One burden entrusted to God.

One responsible action taken.

One person involved.

One sustainable rhythm kept.

If one of those lines is blank, that is not a failing grade. It shows you where your next step begins.

If Lesson 27 showed that you needed additional help, record the call you made, the appointment you booked, or the next access step you will take.

Finally, confirm the date of your next deliberate pause. A rhythm needs a next time, and the calendar is where rhythms survive.

Psalm 131 is your psalm for this week: the picture of a soul calmed and quieted before God.

You began this course tired in a place that sleep could not reach.

Go on your way, and go with others.

Work when it is yours to work. Stop when it is time to stop.

The world is held by God, not by you.

The Lord bless you and keep you.

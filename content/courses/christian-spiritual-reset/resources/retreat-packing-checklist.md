# Retreat Packing Checklist

Copy this and tick it off before you leave. Items marked (optional) are just that.

**Sessions and reflection**

- Bible, printed or prepared
- This course or printed session lessons from Module 4
- Journal (or accessible alternative) and two pens
- Lesson 4 check-in, Lesson 7 intention, Lesson 8 notes
- Timer or watch
- Index cards or loose paper
- Settling object (optional)

**Medication and health**

- All regular medication, with extra
- Prescriptions, supplies, or devices
- Essential medical information, accessible in an emergency
- Accessibility aids and medical equipment

**Clothing and sleep**

- Layered clothes for the season
- Walking shoes
- Pillow, earplugs, eye mask (optional)
- Outdoor items: sunscreen, hat, repellent, rain protection, first-aid kit

**Personal care and accessibility**

- Toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, deodorant
- Towel, if not provided
- Menstrual products, if needed
- Glasses, contact-lens supplies, hearing-aid batteries

**Food and water**

- Simple food meeting your needs (see Lesson 11)
- Refillable water bottle

**Safety, communication, and transport**

- Phone, prepared and tested
- Charger and power bank
- Paper card: address, emergency contact, host, doctor, emergency number
- Identification and booking details
- Cash or card
- Transport confirmed both ways

**Location-specific**

- Bedding and towels, if not provided
- Cooking equipment, if self-catering
- Keys or entry code

**Leave behind**

- Work and work devices
- Unfinished projects (completed, delegated, or rescheduled)
- Extra books
- Objects that create pressure
- Guilt acknowledged and addressed using Lesson 2

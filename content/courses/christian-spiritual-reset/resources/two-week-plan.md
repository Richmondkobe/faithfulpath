# Two-Week Plan

Begin with the minimum. Add the rest only if your present capacity allows.

**Minimum, over two weeks**

- One Act step: ______ done on ______
- One Share conversation, with: ______
- One Seek-counsel appointment or access step: ______
- Rhythm practised, imperfectly, on (days): ______
- Jethro questions answered: yes / not yet

**If capacity allows**

- A second Act step: ______
- A second Share conversation, with: ______
- One structural change requested from: ______ Result: ______
- Lesson 24 review date, if testing: ______

If your present capacity allows less, choose the actions that most protect your health, safety, and faithfulness.

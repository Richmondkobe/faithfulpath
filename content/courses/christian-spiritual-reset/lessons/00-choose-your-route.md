# Choose Your Route

You do not have to complete all the preparation lessons before receiving help from this course. Choose the route that matches the capacity you have right now. You can change routes later.

## Route 1: Guided Preparation

Choose this route if you want to understand what you are experiencing and prepare carefully before beginning your retreat.

1. Complete Module 1, Why Your Soul Needs a Retreat.
2. Complete Module 2, Preparing to Meet With God.
3. Choose your retreat format in Module 3.
4. Put the retreat dates in your diary and protect the time.
5. Begin the core retreat journey in Module 4. The core three-day retreat uses seven guided sessions; three additional sessions are optional.
6. After the retreat, continue to Module 5 and begin your 30-day return plan.

Time commitment: approximately four to six hours of preparation, spread over one or two weeks, followed by your chosen retreat and the 30-day return plan.

Choose Guided Preparation if:

- You can concentrate on reading and reflection.
- You want to understand burnout, spiritual dryness, and emotional overload more clearly.
- You can protect three days, or several shorter periods, for a retreat within the next month.
- You prefer to prepare thoroughly before beginning.

## Route 2: Quick Start

Choose this route if you are already exhausted and need a shorter spiritual pause before completing the full teaching.

1. Read [Lesson 5, What a Retreat Can and Cannot Do](./05-what-a-retreat-can-and-cannot-do), including the four paths at the end.
2. Read [Lesson 9, Creating a Digital Boundary](./09-creating-a-digital-boundary), and put your boundary in place.
3. Complete the Quick-Start Preparation Checklist below.
4. Choose either the [Three-Hour Spiritual Reset](./33-the-three-hour-spiritual-reset) or the [One-Day Retreat](./32-the-one-day-retreat-with-god) in Module 3.
5. Complete your chosen reset.
6. Afterwards, return to Modules 1 and 2 when you have more capacity.
7. Plan the full retreat later if it is appropriate for you.

Time commitment: approximately one hour of preparation, followed by a three-hour reset or a one-day retreat.

### Quick-Start Preparation Checklist

Before beginning, confirm that you have:

- Read the course's safety guidance.
- Chosen a safe place where you are unlikely to be interrupted.
- Written a simple intention for your reset.
- Protected the necessary time.
- Created your digital boundary.
- Prepared your Bible, journal, pen, water, food, and medication.
- Told one trusted person where you will be.
- Decided how a genuine emergency can reach you.
- Allowed some quiet transition time after the reset.

## Not sure which route to choose?

Answer these three questions.

**1. Can you protect three consecutive days within the next month?**

Yes: Guided Preparation may be the better route. It will help you prepare carefully for the full experience.

No: Choose Quick Start. Begin with the three-hour or one-day reset. You can also consider the at-home retreat, which spreads the sessions over several days.

**2. Are you able to concentrate on reading and reflection right now?**

Yes: You may benefit from completing Modules 1 and 2 before your retreat.

No: Choose Quick Start. Complete only the essential preparation and begin with a shorter reset. Return to the deeper teaching when you have more capacity.

**3. Did anything in the welcome lesson's safety guidance describe your present situation?**

No: You may choose either route.

Yes, or you are uncertain: Go first to [Finding Help Where You Live](./00-finding-help-where-you-live) and read Lesson 5 before attempting any retreat. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact emergency or crisis support now and tell a trusted person who can remain with you. Do not begin the retreat alone.

## Before you continue

Whichever route you choose, Lesson 5, What a Retreat Can and Cannot Do, and Lesson 9, Creating a Digital Boundary, are essential before any retreat.

Choosing a route is not a permanent commitment. It is simply an honest decision about what you can carry today.

You can begin gently and go deeper later.

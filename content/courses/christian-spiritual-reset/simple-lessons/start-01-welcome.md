## Audio script and transcript

*(About four to five minutes. Read calmly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed “Read the transcript” section.)*

Hello, and welcome. My name is Richmond Kobe. I have served as a pastor for more than twenty years, in different countries and different kinds of churches.

During that time, I have sat with many people who were running on empty. Some were pastors and church leaders. Some were parents or carers. Others were people whose faith had quietly grown dry, but nobody around them had noticed.

Most of them did not need another sermon. They did not need another spiritual task. They needed permission to stop and a safe, simple way to do it.

That is what this course is for.

This is not a course to finish quickly. It is a guided retreat.

A retreat is planned time away from the usual noise and demands of life. It gives you space to rest, slow down and become honest with God.

Here is the promise of this course: you will pause, become honest with God about what you are carrying, and return to daily life with a simple rhythm you can keep.

I also want to be honest about what this retreat cannot promise.

I cannot promise that you will feel God in a particular way. I cannot promise that God will answer a particular question during your retreat. Your time with God may be quiet and gentle rather than dramatic. That is all right.

My hope is that you will take home four simple things.

First, one burden placed in God’s care.

Second, one next step.

Third, one person to speak with.

And fourth, one rhythm of prayer and rest.

Those four things are enough. Everything else in this course will help you move towards them.

You can choose how long your retreat will be. The full retreat lasts three days, and I recommend it when that is possible. There is also a one-day retreat and a three-hour pause if you need a shorter place to begin. You will choose your retreat later.

There is something else I need to say, because your safety matters.

A retreat is not a replacement for a doctor, counsellor or crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, thinking about harming yourself, or being hurt or threatened by someone, please do not begin a long period of silence alone. Get help first.

Asking for help is not a lack of faith. It is wise and brave. The Safety and Support page will help you find the right kind of support.

One story from the Bible will walk with us through this course. It is the story of Elijah.

Elijah was a prophet. After a great victory, he became afraid and completely exhausted. He ran into the wilderness, lay down under a tree and asked God to let him die.

Notice what God did next.

God did not tell Elijah to try harder. He did not tell him to have more faith. He allowed him to sleep. He sent an angel with food and water. Then Elijah rested again.

The angel said, “Arise and eat, for the journey is too great for you.”

Only later did God speak to Elijah about what he should do next.

Rest came before direction.

Perhaps you are tired in a similar way. If so, you are in the right place.

Before you go on, take one small step. Choose whether to continue now or return when you have more time and space. <#3#>

When you are ready, continue to the next page. I am glad you are here.

Let us begin.

## PLAN VIEW: THREE-DAY RETREAT *(Recommended)*

**Seven core sessions over three days, with meals, sleep and long breaks.**

### Day 1 — Arrive and become present

* **Morning:** breakfast, settle in, no session yet. If you are exhausted, sleep.
* **Late morning:** Session 1 — Come As You Are
* **Midday:** lunch, then rest or sleep
* **Mid-afternoon:** Session 2 — Be Still and Become Present
* **Evening:** simple meal and rest. Day 1 is finished.

### Day 2 — Tell the truth and set things down

* **Morning:** Session 3 — Put Down What You Are Carrying
* **Late morning:** rest for at least an hour
* **Midday:** lunch and rest
* **Mid-afternoon:** Session 4 — Honest Prayer When It Hurts
* **Late afternoon:** a long break of two hours or more
* **Evening:** stop here. Session 5 — Confession and Grace only if you feel rested, settled and willing. If in doubt, leave it for Day 3.

### Day 3 — Listen and return

* **Morning:** Session 5 — Confession and Grace, *if you did not do it on Day 2*
* **After Session 5:** rest for at least an hour before continuing.
* **Late morning:** Session 7 — Listening for God's Direction
* **Midday:** lunch and a long rest
* **Afternoon (optional):** Session 8 — Remember Who You Are **or** Session 9 — Renewing Your Purpose, only if you did Session 5 on Day 2 and feel rested. Choose one, not both. Resting instead is a good choice.
* **Late afternoon:** Session 10 — Going Home With a New Rhythm
* **Evening:** simple meal, pack slowly, rest. The retreat is over.

**Later, not during the three days:** Session 6 — Forgiving Others and Yourself (optional, with support where needed), and any flex session you did not use.

**Five shortened sessions with generous breaks.**

| | Long break — about an hour and a quarter |

| | Break — 20 minutes. Eat something. |

## Audio script and transcript

*(About three minutes. Read calmly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed “Read the transcript” section. This one recording is used for every plan.)*

You have prepared well. Now your retreat is ready to begin.

On this page, you will see the plan for the retreat you chose. You do not need to remember it all. Each session page will tell you what to do, and what comes next.

Before you begin, here are a few things to keep in mind.

First, rest is part of the retreat. The breaks, meals and times of sleep are not time taken away from God. They are part of the plan. Remember Elijah. He slept and ate before God spoke to him about what came next.

Second, the times on this page are a guide, not a rule. If a session takes longer, or you need more rest, adjust the plan. Follow your own needs for food, medication and sleep.

Third, you may pause or stop any session. If something becomes too much, stop, stand up, notice where you are, and contact your support person if you need to. A paused retreat is not a failed retreat.

Fourth, you do not have to feel something special. Some retreats feel peaceful. Some feel quiet and ordinary. Both are real. Simply keep turning towards God.

And finally, some sessions are marked as optional or only if you feel settled. Please take that seriously. Doing less, and doing it gently, is often the wiser choice.

If you chose the three-hour reset, remember that it is a first pause. It is helpful and real, but it is not the whole retreat. You are welcome to return for the longer journey later.

When you are ready, take a slow breath, and begin Session 1. <#3#>

God is already with you. Let us begin.

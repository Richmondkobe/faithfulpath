# Check-in 2 — Ready to Begin

*Part 2 — Prepare Your Retreat · Check-in*

**What you will do here:** Check six simple arrangements, then open your retreat plan.

This is not a test. You do not have to tick every box to continue. If something is not ready, the link beside it will take you to the lesson that can help.

**Need urgent help?** → Get Help Now

## Six things to check

☐ **My retreat, place and date are chosen.**
*Not yet?* → Lesson 5: Choose Your Retreat, Place and Date

☐ **A trusted person knows that I am planning a retreat.**
*Not yet?* → Lesson 2: You Have Permission to Pause

☐ **My phone plan is chosen and tested.**
*Not yet?* → Lesson 7: Put Your Phone in Its Place

☐ **I have planned for food, water, medication and sleep.**
*Not yet?* → Lesson 8: Pack Simply and Care for Your Body

☐ **My Bible, journal and simple supplies are ready.**
*Not yet?* → Lesson 8: Pack Simply and Care for Your Body

☐ **I have read the Safety and Support page and know how to reach help.**
*Not yet?* → Start Here 4: Safety and Support

*Your ticks are not saved. They are only here to help you prepare.*

---

## Before you begin

* You may pause or stop any session.
* You do not need to fast.
* A quiet retreat is not a failed retreat.
* **Get Help Now** is available on every page.

If your situation has changed since Check-in 1, or you no longer feel safe being alone in quiet, return to **Check-in 1** or **Safety and Support** before starting your retreat.

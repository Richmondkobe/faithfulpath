# Day 30 Review

*Part 4 — Bring It Home · Follow-up*

**Best completed after 30 days.** You may open it at any time.

**What you will do here:** Look back gently on your first month after the retreat or reset: what helped, what was difficult and what support you need next.

This is not a test. Nothing is scored, and this review does not decide whether your retreat or reset “worked.” It is simply a time to notice.

## If you need help right now

If you are in immediate danger, may act on thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or cannot keep yourself safe, stop this review and use **Get Help Now**. If you are struggling but not in immediate danger, go to **Lesson 23: Know When to Ask for Help**.

---

## Before you begin

Set aside about 15–30 minutes. Stop earlier if that is enough. Have your journal, your cards and your Session 10 rhythm card nearby.

Begin with a short prayer, in your own words or these:

> *“Lord, thank you for being with me this month, in the good days and the hard ones. Help me see honestly and gently.”*

---

## Three questions

**1. What helped?**
Where did you notice help, kindness, rest or God's presence this month, especially things you did not produce by your own effort?

*[ Optional: write your answer ]*

**2. What was difficult?**
What was hard to keep, hard to face or still unresolved? You may simply name it.

*[ Optional: write your answer ]*

**3. What support do I need next?**
Is there a conversation, an appointment, a change in your load or a person you need to involve?

*[ Optional: write your answer ]*

---

## Your four things from Session 10

Look at each one. You do not need to write anything unless it helps.

* **One burden entrusted to God:** how are you carrying it now?
* **One action:** did it happen, or does it need a new time?
* **One person or service:** did you make contact?
* **One rhythm:** keep it, make it smaller, move it or replace it?

*A blank or unfinished one is not a failure. It simply shows where your next step begins.*

---

## If help was needed

If you recognised any of the signs in **Lesson 23** this month and have not yet reached out, this is a place to begin again. If the need is urgent, seek help now. Otherwise, choose one contact and a time to reach out this week.

---

## Close

Read these words slowly:

> Go on your way, and go with others.
> Work when it is yours to work.
> Stop when it is time to stop.
> The world is held by God, not by me.

☐ I have paused and reviewed my month.

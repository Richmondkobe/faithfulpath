## Audio script and transcript

*(About three to four minutes. Read calmly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed “Read the transcript” section.)*

Let me show you how this course works. It is simple.

Every teaching lesson follows the same three steps.

First, listen. Each lesson begins with a short recording like this one. If you prefer to read, you can open the transcript instead.

Second, think. After the recording, there will be one main question. You can simply think about it, or you can write your answer. Writing is always optional.

Third, take one step. Each lesson gives you one small, practical action. It is not a long list of homework. It is one thing you can actually do, usually in less than fifteen minutes.

Listen. Think. Take one step.

That is all you need to do in each teaching lesson.

When you begin your retreat, the pattern changes slightly. Each retreat session has four parts.

First, listen. You will hear a Bible passage and a short teaching.

Second, pray. I will guide you through a short spoken prayer.

Third, be still. You will spend a few minutes in quiet with God. A simple timer will help you, and you can choose how long you want the silence to last.

Fourth, respond. Each session will give you one question and one practical exercise. You may write your answer, think about it quietly, or complete the exercise in the way the page explains.

Listen. Pray. Be still. Respond.

You do not need to remember everything now. The instructions will be on every page.

Here are a few other things I want you to know.

Nothing in this course is timed or graded. There are no scores and no tests. You cannot fail this course.

One lesson is enough for one sitting. You do not need to rush. If you become tired or distressed, stop. You can return later, and the course will remember what you have completed.

Your answers are private. I do not read them, and no counsellor monitors them. They are there to help you reflect, not to be checked or judged by anyone.

Please also remember that this course does not replace a doctor, counsellor or crisis service. If you need that kind of help, please reach out. The Safety and Support link appears on every page, so you can find help whenever you need it.

Some pages also include a quiet link to the Go Deeper Library. That is where you can find the complete teaching from the book. It is available if you want to study more, but you do not need to read it to complete the course.

Now take one small step.

Choose a place where you can listen with as few interruptions as possible. It might be a chair by a window, a corner of your bedroom or a table you can use early in the morning. <#3#>

It does not have to be perfectly quiet. Choose the best place available to you.

When you have chosen your place, continue to the next page.

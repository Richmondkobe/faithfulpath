## Audio script and transcript

*(About three to four minutes. Read calmly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed “Read the transcript” section. This one recording is used for every route.)*

Welcome home.

Whether you took a three-hour reset, a one-day retreat or the full three-day retreat, you did something many tired people never do. You stopped. You became honest with God about what you were carrying. And you brought something home.

I want to say thank you. I know that making time was not easy.

At the start of this course, I said I hoped you would take home four simple things: one burden placed in God's care, one next step, one person to speak with, and one rhythm of prayer and rest. Those four things are enough. If some of them are still unfinished, that is all right. They show you where to begin next.

We have followed Elijah through this whole course. Remember what God did for him. God did not tell him to try harder. God let him sleep, and gave him food and water. God listened to his honest answer. God gave him a next step, and a companion to share the work. And God told him, “Go, return on your way.”

Rest came before direction. And God did not send Elijah back alone.

That is still the pattern. Go on your way, and go with others. Work when it is yours to work. Stop when it is time to stop. The world is held by God, not by you.

If you took the one-day retreat or the three-hour reset, you are welcome to come back for the longer journey whenever you are ready. Everything you have completed is saved.

A rhythm needs a next time. So on this page, if you are ready, you can choose when you will pause with God again: perhaps a three-hour reset next month, a one-day retreat within three months, or a full retreat within the year. If choosing now feels like pressure, you can leave it for later.

And in about a month, you are invited to open the Day 30 Review. It is not a test. It is simply a chance to look back gently and notice what has helped.

Please remember that help is always available. If things become heavy, the Safety and Support page and Get Help Now are always there.

Thank you for walking this path. <#3#>

The Lord bless you and keep you.

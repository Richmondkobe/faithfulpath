## Audio script and transcript

*(About two to three minutes. Read calmly and naturally. On the course page, place this inside the closed “Read the transcript” section.)*

Now it is time to choose where you would like to begin.

There are three choices on this page. There is no right or wrong choice. Choose the one that best fits what you need today.

The first choice is, “I want to prepare for a retreat.”

Choose this if you have some time and energy to prepare. After one short Safety and Support page, you will begin with Lesson 1. The lessons will help you understand the kind of tiredness you may be carrying and prepare carefully for your retreat.

The second choice is, “I need a short pause soon.”

Choose this if you are already very tired and need a simple place to begin. After the Safety and Support page, you will check a few essential arrangements and begin a three-hour reset.

The three-hour reset is a first pause. It does not include everything in the full course. When you have more time and energy, you can return and continue with the complete retreat.

The third choice is, “I need support before I begin.”

Choose this if you may be unsafe, in crisis or too distressed to spend time in retreat alone. You may need help with your health, safety or circumstances before a retreat would be helpful.

This choice will take you to the Safety and Support page, where finding appropriate help will be the first option.

Choosing support is not failure. It does not mean you are being turned away. It means you are recognising what you need right now.

You are welcome to return to the course when you have the support you need.

Whichever route you choose, no course page is permanently locked. You can return to this page and change your starting point at any time. Anything you have already completed will remain saved.

Take a moment to notice which choice best describes what you need today. <#3#>

When you are ready, select one of the three cards.

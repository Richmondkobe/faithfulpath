*Recording note (not spoken): record one opening and one closing, in the same unhurried voice as the prayers. The platform inserts the silence between them, so the same two clips serve every length; only the spoken number changes, so record the opening four times (five, ten, twenty, thirty) or leave the number out.*

## Opening

This is a silence of [five / ten / twenty / thirty] minutes.

You do not have to fill it.

Hold your anchor phrase. When your attention wanders, and it will, say the phrase once, slowly, and let it go again.

You may keep your eyes open. You may move gently. You may stop at any time.

When the time is finished, you will hear my voice again.

[silence]

## Closing

The silence is ending.

Take one slow breath.

Notice the room. Notice where you are.

There is no need to hurry. When you are ready, open your journal.

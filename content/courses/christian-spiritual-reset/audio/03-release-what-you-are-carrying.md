---
title: "Guided prayer: Release What You Are Carrying"
lesson: 15-release-what-you-are-carrying
length: "4 min"
---

*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds.*

Before we pray, notice where you are holding tension.

Perhaps in your jaw, shoulders, hands, or somewhere else.

You do not have to change it.

Simply notice.

[pause]

Jesus, you said, "Come to me."

[pause]

And I have come.

[long pause]

I am carrying more than I can hold.

[pause]

I have been carrying some of it as though no one else would.

[pause]

Some of it is mine to act upon.

Some of it I cannot control.

Some of it should be shared with another person.

Some of it needs counsel or qualified help.

[pause]

And I cannot always tell the difference.

[long pause]

Teach me.

[pause]

Show me what is mine to do.

[pause]

Show me what to share.

[pause]

Show me where I need counsel or help.

[pause]

Show me what to entrust to you.

[long pause]

I take your yoke because you are gentle and lowly in heart.

[pause]

I place before you the anxiety I have been holding because you have said that you care for me.

[pause]

Say your own name now, quietly.

Then receive the invitation of Jesus personally:

"Come to me."

[long pause]

Give me rest.

Not rest from every responsibility.

Rest from carrying everything as though it all depends on me.

[long pause]

Amen.

Remain where you are and move into the silence.

Your anchor phrase is:

"Come to me, and I will give you rest."

Or, more simply:

"Gentle and lowly in heart."

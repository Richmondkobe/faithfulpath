*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds. The listener may fill the gaps or leave them empty.*

Before anything is confessed, we begin with who God is.

That is the order of Psalm 51, and it is the order here.

[pause]

Have mercy on me, O God, according to your steadfast love.

[pause]

Before I name what I have done, I remember what you are like.

[pause]

Faithful.

[pause]

Just.

[pause]

Abundant in mercy.

[long pause]

I have seen something during these days that I did not see before.

[long pause]

I have chosen…

when I knew better.

[long pause]

I have avoided…

[long pause]

I do not offer excuses.

[pause]

I bring the truth to you.

[long pause]

Create in me a clean heart, O God.

[pause]

Renew a right spirit within me.

[pause]

Restore to me the joy of your salvation.

[long pause]

You have promised that when we confess our sins, you are faithful and just to forgive and cleanse us.

[pause]

Help me receive what you have promised, whether or not I feel it yet.

[long pause]

If you notice yourself returning repeatedly to the same matter, searching for something more to confess, stop here.

You have named it.

You do not have to achieve a particular feeling before you may leave it with God.

[pause]

If compulsive confession or persistent religious guilt is a pattern for you, speak with a pastor or mental-health professional who understands scrupulosity and religious OCD.

[pause]

According to your steadfast love, have mercy on me.

[pause]

Amen.

Remain where you are and move gently into the silence.

Your anchor phrase is:

"According to your steadfast love."

Or, more simply:

"Have mercy on me."

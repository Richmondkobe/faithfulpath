title: "Guided prayer: Renewing Your Purpose"
lesson: 21-renewing-your-purpose
length: "4 min"

*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds. The listener may fill the open space or leave it empty.*

On the beach after the resurrection, Jesus asked Peter one question three times.

He began not with Peter's competence, but with his love.

[pause]

Lord, you know everything.

[pause]

You know what I have loved.

[pause]

You know how tired I am.

[long pause]

You asked Peter whether he loved you.

Then you entrusted work to him.

[pause]

Ask me.

[long pause]

Here is my answer, as honestly as I can give it today.

[long pause]

Help me recognize the responsibilities and relationships that are legitimately mine in this season.

[pause]

Help me also recognize what I have taken on through fear, pressure, guilt, or the need to feel indispensable.

[long pause]

Renew love where it has been buried beneath duty.

[pause]

Show me where I need rest.

[pause]

Where I need support.

[pause]

Where the structure of my life needs to change.

[pause]

Where I need to grieve.

[pause]

Or where service may need to take a different form.

[long pause]

If something must become smaller or come to an end, help me see it clearly.

I will test what I sense before I act.

[long pause]

Keep me following you into whatever comes next.

[pause]

Amen.

Remain where you are and move gently into the silence.

Your anchor phrase is:

"Lord, you know everything."

Or, if it is honest today:

"Lord, you know that I love you."

*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds. The listener may fill each gap silently or aloud, or leave it empty.*

This prayer contains open spaces.

When you reach one, fill it with your own words, silently or aloud.

If the words do not come, leave the space empty.

An empty space can also be a prayer.

[pause]

You may keep your eyes open.

Many people find that easier during lament.

[pause]

God, I have been carrying grief I did not have time to feel.

[pause]

And anger I did not believe I was allowed to express.

[long pause]

You preserved the laments.

You preserved Psalm 13.

You preserved Psalm 88, which ends in darkness.

You gave these prayers a place in Scripture.

[pause]

So I will risk bringing you one honest question.

[pause]

How long?

[long pause]

I do not understand why…

[long pause]

I am tired of…

[long pause]

I have lost…

[long pause]

And I have not been able to say it until now.

[long pause]

Consider me.

[pause]

Answer me.

[pause]

Light up my eyes.

[long pause]

I do not have the final verses yet.

I cannot say, "I will sing," today.

I will not pretend.

[pause]

Meet me in the part of the prayer I can honestly pray.

[long pause]

Amen.

Before entering the silence, notice how you are.

If this prayer has brought up more than you can safely hold, do not continue into silence.

Open your eyes. Stand and move. Orient yourself to the room. Contact your support person if you need them, and follow the guidance from Lesson 5.

If you feel able to continue safely, remain where you are and move gently into the silence.

Your anchor phrase is:

"How long, O LORD?"

Or, if that feels too raw today:

"Consider and answer me."

*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds. This prayer is only for learners who have freely chosen this session. The shorter prayer at the beginning of the session remains a complete alternative.*

Before we begin, hear this again.

Forgiveness is not reconciliation.

It is not restored trust.

It is not returning to danger.

It does not cancel justice, and it does not require contact with the person who harmed you.

You may keep every boundary you need.

[pause]

If this prayer becomes more than you can safely hold, stop.

Open your eyes. Stand and move. Contact your support person if you need them.

Stopping is allowed.

[long pause]

Lord, I have received mercy I could never earn.

[pause]

I also carry a real wrong committed against me.

It caused real harm.

[long pause]

Someone did something.

[pause]

It was wrong.

[pause]

I will not pretend that it was not.

[long pause]

Where the desire for vengeance has bound me to what happened, begin your work of release within me.

[pause]

Do not force today what cannot safely be forced today.

[long pause]

I keep the boundaries I need.

[pause]

I do not owe the person who harmed me my trust.

[pause]

I will not abandon truth or justice.

[long pause]

Do within me what I cannot do by willpower.

[long pause]

Where I have tried to pay for my own guilt by continuing to punish myself, help me receive your forgiveness.

[pause]

Christ has already borne what I could never repay.

[long pause]

Show me the next safe and faithful step.

Only the next one.

[long pause]

Amen.

Before continuing, notice where you are and whether silence feels safe today.

You may shorten it, keep your eyes open, use supported silence, or end the session here.

If you continue, your anchor phrase is:

"Into your hands I place this wound."

Or:

"God sees, and God cares."

Or simply:

"Show me the next safe step."

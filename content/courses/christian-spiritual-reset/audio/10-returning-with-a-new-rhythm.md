*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds. The listener may fill the open space or leave it empty.*

Keep the cards from your retreat nearby, but do not sort or select the final four yet.

That exercise will come after the silence and journaling.

[pause]

Lord, you told Elijah to return.

You also showed him that he was not alone.

There were seven thousand.

There was Elisha.

[long pause]

I am preparing to return.

[pause]

Unfinished.

[pause]

And still held by your grace.

[long pause]

Thank you for what these days contained.

If there is something particular for which you want to give thanks, name it now.

[long pause]

If nothing comes to mind, let the silence remain.

[pause]

What is still unclear, help me carry patiently and test wisely.

[long pause]

What needs action, help me approach responsibly.

[pause]

What needs counsel, lead me toward the right people.

[pause]

What is not mine to control, teach me to entrust to you.

[long pause]

Give me appropriate companionship and help me receive it.

[pause]

Go with me into ordinary life.

Teach me to recognize your presence there.

In the kitchen.

In the inbox.

In the car.

In conversations and responsibilities.

[long pause]

Give me a sustainable rhythm rather than a dramatic resolution.

[pause]

Teach me to resume when I miss it, without punishment or guilt.

[long pause]

Amen.

Remain where you are and move into the final silence of the retreat.

Do not sort the cards yet.

For these few minutes, do not look back or plan ahead.

Simply remain here with the God who has been present throughout these days.

Your anchor phrase is:

"Go, return on your way."

Or:

"I am not alone."

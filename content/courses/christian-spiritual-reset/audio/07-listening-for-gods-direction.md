*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds. The listener may fill the gap or leave it empty.*

Place the one-line question you wrote in your journal somewhere nearby.

[pause]

Speak, Lord, for your servant hears.

[long pause]

You asked Elijah, "What are you doing here?"

And you allowed him to answer twice.

[pause]

Here is what I am doing here.

[long pause]

I am not asking only for comfort.

[pause]

I am not asking only for permission.

[pause]

I am asking for direction, even where it corrects me.

[long pause]

Show me what your Word already says.

[pause]

Give me wise and appropriately qualified people who can help me test what I believe I hear.

[long pause]

Whatever I sense today, I will write down without immediately calling it your instruction.

[pause]

I will test it.

[pause]

I will not act rashly.

[long pause]

If I sense nothing today, I will trust you anyway.

[pause]

You met Elijah after the wind, the earthquake, and the fire had passed.

He heard a low whisper.

[pause]

I can wait without forcing a whisper of my own.

[long pause]

Amen.

Remain where you are and move into the silence.

Bring your question before God, then release your demand for an immediate answer.

Your anchor phrase is:

"Speak, Lord, for your servant hears."

Or, more simply:

"Here I am."

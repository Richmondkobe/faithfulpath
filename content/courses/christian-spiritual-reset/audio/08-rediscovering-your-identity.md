*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds.*

If parental language is difficult because of your history, you may address this prayer simply to "God of steadfast love" or "Lord."

You may also use the final alternative anchor phrase.

[pause]

Father—

or, God of steadfast love—

before Jesus began his public ministry, before he preached a sermon or performed a public miracle, you said:

"This is my beloved Son."

[long pause]

Through Christ, you have adopted me and called me your child.

[pause]

Not your employee.

Not your project.

Your child.

[long pause]

I have measured my worth by usefulness.

[pause]

By approval.

[pause]

By achievement.

[pause]

It has made me tired because I never feel permitted to stop.

[long pause]

Help me recognize what is true without pretending that my responsibilities and callings do not matter.

[pause]

Let my roles grow from belonging rather than becoming the price of belonging.

[long pause]

Teach me to serve without making service the measure of whether I am loved.

[long pause]

I am loved before I am useful.

[pause]

Say that once more, quietly, in your own voice:

"I am loved before I am useful."

[long pause]

Amen.

Remain where you are and move gently into the silence.

Your anchor phrase is:

"See what love the Father has given us."

Or:

"Abba, Father."

If parental language feels difficult today, use:

"Nothing can separate me from the love of God in Christ."

Or simply:

"I am loved before I am useful."

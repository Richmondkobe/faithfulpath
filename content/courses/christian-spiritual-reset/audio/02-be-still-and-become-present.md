---
title: "Guided prayer: Be Still and Become Present"
lesson: 14-be-still-and-become-present
length: "3 min"
---

*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds.*

Settle into your seat.

Feel the chair supporting you.

Let your shoulders rest.

[pause]

You may close your eyes or rest them on a fixed point.

[pause]

God, you are a very present help.

[pause]

I have been living somewhere else.

Ahead of myself, planning.

Behind myself, replaying.

Beside myself, watching.

[pause]

I bring my attention back now.

To this room.

[pause]

To this hour.

[pause]

To this body, which you made and know completely.

[long pause]

The storm is real.

I will not pretend it is not.

[pause]

The noise in my life is real.

The noise within me is real.

[pause]

But Christ is with me here.

[long pause]

Teach me to be still.

Not because the world has become quiet.

It has not.

[pause]

Teach me to be still because you are here.

[long pause]

Help me cease striving for the rest of this day.

[pause]

"Be still, and know that I am God."

[long pause]

Amen.

Stay where you are and move gently into the silence.

Your anchor phrase is:

"Be still, and know that I am God."

Or, more simply:

"God is here."

---
title: "Guided prayer: Come As You Are"
lesson: 13-come-as-you-are
length: "4–5 min"
---

*Recording note (not spoken): Read slowly. Do not speak the directions in brackets. [pause] is approximately five seconds. [long pause] is approximately fifteen seconds.*

Find a position in which you can remain for a few minutes.

You may close your eyes, or keep them open and rest them on something still.

[pause]

Take one slow breath.

If noticing your breathing feels uncomfortable, notice your feet on the floor or the chair supporting you instead.

[pause]

Let us pray.

Father, I am here.

If the word "Father" is difficult because of your history, you may simply pray, "Lord, I am here."

[pause]

That is the most honest thing I can say.

I am here.

[pause]

I have come tired.

I have come with explanations.

I have come with fears and with plans for proving myself to you.

[pause]

I set those plans down now.

[pause]

I cannot earn the welcome you give by grace.

I never could.

[pause]

You saw me before I began this journey.

While I was still a long way off, you saw me.

[long pause]

Help me receive your mercy before I try to explain or improve myself.

[pause]

Let me be honest with you during these days.

Not impressive.

Honest.

[pause]

I place the outcome of this retreat in your hands.

[long pause]

If the picture of an embrace feels difficult, you do not have to use it.

Pray instead:

"Help me know that I am welcomed in your presence."

[long pause]

Now take the intention you wrote before coming.

Read it to God exactly as you wrote it—including what you had not said and the line about releasing the outcome.

If you need more time than the silence that follows, pause this recording. Continue when you are ready.

[long pause]

Your intention does not need to be improved.

It only needs to be honest.

[pause]

Father, I am here.

Or simply:

Lord, I am here.

[pause]

Amen.

Remain where you are and move gently into the silence.

Your anchor phrase is:

"While he was still a long way off, his father saw him."

Or, if you prefer something shorter:

"I am here."

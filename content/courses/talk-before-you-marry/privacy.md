## What the site stores

- **Your account.** Your email address and a securely stored password.
- **Your lesson-completion progress.** Which lessons you have marked complete.

That is all this course keeps about you.

## What the site does not store

- **Your answers.** The lessons have no place to type anything. The reflection questions are for prayer and thought, and nothing you think or write in response is sent anywhere.
- **Your worksheets.** You download them and complete them on your own device or on paper. They are never uploaded. They stay with you unless you choose to share them.
- **Your card details.** Payments are handled by Stripe.

## Tracking

This course does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Like most websites, its hosting service keeps basic technical records, such as the time of a visit and the IP address, to keep the site working and secure, and keeps them only for a limited time.

## Who can see your information

Each partner has a separate account. Your partner cannot see your account or your progress, and you cannot see theirs.

Only the course author, who runs the site, can technically access account and completion information, and uses it only to run the course and give support.

## Keeping your own notes safe

Because your private preparation stays with you, keep it somewhere private and secure. If writing something down would not feel safe, you may hold the reflection in thought or prayer instead. The Safety and Support page has more on using this site safely when someone else may see your device.

## Asking about your information

Write to info@faithfulpathcommunity.com with any question about what is held about you, or to ask for your account to be closed.

## What this course is

*Talk Before You Marry* is Christian teaching and education, written to help a couple speak honestly with each other before they marry.

## What this course is not

It is not therapy, and it is not medical, legal or financial advice. Nothing in it replaces care from a qualified professional, and nothing in it should be relied on as advice about your own situation.

**It is not an emergency service.** If you are in immediate danger, contact the emergency service where you are, if you can safely do so. The Safety and Support page says more about where to find help.

Finishing the course does not certify that you are ready, or not ready, to marry. There is no score and no assessment.

## Your use of the course

Your membership is for your own personal use. Each person uses their own account.

Please do not copy, share, publish or teach from the course material without permission. If you are a pastor, counsellor or mentor and want to use it with a couple, the Facilitator Guide explains how, and you are welcome to write to us.

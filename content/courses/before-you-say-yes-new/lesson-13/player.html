Lesson 13 · Book chapter 13 · Part 4

“God Told Me” and Other Hard Questions

How do I test what feels like God’s leading?

“God told me you’re going to be my wife.”

Not every feeling is God’s voice, and God’s guidance is not to be dismissed. Tests that Christians on every side can recognise.

“God told me” must never be used to pressure anyone. Guidance is on the page beneath this lesson.

1 Thessalonians 5:19–22 · the end of Paul’s first letter

“Do not quench the Spirit. Do not despise prophecies, but test everything; hold fast what is good. Abstain from every form of evil.”1 Thessalonians 5:19–22 (ESV)

Understand the original meaning

δοκιμάζω

dokimazō ✱ · “test”: to test or examine, to see whether something is genuine

Not believe everything. Not dismiss everything. Test, then hold fast what is good.

Explore the background

A young, eager church, unsettled by claims attributed to Paul: neither quench, nor accept every claim.

“Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others weigh what is said.”1 Corinthians 14:29 (ESV)

Testing is not a lack of faith. It is obedience.

Point 1 · What Scripture says about guidance

God has already given a great deal of direction.

“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”Psalm 119:105 (ESV)

“…free to be married to whom she wishes, only in the Lord.” 1 Corinthians 7:39 A genuine choice, not a treasure hunt for one hidden name.

How does God guide?

WisdomPrayerCounselConscienceProvidence

“If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach, and it will be given him.”James 1:5 (ESV)

“…in an abundance of counselors there is safety.”Proverbs 11:14 (ESV)

Conscience matters, but it must itself be formed and tested.

Christians differ

Christians differ on how God speaks today through impressions, dreams and prophetic words.

On every side: test everything. And no claim that God has spoken binds another person’s conscience.

Point 2 · The problem with “God told me”

Nothing wrong with sensing God’s leading. The problem is how the claim works, spoken aloud.

Not self-proving
Not self-proving

The other person cannot examine the experience behind it.

Pressure
Pressure

Saying no feels like saying no to God.

A feeling, baptised
A feeling, baptised

Loneliness, fear and desire can feel like conviction.

Sincere, and wrong
Sincere, and wrong

Sincerity is not accuracy.

Not your obligation

Your own free consent is necessary.

You do not need a competing revelation in order to say no.

Point 3 · How to test a claim

The same tests, for anyone’s claim, or your own

  1. 1Conflict with Scripture?a “word” to marry an unbeliever, or ignore what is unsafe, is not from God
  2. 2Bypass wisdom?an impression over a fact
  3. 3Require haste?urgency from a person is not divine urgency
  4. 4Require isolation?private is fine; isolated from wise evaluation is not
  1. 5Try to decide for someone else?no authority over another adult’s choice
  2. 6Weighed, and by whom?independent believers, free to disagree
  3. 7What fruit, in conduct?humility and respect, or pressure and punishment?
  4. 8Open to being wrong?the most telling test

“This is what I believe God has shown me, and I may be mistaken, and you are free.”

The test is not sincerity, but humility.

One further test, the plainest

“…if the word does not come to pass or come true, that is a word that the LORD has not spoken.”Deuteronomy 18:22 (ESV)

Failure is decisive. Fulfilment is not enough. The setting is Israel’s prophets: apply with care.

Their claim does not override your freedom to say no. You need not wait for the outcome.

Point 4 · Peace, open doors and fleeces

1“I have peace about it”

In context, the church together “in one body”.

Attraction can feel like peace; weight can feel like anxiety.

Worth noticing. Not worth obeying on its own.

2“God opened a door”

An open door is not a command.

A wide door in Ephesus, with many adversaries.

Wisdom decides which to enter.

“…behold, I am laying a fleece of wool on the threshing floor…”Judges 6:37 (ESV)

God’s patience with a frightened man, not a method for choosing a spouse.

“If they text me before nine, it’s a sign” is chance dressed as revelation.

“Is there one person?”Not “Is this the one?” but “Is this wise?”
“What if I miss God’s will?”“For this is the will of God, your sanctification.” 1 Thessalonians 4:3

If you choose foolishly and repent, his grace is not smaller than your mistake.

“Am I disobeying God by saying no?”No. You are not. It may be the most obedient thing you do.
“My pastor said…”Telling you whom to marry steps beyond counsel. You are among those who weigh it.
“I prayed and felt nothing.”Normal. Use the tools God has already given.

“I don’t feel led to end it, even though it’s harming me.”

You do not need a special word to leave harm.

“The prudent sees danger and hides himself…”Proverbs 22:3 (ESV)

If leaving could put you in greater danger, get confidential, independent help to plan your next step safely.

“Should I wait for a sign?” Waiting can postpone what is already clear.

A word about safety

Used to override your judgement, silence your questions, hurry you, or make you afraid? Take the spiritual language off, and look at what is being asked.

If a church leader is applying the pressure: confidential help outside that leader’s influence. Threats or danger: Lesson 6’s safety guidance applies.

Your own sense of leading: hold it with open hands.

“I am interested in getting to know you.”

If it is from God, testing will not harm it. If it is not, testing is a mercy.

Used “God told me” to pressure someone? Acknowledge it, and release them. Apologise only where appropriate and welcome; respect any request for no contact.

Had it said to you? Test it, and decide on grounds both of you can examine.

He may have been speaking all along, in the ordinary ways.

Bringing it together

  1. Test everything; hold fast what is good (1 Thessalonians 5:21)
  2. No claim proves itself, and none binds another person’s conscience
  3. Test by Scripture, wisdom, time, counsel, fruit and humility
  4. Peace, open doors and fleeces: worth noticing, not obeying on their own

One step now

Write one sense of leading you have had, and one way to test it.

“I sensed I should take this relationship further; I will ask two wise friends who know us both.”

If it is safe, write it privately, or simply think it through before God.

Prayer

Lord, I do not want to be deceived, and I do not want to deceive myself. Where your name has been used to move me, help me see what is really being asked.

Where I have used your name to get what I wanted, forgive me. Teach me to want your will more than I want this to be your will.

And give me the wisdom you promised — not a sign or a feeling, but clear wisdom to see what is in front of me and decide honestly. In Jesus’ name, amen.

Before You Say Yes to Engagement

How do we know we are near the threshold?

A threshold, not a finish line

Not a premarital guide: Talk Before You Marry is for the conversations that belong to engagement.

Single, or not near it? This lesson is still for you: it shows what you are walking towards.

Genesis 2 · the foundation of marriage

“Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh.”Genesis 2:24 (ESV)

Understand the original meaning

1leave2hold fast3one flesh

A change of first loyalty, and a new household, freely formed. Scripture also speaks of marriage as a covenant (Malachi 2:14).

Engagement is not that covenant. It is a serious promise to enter it.

Explore the background

“For which of you, desiring to build a tower, does not first sit down and count the cost…”Luke 14:28 (ESV)

About the cost of discipleship, not marriage: used here only as an illustration.

“Will you go with this man?” She said, “I will go.”Genesis 24:58 (ESV)

Point 1 · What engagement is

A mutually declared intention to marry. Serious, but not yet marriage.

Not a light thing: ending an engagement can carry emotional, financial and communal costs.

Not a way to discover whether you are interested in marriage. Dating should have established that.

Engagement is not irreversible.

It can be paused, and it can be ended. Either is far better than a marriage entered because the invitations were already printed.

Engaged and troubled? Lesson 16. Afraid? Lessons 6, 10 and 19 first.

Point 2 · Essential before engagement

These are not signs. They are conditions.

  1. •Both people freely intend marriageA reluctant yes is not made free merely because it is eventually spoken.
  2. •Neither person is pressured or frightenedIf “not yet” would bring threats or punishment: not jointly. Seek independent help.

Essential before engagement (continued)

  1. •No ignored directional incompatibilityEngagement does not resolve it. It only makes it public.
  2. •No serious concern still under reviewIf you are in a slowdown, finish it.
  3. •A shared understandingA season of preparation, not an experiment with the idea of marriage.

Point 3 · Signs that you may be near the threshold

Descriptions, not a scorecard.

  1. •The important questions have been facedNot to silence a concern, force progress, or find out whether you want marriage.
  2. •You know each other across ordinary lifeTiredness, disagreement, family, money, disrupted plans. No manufactured tests.
  3. •Words and observed character agreeThey describe the same person.

Signs you may be near (continued)

  1. •Known, not hiddenCounsel informs your discernment; it does not replace your decision.
  2. •The shape of married life discussedIf most of your talk is the ceremony, you are further away than it feels.
  3. •It can tolerate difficult questions“I’m not sure about that”, without punishment, sulking or rebuke.

Point 4 · The pressures that create a premature engagement

Every pressure in this course shows up again here.

  1. •FamilyTheir voice belongs in counsel, not in the decision.
  2. •ChurchThe enthusiasm is kind, and it is not discernment.
  3. •Age and timeFear of time is not a reason to propose.

The pressures (continued)

  1. •Sexual pressureDesire is legitimate; engagement is not sexual permission, and a rushed wedding repairs nothing.
  2. •MomentumA long relationship is not owed an engagement.
  3. •The other personReadiness may be unequal; your readiness is not their obligation.

Point 5 · How to approach the decision

Talk about it before you decide it.

The manner may be a surprise; the question should not be.

Bring it to counsel, separately: is there anything we should look at first? Counsel observes; it cannot certify a marriage.

Work through the questions, before the ring, not after.

Decide, both of you, and say it plainly. The proposal celebrates a decision already made: not a leap, but a step you can both see the ground under.

Questions Before Engagement

1Free intention2What we have faced3What we know4Faith5Direction6Counsel7Pressure8The relationship9Readiness10The decision

Two free and informed yeses. One no ends it. One “not yet” postpones it.

Each person alone first. Not together if anyone is afraid, or if “not yet” would bring punishment: the safety guidance comes first.

If you become engaged, the work changes.

Take Talk Before You Marry with you, and seek thoughtful premarital preparation where it is safely available.

Where fear, coercion or abuse is present: individual specialist help, not joint counselling.

The freedom to say yes with your eyes open, and to keep them open.

Not near the threshold? That is not a failure.

Go back to the lesson that fits. Do not let urgency make the decision for you.

“Desire without knowledge is not good…”Proverbs 19:2 (ESV)

Give an honest horizon, and move forward only when both of you can do so freely.

At the beginning: why do you want a relationship?

You know what you are looking for, and what you will not accept. None of it depends on marriage coming.

Before you say yes to anyone, you must know that you are free to say no.

Bringing it together

  1. Engagement is a promise to enter the covenant of marriage: serious, and still breakable
  2. Five conditions first: free intention, no fear, no ignored incompatibility, no open concern, a shared understanding
  3. Notice the signs, and name the pressures
  4. Talk, take counsel, work through the questions, then decide plainly, together

One step now

Write the one question you most need answered before engagement.

“Have we really talked about where we would live, and whether we want children?” Or: “What would I need to see before I could say yes freely?”

If it is safe, write it privately, or simply think it through before God.

The full Questions Before Engagement are below this lesson (optional): each person alone first. Nothing is saved. If anyone is afraid, do not complete them together.

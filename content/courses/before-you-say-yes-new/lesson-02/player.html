A foundation, not a guarantee

Equally yoked: sharing a genuine allegiance to Christ, from which you can build a life together.

A shared foundation is necessary. It does not decide that the house should be built.

Point 3 · Four mistakes to avoid

1234

1. Missionary dating

Hope is not a strategy. Share the gospel with anyone; build a marriage on shared faith.

2. Assuming the more “spiritual” person is safer

Spiritual vocabulary is evidence of exposure, not of transformation.

3. Demanding a finished product, or accepting an unproven one

4. Forgetting that the yoke has two sides

Is your own faith your own? Where are you going?

That is not discernment. It is pressure wearing religious clothes.

You do not have to prove your faith by submitting to pressure. You may step back and seek support from someone trustworthy.

Point 4 · Where you are right now

Not datingtime to look at your own faith
Dating someone who is not a Christianbe honest; talk with a pastor
Married to someone who does not share your faithnot permission to leave (1 Corinthians 7:12–14)

This does not mean remaining in an abusive or dangerous situation. If you are unsafe, seek appropriate support.

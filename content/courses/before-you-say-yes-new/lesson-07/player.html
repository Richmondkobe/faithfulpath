Point 2 · Ten green flags

Ten green flags

relationalcommended by Scriptureobservable

1They respect your “no”

No punishment, no pressure, no affection on condition.

Consent concerns each occasion and can always be withdrawn (Lesson 9).

What it will be like to say no to them for years to come.

2Honest disagreement

Respect and safety remain.

Time out, then back to the conversation.

“Speaking the truth in love” (Ephesians 4:15)

3Responsibility and repair

Names it · sees the effect · no blame · repair

“…he who confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy.” (Proverbs 28:13)

4. Their words and behaviour increasingly align.

Say it · do it

5Your relationships and identity

Glad when you are with people they are not.

Disappointment, without punishing or blaming.

7Relevant truths

Privacy, but not as permission for deception.

8Teachable

10Mutuality

Both can speak, choose, give and receive.

Few long friendships is not automatically a concern.

Quality, reciprocity and openness.

Every one of the ten is undramatic.

Point 3 · The decision: exclusivity

“Are we exclusive?”

Both people freely agree to stop pursuing other romantic possibilities for a season, to explore this relationship more intentionally.

not engagementnot ownershipnot a reason to speed up

A real decision, not a drift.

Before exclusivity

Exclusivity should come after a certain amount has been seen, not before.

Strong recommendations, not universal laws

  1. ✓Several windows have opened (Lesson 5)
  2. ✓No abuse or immediate safety concern
  3. ✓Green flags seen, with evidence
  4. ✓A shared foundation in faith (Lesson 2)
  5. ✓Nothing foundational bent (Lesson 3)
  6. ✓Seen among each other’s people, where safe
  7. ✓I can say no, and I am still myself

We have both said, in plain words, what it means, and we mean the same thing.

Pace · physical boundaries · who is told · what we are moving towards

Online and long-distance

Online contact can create deep attachment before ordinary life has been seen.

A screen reveals something real, but not everything.

no moneyno dependent travelno engagement

…before identity and circumstances are independently confirmed. “Not yet” is a reason for patience, not shame.

Point 3

An honest word about contentment

“I have learned in whatever situation I am to be content.”Philippians 4:11

Contentment is not the absence of desire. It is the presence of trust.

A content person can date carefully, because they can afford to say no.

Contentment helps us date wisely, because it gives us freedom to say no.

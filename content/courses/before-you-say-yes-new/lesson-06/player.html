Red Flags Christians Spiritualise

When is a spiritual explanation hiding a real warning?

Easier to see from the outside

Warning signs can appear clearer in someone else's relationship than in our own.

The disguises

The language of faith can turn a warning sign into a virtue

“God's timing”rushed
“spiritual leadership”control
“guarding what God has given us”isolation
“between us and the Lord”secrecy
“protection”jealousy

None of these reframings changes what the behaviour is.

1. Spiritual manipulation

A genuine word from God does not need you to stop thinking.

2Jealousy disguised as protection

Care helps your life flourish; control makes your life revolve around the controller.

3Rushed commitment

“whoever makes haste with his feet misses his way”

A shorter courtship is not automatically a warning. The warning lies in pressure.

4Secrecy

If someone needs the relationship to be invisible to the people who love you, ask what would become visible if it were not.

5Repeated boundary violations

An apology without changed behaviour does not restore safety.

6Lack of accountability

Isolation is the environment in which every other pattern grows.

7Refusal to accept correction

A person who turns every correction back onto you is teaching you to stop raising things.

Serious the first time

Some actions are serious the very first time.

Not differences to work on. Reasons to seek safety and help.

Where to find help is on the page beneath this lesson.

Point 3

Why warning signs can be hard for Christians to name

Forgiveness“I should forgive.”Forgiveness is not the same as restored trust.Matthew 18:22
Not judging“Who am I to judge?”You can recognise fruit.Matthew 7:20
Not gossiping“Isn’t this gossip?”Seeking counsel is wisdom.Proverbs 11:14
Suffering“God uses hardship.”He does not ask you to volunteer for mistreatment.
Grace“Everyone deserves a second chance.”Grace is not another opportunity to harm.
ForgivenessNot judgingNot gossipingSufferingGrace
The teaching is true. The distortion is not.

Faith does not require us to ignore what is true.

Forgiveness does not make repeated wrongdoing harmless.

Love and wisdom belong together.

Beware of him yourself

Wariness is not unforgiveness.

Entrust repayment to God, and protect those who could be hurt.

Point 4

What to do when you recognise a flag

  1. Consider severity and pattern
  2. Take the spiritual language off
  3. Talk to someone outside
  4. Notice whether raising it is possible
  5. If safety is involved, act on safety first

Turning the list on yourself

Do not use this to blame yourself for another person’s harm.

Recognition is a beginning. Change is what matters.

Lesson 4 · Book chapter 4 · Part 2

Attraction Is Not Discernment

If I feel this strongly, doesn’t it mean something?

“There was just something about him.”

“I knew the moment I saw her.”

Attraction is real, powerful, and often the first thing that happens.

Two unhelpful ways

Everything“If it is strong, it must be right.”
Suspicious“Wanting someone is shallow.”

Attraction may open the door. Discernment decides whether it is wise to walk through it.

Hebrews 5:14

“But solid food is for the mature, for those who have their powers of discernment trained by constant practice to distinguish good from evil.”Hebrews 5:14 (ESV)

Discernment is not something you are handed in a moment. It grows.

Understand the original meaning

γεγυμνασμένα

from gymnazō · “trained, exercised”

our wordgymnasium learned over time, not a single moment of certainty
Discernment is a trained capacity. it grows withpractice · honesty · patience It does not come free with a strong feeling.

Explore the background

First about spiritual maturity in general, not about dating.

Choosing a spouse needs a trained, practised judgement.

Point 1 · What attraction is

A response, not a decision. But you choose what you do with it.

Physical
Emotional
Intellectual
Spiritual

They often overlap, and they do not always arrive together.

Not shameful: desire is part of how God made us.

What attraction can tell you

1. A door is open
2. Something about yourself
3. It matters for marriage

Attraction may grow, but that growth cannot be promised.

Pretending to feel what you do not feel is not a spiritual discipline.

Point 2 · What attraction cannot tell you

Honest?Kind?Faithful?Self-controlled?Safe?

Early attraction responds to limited information. Character becomes clear through repeated patterns.

Chemistry is fast.
Character is slow.

“For the Lord sees not as man sees: man looks on the outward appearance, but the Lord looks on the heart.”1 Samuel 16:7 (ESV)

The choice of a king, not a spouse. The principle carries.

“Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain, but a woman who fears the Lord is to be praised.”Proverbs 31:30 (ESV)

It applies in both directions.

“Vain”: fleeting, unable to secure what it seems to promise.

Point 3 · Why attraction clouds judgement

a sharp remark

“just their sense of humour”

lateness

“they’re so busy”

a flash of temper

“they’re passionate”

silence about their past

“they’re private”

In someone else, it would be noted. In someone you are drawn to, it is explained.

The explaining is not stupidity. It is attraction, doing what attraction does.

Intensity is not meaning.

The strength of the pull is not proof of the goodness of the person.

Familiar is not always safe.

comforting because it is healthy?
or only recognisable?

“I’ve known you forever” is worth examining before it is trusted.

Point 4 · What discernment is

A prayerful, evidence-based judgement, made over time, with people who are not attracted to them.

Discernment does not cancel attraction. It tests it.

“The simple believes everything, but the prudent gives thought to his steps.”Proverbs 14:15 (ESV)

1. Give it time.

Not suspicion. Wait long enough to see them clearly.

2. Let ordinary life show you who they are.

tired
disappointed
with people they need not impress
receiving a “no”

No tests. No traps. Just ordinary life.

3. Separate what you feel from what you know.

What I feel

drawn · excited · at ease · hopeful · sure

What I know

kind to the waiter (March)

kept their word (April)

If the second column is nearly empty, you are not ready to make decisions yet.

4. Notice what you are explaining away.

Pause and examine it. If it is safe, you may make a private note.

  1. How serious was it?
  2. Has it happened before?
  3. What did they do when it was addressed?

Repetition · severity · accountability

5. Involve people who are not attracted to them.

They see the same person without the halo.

What concern am I tempted to minimise? What would I tell a friend?

Wanting someone does not make a relationship safe.

If you fear their reaction, seek individual support first. Help is on the page beneath this lesson.

Where you are right now

Newly attractedenjoy it; let the second column fill
Uncomfortablea signal to examine, not a verdict
Little attractiondo not force it; do not feel guilty

If it does not grow, you are not failing anyone by being truthful.

Bringing it together

  1. Attraction is a gift, and it is real
  2. It can open a door; it cannot tell you about character
  3. It changes how you interpret what you see
  4. Discernment is trained: time, ordinary life, two columns, honest questions, other people

One step now

One thing you are attracted to · one thing you have actually observed about their character

Not dating? Think of someone you were drawn to in the past.

Write it down privately, or simply think it through before God.

Prayer

Father, thank you for creating us with the ability to love, to desire, and to delight in another person.

Help me not to be ashamed of attraction, and not to be ruled by it.

Give me patience to wait, eyes to see clearly, and wisdom to know the difference between a feeling and the truth. In Jesus’ name, amen.

Ask God for the slower grace of discernment, so that attraction is neither shamed nor mistaken for his approval.

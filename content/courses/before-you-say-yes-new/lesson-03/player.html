Point 3 · Your patterns

Who were you drawn to, and why?

What did you tolerate? What did you demand?

What part was yours?

Not dated much? Ask the same of your friendships and family.

Not condemnation. Information.

Recognising a pattern gives you an opportunity to respond differently.

Knowing yourself includes knowing what you have survived.

You do not have to carry it alone.

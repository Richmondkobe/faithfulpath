Point 1

The Shape of Lament

Address Complaint Petition Trust

It does not skip the first four verses. It does not apologise for the questions.

Trust, spoken while the situation remains unresolved

“I have trusted”: a decision as much as a feeling

A pattern, and a permission

Point 2

Psalm 88 Is Also in the Book

“You have caused my beloved and my friend to shun me; my companions have become darkness.”Psalm 88:18 · ESV

No turn towards visible resolution. No closing declaration of renewed joy.

Still a psalm, kept for worship

If your lament does not reach the trust, you have not prayed wrongly.

Point 4

Jesus Wept

“Jesus wept.”John 11:35 · ESV

The coming resurrection did not make tears inappropriate.

“My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”Mark 15:34, praying Psalm 22:1 · ESV

If lament was appropriate for him, it is appropriate for you.

Honest protest

Job42:7spoke rightly, yet not every conclusion was correct
Jeremiah20:7“you have deceived me”
Psalm 44:23“Why are you sleeping, O Lord?”

Honest prayer does not mean that every conclusion we draw about God is correct.

Bring the real conclusion to him: heard, challenged, transformed.

Your silence does not place you beyond grace.

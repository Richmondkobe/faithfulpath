Part 4 · The first 30 days

Your First 30 Days Home

This month is for landing, not for proving anything.

Lessons 12 to 16A psalm for each week

Here is the shape of the month. Do what you can; when you miss something, begin again the next day without guilt. Come back to this page whenever you need it.

The month

Week by week

First morningLesson 12

  • Watch Lesson 12. Sort the cards into the three categories.
  • Take no major action. Do any necessary safety action.
  • Schedule the Eli conversation. Put a start and review date on the worksheet.
  • Then let the rest of the day be ordinary.

Psalm 23

Week 1Landing

  • Expect the dip. Do little.
  • Keep the rhythm from Session 10, imperfectly.
  • Tell your support person you are home: one conversation, not a report.
  • If capacity allows: Lesson 13 on Day 2 or 3.
  • Do not pronounce a verdict on the retreat or redesign your life this week.

Psalm 23

Week 2Doing

  • One Share conversation.
  • The Eli conversation, if you are testing anything. Bring the worksheet.
  • Optional: Session 8, if you left it for this month.
  • Is the rhythm livable? If not, shrink it. Do not abandon it.

Psalm 62

Week 3Shaping

  • Lesson 14: write a small rule of life.
  • Optional: Session 9, if you left it.
  • Look at Lesson 15, whether or not you first thought it applied.

Psalm 27

Week 4Settling

  • The Lesson 12 review, if you are testing a major direction. If it still needs more time, keep testing beyond Day 30.
  • Session 6 only if the conditions at the top of it are met.

Psalm 131

Day 30Review

  • Lesson 16 and the Day 30 review.
  • Day 30 is not a decision deadline.

Whenever it appliesLesson 15

  • When you need a pastor, counsellor, or mental-health professional. If any warning sign applies, act on it today, whatever week you are in.

Safety

Not a reason to wait

Nothing in this plan asks you to wait in immediate danger, abuse, urgent medical need or a legal reporting duty. Seek qualified help and act now. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

A cloak and the books

The ESV’s quotation marks reflect this interpretation.

Paul, writing about sexual immorality: “not all things are helpful… I will not be dominated by anything.”

Point 1

A Boundary, Not a Test

the queuethe lifttwo minutes before a meetingboredomdiscomfort

Gaps where thoughts surfaced, prayer began, and we noticed how we felt

A retreat deliberately reopens those gaps.

Every reach for the phone closes one again.

One purpose: to protect your attention for God

Slip? You have not failed. You have noticed. Put it down again.

Not a boundary against help: emergency calling stays available.

Point 2

Two Arrangements That Work

Option ARemain reachablephone on · Do Not Disturb · one or two emergency contacts call through
Option BDisconnectphone off · someone nearby takes an emergency call and reaches you in person

Choose one. They do not combine.

Usually right for parents, caregivers, and anyone with safety needs

Only if that person is available for the whole retreat, including overnight

Scheduled checks can be added, but on their own do not make you reachable.

Test it, tell people, define emergency

Test it before you go.

Option A→a real test call, settings on
Option B→they can take the call, and reach you

Tell people.

“I am away from Thursday to Sunday and will not be checking messages. If something urgent comes up, contact [name], who can reach me.”

One emergency contact. Define “emergency” together, out loud.

health or safetya dependantcannot wait

The biblical portrait · Elijah

What God did for Elijah

Fed him before he sent him

Let him sleep

Asked what he was doing there, and listened, twice

Redirected him, and gave him work that was his

Gave him a companion and a successor

He did not tell him to try harder.

Understand the teaching

The short answer

A month of imperfect repetition: a small daily minimum, four light weeks, and a review that is not a deadline.

The Christian Spiritual Reset · Lesson 1

Why am I still tired after I have rested?

You are still showing up.

still go to workstill answer messagesstill sit in church on Sunday

Nothing has collapsed.

But something has gone quiet.

tired in a place that sleep does not reach

Feeling distant from God does not mean God is absent.

You are not a failure, and you are not a fraud.

Point 1

A Tiredness Sleep Cannot Fix

Physical tiredness may improve after sleep. Soul-tiredness often remains.

One more thing we feel we are failing at

These signs can have many causes. Lesson 4 helps us tell them apart.
Trying harder is not always the answer.

unhurried time with God, nothing to prove

what fed you no longer does worship leaves you unmoved short with people you love prayer for others, not for you simple decisions feel heavy and a low-grade guilt

Point 2

Rest First, Direction Second

“Arise and eat, for the journey is too great for you.” (1 Kings 19:7)

Touched · fed · allowed to sleep · twice

The first thing God gives is not a lecture, but care for a tired body.

Rest first, direction second

A pattern in Elijah’s story, not a rule God must always follow

Rest can be part of preparing to hear and obey God

Point 3

Come Away and Rest

“Come away by yourselves to a desolate place and rest a while.” (Mark 6:31)

“But he would withdraw to desolate places and pray.” (Luke 5:16)

He continued to withdraw and pray, and returned to serve.

If Jesus deliberately made room for withdrawal and rest, you are not being weak when you need it too.

Matthew 11:28–30

“…and you will find rest for your souls.”

He is“gentle and lowly in heart”
Rest within following him“Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me”
For whom?“all who labor and are heavy laden”: a description, not a qualification

Point 4

What a Reset Is and Is Not

A reset is

a deliberate pause from noise, responsibility and performance

becoming honest before God again

making yourself available to God

A reset is not

manufacturing an emotional encounter

a way to force God to speak

a test of how spiritual you are

A strong sense of God, or faithful and still dry: both have done the retreat.

Practise · Your step

Take two minutes. Not to fix anything; just to answer honestly.

  1. Can you remember a time when you felt close to God? If so, what was different then? If not, what are you hoping to experience or understand as you begin?
  2. What is one thing you have kept doing for God while feeling nothing?
  3. Elijah said, “It is enough.” What would your honest sentence be?

Keep your answers for Session 10 and Day 30.

Prayer

Lord God, you saw Elijah under the broom tree, and you did not turn away from him.

You see me too, tired in places I cannot always explain.

Thank you that you care for the tired before you ask anything of them.

Lord Jesus, you said, “Come to me.” I am coming, as honestly as I can.

Teach me to rest in you, and prepare my heart for the days ahead.

In Jesus’ name. Amen.

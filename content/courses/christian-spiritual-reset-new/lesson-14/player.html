Point 1

A Trellis, Not a Cage

regula (Latin): a straightedge, a guide that gives shape

A trellis: a living thing grows along it without being crushed.

A small, chosen pattern of prayer, rest, work and relationship

“For freedom Christ has set us free; stand firm therefore, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.”Galatians 5:1 · ESV

Paul’s subject: freedom in Christ, not devotional timetables. But the principle applies.

A rule that earns approval, or that you beat yourself with, is a cage. Tear it up.

Rest declares that God, not you, holds the world together (Lesson 2).

The danger of drift

The loudest demand wins every day.

prayerrestthe people you love

Decide in advance what will not be squeezed.

Not a burden added: a fence around what was being trampled.

Point 2

Sabbath at the Centre

A regular period of

worshipceasingrestdelight

Creation · liberation

“The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath.”

A gift for your good, not a test you must pass.

Christians differ

The seventh daySaturday observed as the Sabbath
The Lord’s DaySunday worship; views differ on a Christian Sabbath
Fulfilled in Christno particular day held to be required

The relationship between the Old Testament command and Christ’s fulfilment of the law

No position required. Follow Scripture conscientiously, consider your church’s teaching, respect believers who differ.

Whatever your conviction: worship, rest, mercy, a sustainable rhythm.

How to practise it

A full day; if impossible, a half-day or evening to evening

Stop ordinary work: also managing, fixing, improving, producing.

worshiprest, including sleepdelightpeople, unhurried

The phone boundary from Lesson 9. Do not catch up.

Sabbath is not neglect.

Necessary care, mercy, emergencies and what cannot be postponed may remain.

Cease avoidable production, not availability to human need.

Three practical problems

Sunday is a working day for you?

Protect another regular period for rest, as deliberately as your responsibilities to others.

Christians differ about calling it Sabbath. The need for rest remains.

A caregiver?

Safe respite is not selfish. If none: Sabbath fragments.

a protected hourhelp with one taskprayer while someone else sits with them

A full day impossible now? Start with a half-day.

A half-day kept for a year beats a full day never taken.

It will cost you something. That is the point.

Your responsibility has limits; God remains God while you rest.

The manna did not fall on the seventh day, and they had enough (Exodus 16).

Point 3

Three Core Commitments

A suggested framework for this course, not a biblical formula · one page · three to five lines

Ceasing and restSabbath, and a realistic sleep window
Prayer and Scripturea fixed time, however short
One limitprotects your health or calling

Daniel’s window was about the fixedness.

A decision made once, not fifty times a week.

Optional, at most two: a relationship (church first) · body · screens

Writing it · deliberately unimpressive

My rule for this season

Ceasing and rest: Saturday noon to evening; no paid work or email; family and a walk. Phone outside the bedroom.

Prayer and Scripture: a psalm and five minutes of prayer, 7:00 a.m., Monday to Friday.

One limit: laptop closed by 7:00 p.m.

When I miss something: I will resume at the next appropriate opportunity without punishment, compensation, or guilt.

Not a double Sabbath to make up. Resume.

Two cautions

A difficult ordinary weekWrite the rule you can keep then, not the one you wish you had capacity for.
Not aloneIf you share a life, write it together; a hidden Sabbath collides.

Read it to God. Share it with your Eli or support person.

You decide what personal details they see.

A rule nobody else knows about is easy to abandon quietly.

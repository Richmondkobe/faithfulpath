Point 1

Purpose Is Given, Not Earned

Rooted in relationship with Christ, expressed through loving service

The work does not purchase the relationship; it expresses it.

Given to someone who had just failed: not a reward for success

Restoration is not automatic return to office

Forgiveness, fellowship, and usefulness in God’s kingdom are not identical to reinstatement in a particular office.

Serious misconduct may permanently disqualify someone from certain roles, even where repentance is genuine.

“Feed my sheep”

Not a strategy or a title: care for people who belong to Jesus

Love God with everything · love your neighbour as yourself (Matthew 22:37–39)

“He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the Lord require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?”Micah 6:8 · ESV

No grand platform required

A small, proportionate act of faithfulness · sometimes rest, receiving help, or letting someone else carry the work

When love has gone quiet

ministry → programmesparenting → logisticscare → caseload

That does not prove that love has disappeared.

Faithful duty can continue when warm feeling is absent.

Feeling empty does not necessarily mean you have stopped loving.

Point 2

Purpose Narrowed

recovering meaningnarrowingsharingrestructuringleaving, after discernment

Feed sheep, not run the world.

Some seasons are mostly for receiving.

Elijah was fed before he was sent.

Not “my sheep”

Who is within my legitimate sphere of love, neighbourliness, service, or responsibility?

Point 3

Follow Me

Led where he did not want to go, and then two words

“Follow me.”

Not an independent project: tested against the person and way of Jesus

Two careful words

Anything larger

A sense that a role should end, or change: do not decide it here.

Write it on a card marked “To test”, for Lesson 12.

Compulsive self-checking

Do not keep asking whether your love is real or pure enough.

Stay with “Lord, you know everything.”

One question to carry into the practice

If Jesus asked me “Do you love me?” today, what would I say?

“I am not sure” is an honest answer.

Allegiance · trust · obedience · desire · return

If it leads to repeated checking or fear, leave it unanswered: “Lord, you know everything.”

Pause here. Put the screen aside.

Point 1

Eat Normally; Fasting Is Optional

This retreat does not require fasting.

Every session, with full benefit, while eating normally

You do not need physical hunger to make the retreat spiritually serious.

When not to fast

Do not fast without individual medical guidance if you are:

  • pregnant, breastfeeding or trying to conceive
  • under eighteen
  • an older adult advised to eat regularly

Examples, not a complete list

… or if you:

have diabetes or a blood-sugar conditionhave a heart, kidney, liver or digestive condition, or any chronic illnesstake regular medicationare recovering from illness, surgery or exhaustionhave a history of fainting, migraines, anaemia or low blood pressure

Without exception

No food fasting for anyone with a current or past eating disorder or disordered eating.

Follow your meal plan and treatment guidance. If food thoughts intensify, pause the retreat exercises and contact your treatment provider or support person.

No spiritual loss at all

Elijah did not fast. He was fed.

If you do fast

Only if it is already a healthy, established practice, and a healthcare professional who knows your current health and medications has confirmed it

Modest, familiar, less intensive than usual. Nothing new or extended while alone.

Do not restrict water.

Stop at once

dizzy · faint · confused · shaky · racing heart · severe headache · unwell in a way you cannot explain

Severe or worsening: seek urgent medical help. Do not drive, and do not stay alone.

No spiritual merit in pushing through a body telling you to stop

Often wiser: a non-food restraint

Point 2

Simple, Nourishing Food

Eat well, eat simply, and do not make food a project.

little preparationfamiliar food that meets your needsnothing new or restrictive

Eat enough.

Simplicity is about not fussing, not about going without.

Eat slowly, with attention and thanks.

Breakfast on the beach (John 21): meals are part of how God cares for people.

Caffeine and alcohol

Caffeine: no sudden change during the retreat.

A withdrawal headache is easy to mistake for spiritual distress.

Cutting down? Do it gradually, beforehand.

Alcohol is not part of this retreat.

If you may be dependent, do not stop suddenly on your own. Withdrawal can be dangerous. Seek medical advice first (Lesson 5).

Point 3

Sleep Is a Gift

“for he gives to his beloved sleep”Psalm 127:2

Elijah, rested and fed twiceJesus, asleep in the storm

Depending on God, not anxious striving

Gethsemane: a failure to keep watch, at a unique moment

“the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak”Mark 14:38

Bodily limits are real.

So sleep.

no five o’clock alarm to prove anythingnaps allowednormal routines, no new sleep aids

Rest is part of the retreat.

Point 4

Walk, Do Not Train

The road to Emmaus: two disciples walked and talked, and Jesus joined them.

Gentle, within your ability, and near help

Not a long run or hard workout. Not alone in unsafe conditions.

A change of position and place, not distance

When you become unwell

Treat it as you would at home.

Do not spiritualise a fever.

Stay in bed, and let the sessions wait.

Not undone: simply paused.

Contagious at a shared place? Tell the host or leader, and protect others.

The sessions are offered, not assigned. Your body’s limits are legitimate limits.

Practise · Your step

A simple plan for your bodyOrdinary, nourishing food is the default.

  1. Food: a simple meal plan for each day
  2. Sleep: when I will allow myself to sleep, and to wake
  3. Movement: what, if any, and where it is safe

Gentle plans, not targets. Adjust them if your health or need for rest requires it.

Considering a food fast? Only if it is already a healthy practice and a professional who knows your health has confirmed it. If not, choose a non-food restraint.

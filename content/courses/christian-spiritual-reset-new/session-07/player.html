“the sound of a low whisper” (ESV) · footnote: “a sound, a thin silence”

“a still small voice” (KJV) · “a sound of sheer silence” (NRSV)

dĕmāmâ, from dāmam: “to become still, or silent” (Lessons 8 and 11)

Not that God always whispers: the Lord was not in the wind, the earthquake, or the fire.

Point 1

“What Are You Doing Here?”

God’s first words are a question.

“I, even I only, am left”

Real danger, and a picture narrowed by isolation

The same question, again
The same answer, word for word

Point 2

Not in the Wind

not in the windnot in the earthquakenot in the fire

Elsewhere: thunder, fire, dreams, prophets, public events

Spectacle is not divine direction.

God is not limited to retreat conditions.

Scripture · counsel · ordinary responsibilities · circumstances · time

Point 3

Correction and Provision

Not a full explanation, nor every threat removed

A tasktwo future kings, and Elisha
A correctionseven thousand: Elijah is not alone
Sent backthe way he came

A successor to continue the prophetic work · God’s purposes greater than Elijah’s own responsibility

We expect explanation; God gave assignment.

Correction calls us to truth, repentance, and faithful action.

Not judged by how it feels: test it against Scripture and Christ.

Scripture first

Scripture · prayer · conscience · wise counsel · circumstances

Only Scripture is the church’s authoritative written standard.

“All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work.”2 Timothy 3:16–17 · ESV

First ask what Scripture already says.

Every impression, circumstance and piece of counsel is tested.

Point 4

Speak, Lord, and Test

Samuel heard three times, and each time ran to Eli.

An imperfect man, who gave a wise response

“Speak, Lord, for your servant hears.”1 Samuel 3:9 · ESV

A particular prophetic calling, not an experience to expect on retreat

Humility and wise counsel matter.

You are not meant to do this alone.

Trusted people help us examine our impressions with humility.

The tests

Scripture, in context The character of Christ The facts, not only feelings Time and questioning Mature, qualified advisers Safety, consent, justice No secrecy or immunity Proportionate, reversible Rested and supported
Urgencya reason to slow down
Peacemay accompany discernment, but is not proof

Two careful words

A voice others cannot hear

Especially one that commands, threatens, or blurs what is real: do not assume it is God. Seek prompt professional assessment.

If it commands harm: seek urgent help, and do not remain alone.

Sensing nothing

You have not failed. Faithful listening that hears nothing is still faithful.

Direction may come later, through ordinary means.

One question to carry into the practice

“What are you doing here?”

Answer it honestly, in a few sentences.

Not why you came, but what you are actually doing here, now, on the third morning.

Pause here. Put the screen aside.

Lead Before You’re Ready

Practical pastoral training for your first year or two of leading in a church.

Course progress: 0 of 10 lessons completed

Welcome

This course is for people in their first year or two of leading a ministry, team, small group, project or area of service in a church. It is also for someone preparing to lead, or returning to leadership after a break, and for anyone who has been leading for longer and was never given the basics.

You are not required to be ready. You are required to be willing, teachable, and honest about what you do not know.

What you will learn

The course has three parts, following the book Lead Before You’re Ready:

Start With YourselfCalling, character, spiritual health and role clarity. Lessons 1–3.
Learn the CraftVolunteers, meetings and difficult conversations. Lessons 4–7.
Face What Tests YouCriticism, over-centralised responsibility, pastoral demands, exhaustion, and developing another leader. Lessons 8–10.

How each lesson works

Listen

A short teaching of about nine minutes, with a transcript.

Think

One or two questions to let it settle. Nothing is typed or saved.

Take one step

One practical action for the week.

Go deeper

Optional: the full chapter and the matching toolkit page.

Writing is optional unless the action needs a written agreement or plan. Keep anything you write in a private format, and keep role agreements and ministry records in your church’s own system, not in this course.

Recommended pace

One lesson each week, for ten weeks. Allow about twenty to thirty minutes for the lesson and reflection, plus time for the week’s action. Repeat a week when you need to. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can stop and return at any time.

If you can work through the course with a friend who also leads, or with the person who put you in the role, better still.

Important to know

This course offers practical pastoral training. Apply it within your church’s governance, supervision, safeguarding and reporting procedures. It does not give you authority that your church has not assigned to you.

Urgent safeguarding, health or misconduct concerns must not be postponed until the relevant lesson. If something needs reporting now, use your church’s reporting route now. See Concerns, Care and Reporting.

Ready to begin?
